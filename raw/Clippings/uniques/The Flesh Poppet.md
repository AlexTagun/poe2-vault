**The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman** is a **[Unique Talisman](/Unique+Talismans "Path of Exile 2 Unique Talismans")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman** has a significant improvement in physical damage, attack speed, and all attributes. It also copies a random modifier from an enemy when the user shapeshifts into an animal form. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Long ago, the witches of the Azak Tribe learned how to tap into the flow of Vivid lifeforce to 'cooperate' with their enemies... forcefully.

### Path of Exile 2 The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman Stats & Modifiers

**The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (82-95) to (135-156)  
Critical Hit Chance: 8%  
Attacks per Second: (1.34-1.41)  
Weapon Range: 1.2

**The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman**features the following Modifiers:

(90-120)% increased Physical Damage  
(7-13)% increased Attack Speed  
+(7-13) to all Attributes  
(30-40)% reduced Presence Area of Effect  
Copy a random Modifier from each enemy in your Presence when you Shapeshift to an Animal form  
Modifiers gained this way are lost after 30 seconds or when you next Shapeshift

### PoE 2 The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman Requirements

To equip **The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 40, 47 Str, 34 Int

### How to get The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **The Flesh Poppet Vicious Talisman** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
