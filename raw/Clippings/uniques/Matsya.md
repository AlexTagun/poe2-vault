**Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff** is a **[Unique Quarterstaff](/Unique+Quarterstaves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Quarterstaves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff** is a quarterstaff that boosts attack speed, critical hit chance, and mana regeneration while reducing skill spirit reservation. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> In our tales, and in our hearts, the rivers still flow.

### Path of Exile 2 Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff Stats & Modifiers

**Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 19-39  
Critical Hit Chance: 10%  
Attacks per Second: (1.73-1.8)  
Weapon Range: 1.3  
Lightning Damage: 1 to (19-29)

**Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (6-9) to (10-15) Cold Damage  
Adds 1 to (19-29) Lightning Damage  
+(3-5)% to Critical Hit Chance  
(15-20)% increased Attack Speed  
(25-40)% increased Mana Regeneration Rate  
Skills reserve 50% less Spirit

### PoE 2 Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff Requirements

To equip **Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 20, 30 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity"), 14 [Int](/Int "Path of Exile 2 Int")

### How to get Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Matsya Crescent Quarterstaff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
