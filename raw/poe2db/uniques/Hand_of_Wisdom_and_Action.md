# Hand of Wisdom and Action

**Base:** Spiral Wraps
**Class:** Gloves

## Base Stats

Evasion Rating : 33
Energy Shield : 14

**Requires: Level 33 , 24 Dex , 24 Int**

## Modifiers

- +(15 — 25) to Dexterity
- +(15 — 25) to Intelligence
- 1 % increased Attack Speed per 20 Dexterity
- Adds 1 to 10 Lightning Damage to Attacks per 20 Intelligence

## Flavour

> She thinks and we act. She acts and we think. Fragments of the whole that washes clean the skies.
