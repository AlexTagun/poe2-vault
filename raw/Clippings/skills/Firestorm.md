**Firestorm** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Firestorm** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Firestorm Effects

**Firestorm**provides the following effects:

Firestorm

* Deals (2–56) to (3–83) Fire Damage
* Impact radius is 1 metre
* Storm radius is 2.8 metres
* Storm duration is 6 seconds
* One impact every 0.2 seconds
* Fire Infusion grants 200% increased bolt frequency
* Fire Infusion grants +1.8 metres to storm radius
* Limit 1 Firestorm

Ice Bolts

* Critical Hit Chance: 12.00%
* Impact radius is 1.5 metres
* One impact every 0.2 seconds
* Deals (2–58) to (3–86) Cold Damage

Lightning Bolts

* Critical Hit Chance: 9.00%
* Impact radius is 0.7 metres
* One impact every 0.2 seconds
* Deals (1–8) to (5–156) Lightning Damage

Additional Effects From Quality:  
+(0–0.2) metres to impact radius

### How to get Firestorm in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Firestorm** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 11**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 41, with at least 95 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").

### Path of Exile 2 Firestorm Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0  
  + Firestorm now has a base Radius of 2.8 metres (previously 5.6), and creates one bolt every 0.2 seconds (previously every 0.1 seconds).
  + No longer consumes Ignite to create improved bolts, instead, it can now consume every type of Elemental Infusion.
  + Fire Infusion causes the storm to double in size and create three times as many bolts.
  + Cold and Lightning Infusions cause the storm to create additional cold and lightning bolts, respectively.
  + One of each Infusion can be consumed by a single use of the Skill.
  + Fire Bolt damage is now 16-56 to 23-83 at Gem levels 11-20 (previously 26-96 to 39-144).
