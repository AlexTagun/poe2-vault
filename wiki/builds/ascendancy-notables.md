## Заметки

- Три аскендансии отсутствуют в raw-данных (patch 0.4.0): [[builds/ascendancies/martial-artist|Martial Artist]], [[builds/ascendancies/spirit-walker|Spirit Walker]], [[builds/ascendancies/disciple-of-varashta|Disciple of Varashta]]
