**Font of Power Omen Sceptre** is a **[Unique Sceptre](/Unique+Sceptres "Path of Exile 2 Unique Sceptres")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Font of Power Omen Sceptre** is a sceptre that enhances spirit, mana regeneration, and supports party members by sharing mana when they cast spells. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Tale-women may not fight directly, for they have a much higher purpose.

### Path of Exile 2 Font of Power Omen Sceptre Stats & Modifiers

**Font of Power Omen Sceptre**has the following Stats:

Spirit: 100

**Font of Power Omen Sceptre**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 5 [Malice](/Malice "Path of Exile 2 Malice")  
(30-50)% increased Spirit  
+(40-60) to maximum Mana  
(20-30)% increased Mana Regeneration Rate  
When a Party Member in your Presence Casts a Spell, you  
Sacrifice 20% of Mana and they Leech that Mana

### PoE 2 Font of Power Omen Sceptre Requirements

To equip **Font of Power Omen Sceptre** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 16, 12 STR, 25 INT

### How to get Font of Power Omen Sceptre in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Font of Power Omen Sceptre** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
