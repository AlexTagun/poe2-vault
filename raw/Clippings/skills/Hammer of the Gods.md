**Hammer of the Gods** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Hammer of the Gods** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Hammer of the Gods Effects

**Hammer of the Gods**provides the following effects:

* Can't be Evaded
* While dual wielding both weapons deal 30% less damage
* Impact radius is 2.4 metres
* Hammerfaill duration is 2.5

ADDITIONAL EFFECTS FROM QUALITY:

* (0–20)% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate

### How to get Hammer of the Gods in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Hammer of the Gods**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 13**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 52, with at least 119 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength"), and must be used with a [Two Hand Mace](/Maces "Path of Exile 2 Maces") or [One Hand Mace](/Maces "Path of Exile 2 Maces").
