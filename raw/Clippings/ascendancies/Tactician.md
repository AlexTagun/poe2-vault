**Tactician** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Mercenary](/Mercenary "Path of Exile 2 Mercenary")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The Tactician is a support class that specializes in party buffs that enhance other members of your party, totems, and minions. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. In Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Tactician Key Features

The Tactician is better utilized when in a party with other players or when having minions and Totems, as they are considered allies as well. With its different buffs, the new Supporting Fire minion skill and the ability to use multiple banners provided by the also new skill Whoever Pays Best, the Tactician shines when buffing the party against the enemies. The Tactician is not recommended for solo players that do not use minions or totems.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [A Solid Plan](/A+Solid+Plan "Path of Exile 2 A Solid Plan"): Persistent Buffs have 50% less Reservation requirements.
* [Watch How I Do It](/Watch+How+I+Do+It "Path of Exile 2 Watch How I Do It"): Allies in your Presence gain added Attack Damage equal to 25% of your main hand's Weapon Damage.
* [Whoever Pays Best](/Whoever+Pays+Best "Path of Exile 2 Whoever Pays Best"): There is no Limit on the number of Banners you can place. Banners gain 5 Glory per Second.
* [Right Where We Want Them](/Right+Where+We+Want+Them "Path of Exile 2 Right Where We Want Them"): Pinned enemies cannot perform actions. Grants Projectile Damage builds Pin.
* [Polish That Gear](/Polish+That+Gear "Path of Exile 2 Polish That Gear"): Gain Deflection Rating equal to 20% of Armor. Gain 100% of Evasion Rating as extra Ailment Threshold.
* [Stay Light, Use Cover](/Stay+Light,+Use+Cover "Path of Exile 2 Stay Light, Use Cover"): Defend with 200% Armor. Enemies have an Accuracy Penalty against you based on Distance. Maximum Evade Chance is 50%. Maximum Physical Damage Reduction is 50%.
* [Unleash hell!](/Unleash+hell! "Path of Exile 2 Unleash hell!"): Grants Skill: [Supporting Fire](/Supporting+Fire "Path of Exile 2 Supporting Fire").
* [Cannons, Ready!](/Cannons,+Ready! "Path of Exile 2 Cannons, Ready!"): Totems have 30% more Skill Speed. Totems only use skills when you fire and Attack Projectile. +1 to maximum number of Summoned Totems.
* [Suppressing Fire](/Suppressing+Fire "Path of Exile 2 Suppressing Fire"): Provides 40% more Immobilisation buildup
* [Strategic Embankments](/Strategic+Embankments "Path of Exile 2 Strategic Embankments"): Grants Embarkment Auras to your Totems when placed. The effect varies depending on the totem used.

### Path of Exile 2 Tactician Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a Tactician, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ High Support Capabilities:**Skills in the Tactician Ascendancy skill align great for Mercenaries that are part of a party or take advantage of Totems and Minions.

**+ Crowd Control**: The Tactician abilities can create a death zone with minions and Totems, distracting and damaging enemies at the same time.

### Cons

**-****Allied Dependent:** Tactician skills are meant to support allies, so if you are a solo player with no minions or Totems, then most skills would go underutilized.

**- Limited Self-enhancements:** Most Tactician skills are designed to affect allies, and not to enhance oneself.
