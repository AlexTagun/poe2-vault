**Shyaba Temple Maul** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Shyaba Temple Maul,** a mystical maul requiring agile hands rather than brute force, greatly enhancing physical strikes, increasing attack speed and illumination, and guaranteeing unerring hits. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Be not deceived by the treachery of men.

### Path of Exile 2 Shyaba Temple Maul Stats & Modifiers

**Shyaba Temple Maul**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (63-77) to (130-158)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.8  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Shyaba Temple Maul**features the following Modifiers:

+50 Dexterity Requirement  
-15 Strength Requirement  
(80–120)% increased Physical Damage  
50% increased Attack Speed  
(15–25)% increased Light Radius  
Always Hits

### PoE 2 Shyaba Temple Maul Requirements

To equip **Shyaba Temple Maul** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 28
* Stats: 37 Str, Dex 50

### How to get Shyaba Temple Maul in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Shyaba Temple Maul** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
