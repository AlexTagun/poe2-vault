**Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow** is a **[Unique Bow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow,** a chaotic weapon that channels lightning into corrupt energy, boosting attack speed and allowing all lightning strikes to deal chaos damage. Strikes can also shock, fueled by the chaos they inflict. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> rained down. Metal withered and flesh melted before its  
> arcane power. There was no escape, no shelter. Only despair.

### Path of Exile 2 Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow Stats & Modifiers

**Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 47-79  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.32-1.38)  
Lightning Damage: 1 to (300-500)

**Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow**features the following Modifiers:

Adds 1 to (200–300) Lightning Damage  
(10–15)% increased Attack Speed  
100% of Lightning Damage Converted to Chaos Damage  
Chaos Damage from Hits also Contributes to Shock Chance

### PoE 2 Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow Requirements

To equip **Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 79
* Stats: 163 Dex

### How to get Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Voltaxic Rift Fanatic Bow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
