**Dread Banner** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Dread Banner** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Dread Banner Effects

**Dread Banner**provides the following effects:

* Requires 200 Glory to use
* Generates 100% of Monster Power as Glory for this Skill on Hitting with an Attack
* Banner Aura radius is 4.5 metres
* Aura grants +4% to all Maximum Elemental Resistances
* Aura grants 60% more Elemental Ailment Threshold
* Aura grants 0.5 Flask charges per second
* Banner duration is (6–9.8) seconds

Additional Effects from Quality:  
(0–10)% increased Reservation Efficiency

### How to get Dread Banner in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Dread Banner**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 14**.
* To use the Dread Banner gem, it requires Character Level 58 and above, with 73 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength") and 73 [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Dread Banner Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0, Dread Banner:
  + now grants 0.5 Flask charges per second (previously 0.17)
  + now grants 60% more Elemental Ailment Threshold (previously 12-15%)
  + now grants +4% to all Maximum Elemental Resistance
  + Dread Banner no longer provides 12-15% more Stun Threshold.
  + Dread Banner now has a duration of 8.6-9.8 seconds at Gem levels 14-20 (previously 6.65-6.9 seconds)
  + Dread Banner is no longer removed when you leave the Aura.
  + Quality now provides 0-10% increased Reservation Efficiency (previously +0-2% more Aura Magnitudes per Valour).
