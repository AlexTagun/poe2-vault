# The Black Doubt

**Base:** Hexer's Robe
**Class:** Body Armours

## Base Stats

Energy Shield : (70-88)
Base Movement Speed: -0.03

**Requires: Level 11 , 21 Int**

## Modifiers

- (60 — 100) % increased Energy Shield
- +(10 — 30) to Intelligence
- +(10 — 20) % to Cold Resistance
- Damage over Time bypasses your Energy Shield While not on Full Life, Sacrifice 10 % of maximum Mana per Second to Recover that much Life

## Flavour

> Suspicion is a sinister shadow slithering in the soul.
