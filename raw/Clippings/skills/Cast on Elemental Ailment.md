**Cast on Elemental Ailment** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Cast on Elemental Ailment** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Cast on Elemental Ailment Effects

**Cast on Elemental Ailment**provides the following effects:

* Gains 10 Energy per Power of enemies you Freeze with Hits from Skills
* Gains 1 Energy per Power of enemies you Ignite with Hits from Skills, modified by the percentage of the enemy's Ailment Threshold the Ignite will deal
* Gains 1 Energy per Power of enemies you Shock with Hits from Skills
* (0–57)% increased Energy gained  
  Triggers all Socketed Spells and loses all Energy on reaching maximum Energy

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–15)% increased Energy gained

### How to get Cast on Elemental Ailment in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Cast on Elemental Ailment** can be crafted by engraving it to an appropriate level of an **Uncut Spirit Gem 14.**

### Path of Exile 2 Cast on Elemental Ailment Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0  
  + Cast on Freeze, Cast on Shock, and Cast on Ignite have been combined into Cast on Elemental Ailment.
  + Cast on Elemental Ailment requires 100 Spirit and a minimum level of 14
  + Existing copies of the previous gems will be updated when you load the character or stash tab holding them.
