**Enfeeble** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Enfeeble** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Enfeeble Effects

**Enfeeble**provides the following effects:

* Curse applies after a 1.5-second delay
* Curse does not apply to enemies above level (0–20)
* Curse radius is (1.5–3.1) metres
* Curse makes targets deal (20–29)% less Damage
* Curse duration is (6–7.4) seconds

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–10)% increased Curse Magnitudes

### How to get Enfeeble in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Enfeeble**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 3**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 6, with at least 16 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").

### Path of Exile 2 Enfeeble Notes and Tips

* Enfeeble no longer has Curse makes Non-Unique targets deal 20-26% less Damage or Curse makes Unique targets deal 10-16% less Damage in Patch 0.3.0
* Curse now makes targets deal 21-29% less Damage at Gem levels 3-20 in Patch 0.3.0
