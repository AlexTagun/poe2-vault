**Armour Piercing Rounds** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Armour Piercing Rounds** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Armour Piercing Rounds Effects

**Armour Piercing Rounds**provides the following effects:

AMMUNITION

* +(12–21) Bolts loaded per clip

PROJECTILE

Attack Speed: 425% of base  
Attack Damage: (25–91)%

* Hits Break (1–845) Armour
* Knock Back Enemies

ADDITIONAL EFFECTS FROM QUALITY:

* Break (0–20)% increased Armour

### POE2 Armor Piercing Rounds Skill Guide

* Armor Piercing Rounds can break enemy armor. Look out for the armor symbol that indicates an armoured enemy.
* This is an ammo skill, use it to charge your bolts, then use basic attack to fire. If you need to, you can use this skill again, to manually reload.

### How to get Armour Piercing Rounds in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Armour Piercing Rounds**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 1**.
* To use this skill gem, it must be used with a [Crossbow](/Crossbows "Path of Exile 2 Crossbows").

### Path of Exile 2 Armour Piercing Rounds Notes and Tips

* Armour Piercing Rounds armour break has been reduced from 0-30% to 0-20% in [Patch 0.3.0](/Patch+Notes#0.3.0 "Path of Exile 2 Patch Notes#0.3.0").
