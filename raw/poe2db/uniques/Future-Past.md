# Future-Past

**Base:** Buff
**Class:** Persistent , Duration

## Base Stats

Level: (1 — 20)

**Requires: Level (1 — 90)**

## Modifiers

- Grants each of Hindsight, Foresight, and Sight Beyond Sight, in sequence Hindsight lasts for a 20 second duration
- Foresight prevents Dodge Roll from Avoiding Hits and lasts until immediately before you are Hit while Dodge Rolling Sight Beyond Sight makes you unable to die and lasts for a (4 — 5.9) second duration Fully recover Life when you gain Sight Beyond Sight
