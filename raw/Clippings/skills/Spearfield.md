**Spearfield** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Spearfield** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Spearfield Effects

**Spearfield**provides the following effects:

Spearfield

* Creates 1 Spear per 0.2 metres of Radius
* Maim on Hit
* 80% less damage if destroyed within 0.5 seconds by something other than you
* Maims from this Skill Slow an additional 10%
* Spears emerge within a (4–6.9) metre length cone
* Spear duration is 10 seconds
* Maximum 70 active spears

Explosion

* Attack Damage: (50–204)%
* Maim on Hit
* 80% less damage if destroyed within 0.5 seconds by something other than you
* Maims from this Skill Slow an additional 10%
* Explosion radius is 0.6 metres

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Hazards created by this Skill have (0–10)% chance to rearm after they are triggered

### How to get Spearfield in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Spearfield** can be crafted by engraving it to an appropriate level of an [Uncut Skill Gem](/Uncut+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Uncut Skill Gems").

* **Spearfield** can be crafted by engraving it to an Uncut Spirit Gem Level 1.
* To use the **Spearfield** gem, it requires Character Level 1, and it must be used with a [Spear](/Spears "Path of Exile 2 Spears").

### Path of Exile 2 Spearfield Notes and Tips

* In Patch 0.3.0, Spearfield:  
  + Now deals 73-204% of Attack Damage at Gem levels 5-20 (previously 17-49%).
  + It now deals 80% less damage in the first 0.5 seconds of its duration.
  + It no longer has an (unintentionally hidden) 300% more damage after 0.5 seconds.
