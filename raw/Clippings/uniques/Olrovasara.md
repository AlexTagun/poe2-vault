**Olrovasara Torment Club** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Olrovasara Torment Club** adds lightning damage, increases accuracy rating, grants increased attack speed, and allows maximum added lightning damage equal to the enemy's power for a duration upon hitting, up to a capped amount. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "True heroes grow stronger in the face of adversity."  
> - Fourth Tenet of the Knights of the Sun

### Path of Exile 2 Olrovasara Torment Club Stats & Modifiers

**Olrovasara Torment Club**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage:: 11-17  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.55  
Weapon Range: 1.1

**Olrovasara Torment Club**features the following Modifiers:

Adds 1 to (60–80) Lightning Damage  
+(300–400) to Accuracy Rating  
(5–30)% increased Attack Speed  
On Hitting an enemy, gains maximum added Lightning damage equal to  
the enemy's Power for 20 seconds, up to a total of 500

### PoE 2 Olrovasara Torment Club Requirements

To equip **Olrovasara Torment Club** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 65, 114 Str

### How to get Olrovasara Torment Club in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Olrovasara Torment Club** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
