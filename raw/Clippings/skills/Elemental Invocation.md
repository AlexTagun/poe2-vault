**Elemental Invocation** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Elemental Invocation** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Elemental Invocation Effects

**Elemental Invocation**provides the following effects:

* Gains 10 Energy per Power of enemies you Freeze with Hits from Skills
* Gains 1 Energy per Power of enemies you Ignite with Hits from Skills, modified by the percentage of the enemy's Ailment Threshold the Ignite will deal
* Gains 1 Energy per Power of enemies you Shock with Hits from Skills
* (0–57)% increased Energy gained
* Maximum Energy is 500

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Maximum Energy is (0–100)

### How to get Elemental Invocation in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Elemental Invocation** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 8**.
* To use the Elemental Invocation gem, it requires Character Level 26 and above, with 34 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") and 34 [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") or more.

**Elemental Invocation** can be crafted by engraving it to an appropriate level of an **Uncut Spirit Gem**.

### Path of Exile 2 Elemental Invocation Notes and Tips

* Elemental Invocation now gains 1 Energy per Power of enemies you Ignite with Hits from Skills, modified by the percentage of the enemy's Ailment Threshold the Ignite will deal (previously 2) in Patch 0.3.0
