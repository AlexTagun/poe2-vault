**Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow** is a **[Unique Crossbow](/Unique+Crossbows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Crossbows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow** is a crossbow that grants cold damage, mana, freeze buildup, and can alter attacks to chain an additional time. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Sibilant promises surrounded them in the night. All the travelers had to give him was their devotion...

### Path of Exile 2 Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow Stats & Modifiers

**Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 7-12  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.6  
Reload Time: 0.8

**Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (8-10) to (13-15) Cold Damage  
Gain 5 Mana per Enemy Killed  
(30-50)% Increased Freeze Buildup  
30% increased Chill Duration on Enemies  
Attacks Chain 2 additional times

### PoE 2 Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow Requirements

To equip **Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 1

* **Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
