# Widow's Reign

**Base:** Knight Armour
**Class:** Body Armours

## Base Stats

Armour : (296-370)
Evasion Rating : (266-332)
Base Movement Speed: -0.04

**Requires: Level 45 , 41 Str , 41 Dex**

## Modifiers

- (100 — 150) % increased Armour and Evasion
- +(100 — 150) to maximum Life
- +(17 — 23) % to Chaos Resistance
- +(200 — 300) to Ailment Threshold
- Life that would be lost by taking Damage is instead Reserved until you take no Damage to Life for 3 seconds
