**Chronomancer** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Sorceress](/Sorceress "Path of Exile 2 Sorceress")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Chronomancer** manipulates time itself, bending reality to her will. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

> The tapestry of time is yours to weave; an element you alone may master.

### Path of Exile 2 Chronomancer Key Features

Manipulate time to stop enemies or rewind time to recover your health and mana, and even reset the cooldown of skills.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Apex of the Moment](/Apex+of+the+Moment "Path of Exile 2 Apex of the Moment"): Enemies in your Presence are Slowed by 20%
* [Circular Heartbeat](/Circular+Heartbeat "Path of Exile 2 Circular Heartbeat"): 30% of Damage taken Recouped as Life
* [Footprints in the Sand](/Footprints+in+the+Sand "Path of Exile 2 Footprints in the Sand"): Grants Skill: [Temporal Rift](/Temporal+Rift "Path of Exile 2 Temporal Rift")
* [Inevitability](/Inevitability "Path of Exile 2 Inevitability"): Grants Skill: [Inevitable Agony](/Inevitable+Agony "Path of Exile 2 Inevitable Agony")
* [Now and Again](/Now+and+Again "Path of Exile 2 Now and Again"): Skills have 33% chance to not consume a Cooldown when used
* [Quicksand Hourglass](/Quicksand+Hourglass "Path of Exile 2 Quicksand Hourglass"): Every 12 seconds, gain 50% more Cast Speed for 4 seconds
* [The Rapid River](/The+Rapid+River "Path of Exile 2 The Rapid River"): Recoup Effects instead occur over 4 seconds
* [Ultimate Command](/Ultimate+Command "Path of Exile 2 Ultimate Command"): Grants Skill: [Time Freeze](/Time+Freeze "Path of Exile 2 Time Freeze")
* [Unbound Encore](/Unbound+Encore "Path of Exile 2 Unbound Encore"): Grants Skill: [Time Snap](/Time+Snap "Path of Exile 2 Time Snap")

### Path of Exile 2 Chronomancer Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Chronomancer**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Good Survivability:**The node **Circular Heartbeat** increases the survivability of the player, recouping health from damage taken.

**+ Short Cooldown**: The **Chronomancer** has nodes that can lower the cooldown of skills, allowing the player to utilize numerous skills.

### Cons

**-****Cooldown Dependent:** Even though the **Chronomancer** has nodes that lower cooldown, the class is heavy on being cooldown dependent.

**- Learning Curve**: Some **Chronomancer**builds allow for a unique gameplay, which makes it different from other ascendancies. It can take time to learn the mechanics of the ascendancy.
