**Archmage** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Archmage** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Archmage Effects

**Archmage**provides the following effects:

* Non-Channelling Spells Gain 4% of Damage as extra Lightning damage for each 100 maximum Mana you have
* Non-Channelling Spells cost an additional (6.1–8)% of your maximum Mana

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–10)% increased Reservation Efficiency

### How to get Archmage in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Archmage** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level (1–20)**.
* To use the Archmage gem, it requires Character Level (1–90) and above, with (4–157) [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Archmage Notes and Tips

* Archmage now has Non-Channelling Spells Gain 4% of Damage as extra Lightning damage for each 100 maximum Mana you have (previously 3%) in Patch 0.3.0
