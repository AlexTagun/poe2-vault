# Bursting Decay

**Base:** Unset Ring
**Class:** Rings

**Requires: Level 60**

## Implicit

- Grants 1 additional Skill Slot

## Modifiers

- (15 — 25) % increased Rarity of Items found
- +(17 — 23) % to Chaos Resistance
- Lose 5 % of maximum Life per second
- Attacks have added Physical damage equal to 3 % of maximum Life
