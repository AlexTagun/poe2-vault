# Clear Skies

**Base:** Delirium Precursor Tablet
**Class:** Tablet

**Requires: Level 65**

## Implicit

- Adds a Mirror of Delirium to a Map 10 uses remaining

## Modifiers

- Delirium Fog in your Maps never dissipates
- Delirium in Map increases (-10 — 10) % slower with distance from the mirror

## Flavour

> A simple instruction that will purify an entire region, making it safe and kind for weary travellers.
