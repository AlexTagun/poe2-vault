**The Searing Touch Pyrophyte Staff** is a **[Unique Staff](/Unique+Staves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Staves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Searing Touch Pyrophyte Staff** is a staff that focuses on fire damage, ignite effects, and increased cast speed for spellcasters. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Burn to cinders, scar and maim, Rule a world, bathed in flame.

### Path of Exile 2 The Searing Touch Pyrophyte Staff Stats & Modifiers

**The Searing Touch Pyrophyte Staff**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Grants Skill: Level 5 Solar Orb  
(80-120)% increased Fire Damage  
(10-20)% increased Cast Speed  
100% increased Flammability Magnitude  
100% increased Ignite Magnitude  
Ignites you inflict spread to other Enemies that stay within 1.5 metres for 1 second

### PoE 2 The Searing Touch Pyrophyte Staff Requirements

To equip **The Searing Touch Pyrophyte Staff** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 16, 31 INT

* **The Searing Touch Pyrophyte Staff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
