**Volcanic Fissure** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Volcanic Fissure** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Volcanic Fissure Effects

**Volcanic Fissure**provides the following effects:

* Converts 80% of Physical Damage to Fire Damage
* While Dual Wielding, both weapons hit with
* 30% less attack speed
* Fissures branch 2 times
* Fissure duration is 12 seconds
* Limit 8 Fissures

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–20)% chance to cause an Aftershock

### How to get Volcanic Fissure in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Volcanic Fissure**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 7**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 31, with at least 72 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength"), and must be used with a [Two Hand Mace](/Maces "Path of Exile 2 Maces") or [One Hand Mace](/Maces "Path of Exile 2 Maces").

### Path of Exile 2 Volcanic Fissure Notes and Tips

* In Patch 0.3.0, Volcanic Fissure:  
  + Now creates two branches that emerge from either side of the main fissure.
  + The length of the fissure is now affected by area modifiers.
  + Now has a minimum Gem level of 7 (previously 9).
  + Now has 80% of Physical Damage converted to Fire Damage (previously 60%), and now deals 88-145% Attack Damage at Gem levels 9-20 (previously 84-163%).
  + Aftershocks cannot trigger more than once every 0.2 seconds.
  + Quality now provides 0-20% chance to cause an Aftershock, instead of +0-4 seconds to Fissure Duration. Allied Slams now trigger Volcanic Fissures. Re-triggers are now treated as Aftershocks and will scale with Aftershock specific effects.
