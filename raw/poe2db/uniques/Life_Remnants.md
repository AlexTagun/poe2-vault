# Life Remnants

**Base:** Buff
**Class:** Persistent , Remnant

## Base Stats

Level: (1 — 20)

**Requires: Level (1 — 90)**

## Modifiers

- Remnants last for 8 seconds
- 25 % chance to spawn a Remnant on killing an enemy
- Spawn a Remnant on Hitting a target, no more than once every 3 seconds
- Each Remnant grants (11 — 410) Life
