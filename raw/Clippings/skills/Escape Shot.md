**Escape Shot** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Escape Shot** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Escape Shot Effects

**Escape Shot**provides the following effects:

Escape Shot

* Converts 80% of Physical Damage to Cold Damage
* Projectile count cannot be modified
* +0.7 seconds to Total Attack Time
* (300–585)% more Freeze Buildup
* Impact radius is 2.4 metres

Ice Fragments

* Attack Damage: (55–172)% of base
* Projectile Speed: 32 Units per Second
* Detonation Time is 2.5 seconds
* Converts 100% of Physical Damage to Cold Damage
* Fires 4 Projectiles
* Explosion radius is 1.3 metres

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–120)% more Freeze Buildup

### How to get Escape Shot in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Escape Shot** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 1**.
* To use the Escape Shot gem, it requires Character Level 1, and must be used with a [Bow](/Bows "Path of Exile 2 Bows").

### Path of Exile 2 Escape Shot Notes and Tips

* Escape Shot now creates ice fragments in its area of effect. These fragments explode after a short duration to deal additional damage in Patch 0.3.0
* Additionally, Escape Shot now causes you to travel 6 metres back (previously 4.5 metres) in Patch 0.3.0
