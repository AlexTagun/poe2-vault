**Guiding Palm of the Mind** is a **[Unique Scepter](/__tag__ "Path of Exile 2 __tag__")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Guiding Palm of the Mind** grants Purity of Lightning that allows you to deal Lightning Damage to enemies. It can also boost your stats, like Dexterity, and can boost the Lightning Damage of your allies. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "Deep in thought, you would tremble the very air before you.  
> Wreathed in light, you nurtured them all.  
> And yet... Your nature became you."  
> The Dreamer mused with aching heart, as remnants of forking tendrils burst forth.

### Path of Exile 2 Guiding Palm of the Mind Stats & Modifiers

**Guiding Palm of the Mind**has the following Stats:

Spirit: 100

**Guiding Palm of the Mind**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 8 [Purity of Lighting](/Purity+of+Lighting "Path of Exile 2 Purity of Lighting")  
Gain 25% of Damage as Extra Lightning Damage  
Allies in your Presence deal 1 to (56-70) added Attack Lightning Damage  
50% of your Base Life Regeneration is granted to Allies in your Presence  
+(20-30) to Dexterity  
25% increased Light Radius  
Grants effect of Guided Tempest Shrine

### PoE 2 Guiding Palm of the Mind Requirements

To equip **Guiding Palm of the Mind** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 65, 17 STR, 38 INT

### How to get Guiding Palm of the Mind in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Guiding Palm of the Mind** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
