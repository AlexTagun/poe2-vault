**Doomfletch Composite Bow** is a **[Unique Shortbow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Doomfletch Composite Bow,** a finely crafted bow that enhances physical strikes, improves dexterity, rapidly restores mana, and channels elemental energy to deal additional damage with every attack. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "Toasted or frozen  
> Or twitching in the light  
> I'm not fussy  
> And neither is Death."  
> - Koralus Doomfletch

### Path of Exile 2 Doomfletch Composite Bow Stats & Modifiers

**Doomfletch Composite Bow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (24-26) to (41-43)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.2

**Doomfletch Composite Bow**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (5–7) to (10–12) Physical Damage  
+10 to Dexterity  
(30–50)% increased Mana Regeneration Rate  
Attacks with this Weapon gain 50% of Physical damage as Extra damage of each Element

### PoE 2 Doomfletch Composite Bow Requirements

To equip **Doomfletch Composite Bow** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 22
* Stats: 41 Dex

### How to get Doomfletch Composite Bow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Doomfletch Composite Bow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
