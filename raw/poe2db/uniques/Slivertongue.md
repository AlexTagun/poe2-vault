# Slivertongue

**Base:** Zealot Bow
**Class:** Bows

## Base Stats

Physical Damage: (71-79) to (112-119)
Critical Hit Chance: 5%
Attacks per Second: 1.2

**Requires: Level 39 , 70 Dex**

- Adds (40 — 48) to (65 — 72) Physical Damage

- Leeches (4 — 7) % of Physical Damage as Mana
