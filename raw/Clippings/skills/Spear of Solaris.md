**Spear of Solaris** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Spear of Solaris** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Spear of Solaris Effects

**Spear of Solaris**provides the following effects:

Impact

* Requires 20 Glory to use
* Generates 100% of Monster Power as Glory for this Skill on Heavy Stunning an enemy
* Converts 75% of Physical Damage to Fire Damage
* +1.4 seconds to Total Attack Time
* Can't be Evaded
* Impact radius is 2 metres

Pulse

* Attack Damage: (27–110)%
* Generates 100% of Monster Power as Glory for this Skill on Heavy Stunning an enemy
* Ground spear pulses every 0.2 seconds, up to 20 times
* Converts 75% of Physical Damage to Fire Damage
* Pulse radius is 2 metres

Ignited Ground

* Attack Damage: (350–1429)%
* Generates 100% of Monster Power as Glory for this Skill on Heavy Stunning an enemy
* Converts 100% of Physical Damage to Fire Damage
* Ignited Ground radius is 2 metres
* Gains Radius over the Ground Spear's lifetime, up to 100% more at the end of the Spear's lifetime

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–25)% chance to retain 40% of Glory on use

### How to get Spear of Solaris in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Primal Strikes**can be crafted by engraving it to an appropriate level of an [Uncut Skill Gem](/Uncut+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Uncut Skill Gems").

* **Spear of Solaris**can be crafted by engraving it onto an Uncut Skill Gem Level 13.
* To use the Spear of Solaris gem, it requires Character Level 52, and it must be used with a [Spear](/Spears "Path of Exile 2 Spears").

### Path of Exile 2 Spear of Solaris Notes and Tips

* In Patch 0.3.0, Spear of Solaris
  + now deals approximately 29% less Damage, while the Pulses now deal 50% more Damage.
  + The Ignited Ground now gains up to 100% Radius of the Ground Spear's Lifetime (previously 30%), and now deals 67% more Damage.
  + Quality now provides 0-25% chance to retain 40% of Glory on use, instead of 0-40% more Magnitude of Ignite inflicted.
