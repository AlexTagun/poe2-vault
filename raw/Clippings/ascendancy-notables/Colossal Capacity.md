**Colossal Capacity** is an **[Ascendancy Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (POE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. Colossal Capacity is an exclusive skill for the [Titan](/Titan "Path of Exile 2 Titan") [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies"). Selecting an Ascendancy opens up a distinct skill tree tailored to that subclass, granting access to unique talents and traits. Every character class offers three Ascendancy choices, allowing players to personalize their combat style and progression path intricately.

### Colossal Capacity Path of Exile 2 Information

The Colossal Capacity skill has the following effects:

* Carry a Chest which adds 20 Inventory Slots

### How to Obtain Colossal Capacity in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Colossal Capacity** is a skill that is exclusively located in the skill tree of the [Titan](/Titan "Path of Exile 2 Titan") Ascendancy class. The [Titan](/Titan "Path of Exile 2 Titan") subclass is exclusive to the [Warrior](/Warrior "Path of Exile 2 Warrior").
