**Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff** is a **[Unique Quarterstaff](/Unique+Quarterstaves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Quarterstaves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff** increases elemental damage with attacks, grants additional critical hit chance, raises the level of all elemental skills, and allows triggered skills to refund half of the energy spent. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The edges bend, the world flexes, the infinite spills into view.

### Path of Exile 2 Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff Stats & Modifiers

**Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage:: 57-94  
Critical Hit Chance: 10%  
Attacks per Second: 1.4  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff**features the following Modifiers:

100% increased Elemental Damage with Attacks  
+(5–10)% to Critical Hit Chance  
+(2–4) to Level of all Elemental Skills  
Trigger skills refund half of Energy spent

### PoE 2 Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff Requirements

To equip **Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 65, 89 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity"), 36 [Int](/Int "Path of Exile 2 Int")

### How to get Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Collapsing Horizon Wyrm Quarterstaff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
