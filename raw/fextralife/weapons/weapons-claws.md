# Claws

Claws emphasize rapid, close-combat engagements, offering life and mana steal to sustain through fights. They are Dexterity-based, supporting builds that focus on speed and continuous damage.

## Affixes

- #% increased Physical Damage, # to Accuracy Rating

- #% increased Attack Speed
