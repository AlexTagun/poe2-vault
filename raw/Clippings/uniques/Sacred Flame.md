**Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre** is a **[Unique Sceptre](/Unique+Sceptres "Path of Exile 2 Unique Sceptres")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre** grants extra fire damage, allows allies in your presence to gain additional fire damage and regenerate life per second, and causes enemies in your presence to resist elemental damage proportionally to their lowest resistance. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Fire destroys, but fire also purifies.  
> Life always springs anew.

### Path of Exile 2 Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre Stats & Modifiers

**Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre**has the following Stats:

Spirit: 100

**Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: [Purity of Fire](/Purity+of+Fire "Path of Exile 2 Purity of Fire")  
Gain (40–60)% of Damage as Extra Fire Damage  
Allies in your Presence Regenerate (2–3)% of their Maximum Life per second  
Allies in your Presence Gain (20–30)% of Damage as Extra Fire Damage  
Enemies in your Presence Resist Elemental Damage based on their Lowest Resistance

### PoE 2 Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre Requirements

To equip **Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 26, 17 STR, 38  INT

### How to get Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Sacred Flame Shrine Sceptre** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
