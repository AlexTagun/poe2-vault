# Path of Exile 2: Online & Multiplayer

## Party System

The game supports parties of up to six members. Players can access the Social tab by pressing **J** on PC or through the menu on console platforms. Party invitations can be sent to friends or by clicking names in chat.

## Cross-Platform Support

"Team up with players across different platforms, including PC, Xbox, and PlayStation." Cross-progression allows seamless continuation of progress across these platforms when linked to your account.

## Local Co-op

Local cooperative play is available on consoles. After connecting an additional controller, a pop-up appears on the main menu allowing you to select local co-op mode. "In local co-op, all progress is shared between players for streamlined gameplay."

## Loot Allocation System

Three item allocation options exist, configured in Game settings:

- **Free for All**: All players can immediately pick up any dropped items
- **Short Allocation**: Items are temporarily reserved for one player before becoming available to others
- **Permanent Allocation**: Items remain exclusive to a single player

Quest items cannot be traded regardless of allocation type, though other drops can be traded between party members.

## Guilds

Guild functionality is planned. A Guild Stash Tab for shared item storage is mentioned, though this feature isn't yet available in the store.
