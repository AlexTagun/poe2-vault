# Weapons

Weapons in Path of Exile 2 serve as the primary means for dealing damage to Enemies and Bosses, offering a wide range of types to match various playstyles.

## Weapon Categories

- Talismans
- Axes (One-Handed and Two-Handed)
- Bows
- Claws
- Crossbows
- Daggers
- Flails
- Maces (One-Handed and Two-Handed)
- Quarterstaves
- Sceptres
- Spears
- Staves
- Swords (One-Handed and Two-Handed)
- Wands
- Traps

## Key Mechanics

**Gem Sockets:** Weapons no longer require sockets for Skill Gems (change from PoE 1). One-handed weapons can feature up to one socket; two-handed weapons may have up to two sockets.

**Weapon Swapping:** Players can maintain two active weapon sets and switch between them using the X key (keyboard) or radial menu (controller). Individual skills can be configured to automatically use specific weapon sets.

**Dual Specialization:** The passive skill tree supports weapon-specific allocations, with nodes marked in red for Set I and green for Set II.

## Item Rarities

- Normal
- Magic
- Rare
- Unique

Each rarity tier offers progressively more modifiers and enhancements through crafting orbs.

## Crafting / Currencies

- Orb of Transmutation — upgrades Normal to Magic
- Orb of Augmentation — adds modifier to Magic item
- Regal Orb — upgrades Magic to Rare
- Exalted Orb — adds modifier to Rare item
- Ezomyte Runes — socket enhancements via Artificer's Orb
