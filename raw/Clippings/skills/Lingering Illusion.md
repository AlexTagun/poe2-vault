**Lingering Illusion** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Lingering Illusion** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Lingering Illusion Effects

**Lingering Illusion**provides the following effects:

* Cost: 10 Mana
* Illusions have 20% of your Maximum Life
* 28% Chance to grant an Additional Power Charge when killed

Additional Effect from Quality:

* +0(0-20)% Chance to grant an additional Power Charge when killed

### How to get Lingering Illusion in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Lingering Illusion** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 8**.
* To use the Lingering Illusion gem, it requires Character Level 26 and above, with 34 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") and 34 [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") or more.
