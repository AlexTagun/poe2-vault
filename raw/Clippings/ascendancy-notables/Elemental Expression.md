**Elemental Expression** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Elemental Expression** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Elemental Expression Effects

**Elemental Expression**provides the following effects:

Fiery Explosion

* Critical Hit Chance: 7.00%
* Explosion Radius is 3.6 metres
* Deals (8–244) to (12–366) Fire Damage

Icy Wave

* Critical Hit Chance: 12.00%
* Pierces All Targets
* Fires 3 Projectiles
* Deals (7–200) to (10–300) Cold Damage

Arcing Bolt

* Critical Hit Chance: 9.00%
* Chains 7 Times
* Deals (3–84) to (16-477) Lightning Damage

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–20)% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate

### How to get Elemental Expression in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Elemental Expression** can be crafted by engraving it to an appropriate level of an [Uncut Skill Gem](/Uncut+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Uncut Skill Gems").

* **Elemental Expression** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level** (1–20).
* To use the **Elemental Expression** gem, it requires Character Level (1–90).

### Path of Exile 2 Elemental Expression Notes and Tips

* The Elemental Expression's Fire Damage was increased from (5-152 to 8-228) to (8-244 to 12-366) in Patch 0.3.0
* The Elemental Expression's Ice Damage was increased from (4-125 to 7-187) to (7-200 to 10-300) in Patch 0.3.0
* The Elemental Expression's Lightning Damage was increased from (2-53 to 11-298) to ( 3-84 to 16-477) in Patch 0.3.0
