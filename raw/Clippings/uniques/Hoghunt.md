**Hoghunt Felled Greatclub** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Hoghunt Felled Greatclub** is a two-handed mace with high physical damage, increased critical strike chance, and the ability to maim enemies on critical hits. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> There was a very clear and delicious reason why the Ezomytes chose tostop their flight and settle in Phaaryl.

### Path of Exile 2 Hoghunt Felled Greatclub Stats & Modifiers

**Hoghunt Felled Greatclub**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (29-33) to (41-45)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 0.99  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Hoghunt Felled Greatclub**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (16-20) to (23-27) Physical Damage  
+15% to Critical Strike Chance  
10% reduced Attack Speed  
+10 to Strength  
Maim on Critical Hit
