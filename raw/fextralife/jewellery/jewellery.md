# Jewellery

Jewellery in Path of Exile 2 consists of accessories that players can equip alongside their Armor. Unlike armor, jewellery enhances character abilities through stat boosts rather than physical defense.

## Item Rarities

- **Normal** — No modifiers. Upgrade with Orb of Transmutation.
- **Magic** — 1–2 modifiers. Enhance with Orb of Augmentation.
- **Rare** — Up to 6 modifiers. Apply Regal Orb to upgrade from Magic.
- **Unique** — Fixed predefined modifiers. Often playstyle-defining.

## Crafting Currency

| Currency | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Orb of Transmutation | Normal → Magic |
| Orb of Augmentation | Adds affix to Magic item |
| Regal Orb | Magic → Rare |
| Exalted Orb | Adds random affix to Rare |
| Chaos Orb | Replaces random modifier |
| Divine Orb | Randomizes numerical values |
