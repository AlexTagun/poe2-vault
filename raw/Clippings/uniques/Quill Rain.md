**Quill Rain Shortbow** is a **[Unique Shortbow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Quill Rain Shortbow** is a bow that excels in attack speed and arrow speed but deals reduced damage per shot. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "The rain of a thousand quills that whittlepresent into past, life into death."- Rigwald of the Ezomytes

### Path of Exile 2 Quill Rain Shortbow Stats & Modifiers

**Quill Rain Shortbow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 7-14  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 2.5

**Quill Rain Shortbow**features the following Modifiers:

100% increased Attack Speed  
+(10-20) to Dexterity  
(50-100)% increased Arrow Speed  
40% less Attack Damage

### PoE 2 Quill Rain Shortbow Requirements

To equip **Quill Rain Shortbow**,players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 5, 14 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity")

### How to get Quill Rain Shortbow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Quill Rain Shortbow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
