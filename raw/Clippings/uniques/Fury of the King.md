**Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman** is a **[Unique Talisman](/Unique+Talismans "Path of Exile 2 Unique Talismans")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman** will provide the user with the skill of Molten Crash as well as a significant increase in flammability magnitude. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Gruthkul was the Mother of Despair... but one day, the Father will return, and discover the fate of his children.

### Path of Exile 2 Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman Stats & Modifiers

**Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 50.4-105  
Fire Damage: 21.6-45  
Critical Hit Chance: 8%  
Attacks per Second: (0.96-1.02)  
Weapon Range: 1.2

**Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (503-589) to (647-713) Fire Damage  
(15-20)% reduced Attack Speed  
+(30-40) to Strength  
Bear Skills Convert 80% of Physical Damage to Fire Damage  
Skills which require Glory generate (2-5) Glory every 2 seconds  
Enemies in your Presence have Exposure  
exposure art variation [1]

### PoE 2 Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman Requirements

To equip **Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 72, 94 Str, 67 Int

### How to get Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Fury of the King Ashbark Talisman** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
