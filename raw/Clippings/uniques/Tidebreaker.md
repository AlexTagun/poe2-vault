**Tidebreaker Pointed Maul** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Tidebreaker Pointed Maul,** a heavy weapon that empowers melee abilities, boosts intelligence, significantly raises stun potential, and channels all impact damage into chilling force. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The sea strikes the rock relentlessly.  
> Whether in one day or in ten thousand years,  
> eventually the rock will crumble,  
> and the Brine King's domain will grow.

### Path of Exile 2 Tidebreaker Pointed Maul Stats & Modifiers

**Tidebreaker Pointed Maul**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (150-170) to (224-255)  
Critical Hit Chance: 6.5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.1  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Tidebreaker Pointed Maul**features the following Modifiers:

(120–150)% increased Physical Damage  
+(2–3) to Level of all Melee Skills  
+(20–30) to Intelligence  
Causes (150–200)% increased Stun Buildup  
All Damage from Hits with this Weapon Contributes to Chill Magnitude

### PoE 2 Tidebreaker Pointed Maul Requirements

To equip **Tidebreaker Pointed Maul** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 45
* Stats: 80 Str

### How to get Tidebreaker Pointed Maul in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Tidebreaker Pointed Maul** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
