**Glacial Cascade** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Glacial Cascade** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Glacial Cascade Effects

**Glacial Cascade**provides the following effects:

Wave

* Converts 60% of Physical Damage to Cold Damage
* Cannot Freeze
* Burst radius is 0.6 metres
* Wave length is 4.8 metres
* Causes 8 Bursts

Final Burst

* Attack Damage: (120–374)%
* Converts 60% of Physical Damage to Cold Damage
* Cannot Freeze
* Consumes Freeze on Non-Unique enemies to deal 250% more Damage
* Consumes Freeze on Unique enemies to deal 425% more Damage
* Cannot Knock Enemies Back
* Burst radius is 0.9 metres

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Gains (0–15)% of Physical damage as Cold damage

### How to get Glacial Cascade in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Glacial Cascade**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 1**.
* To use this skill gem, it must be used with a [Quarterstaff](/Quarterstaves "Path of Exile 2 Quarterstaves").

**Glacial Cascade** can be crafted by engraving it to an appropriate level of an [Uncut Skill Gem](/Uncut+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Uncut Skill Gems").

### Path of Exile 2 Glacial Cascade Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0  
  + Glacial Cascade no longer has Consumes Freeze on enemies to deal 350% more Damage. It now Consumes Freeze on non-Unique enemies to deal 150% more Damage, and Consumes Freeze on Unique enemies to deal 500% more Damage.
  + Burst Radius is now 0.6 metres (previously 0.5 metres).
  + The Final Burst Radius is now 0.9-1.2 metres at Gem levels 1-20 (previously 0.7 metres at all Gem levels).
  + Quality now provides Gain 0-15% of Physical Damage as Cold Damage (previously 0-10%).
