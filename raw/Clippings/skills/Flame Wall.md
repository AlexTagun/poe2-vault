**Flame Wall** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Flame Wall** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Flame Wall Effects

**Flame Wall**provides the following effects:

Wall

* Wall deals (1.8–59.6) Fire Damage per second
* Wall duration is (4.5–6.4) seconds
* Ignites as though dealing (5–181) to (8–271) Fire damage

Projectile Damage

* Projectiles which pass through the wall deal (2–27) to (3–41) Added Fire Damage
* Projectiles which pass through the Infused wall deal (1–3) to (4–65) Added Lightning Damage

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Inflicts (0–20)% more Flammability Magnitude

### How to get Flame Wall in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Flame Wall**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 1**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 1.

### Path of Exile 2 Flame Wall Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0  
  + Flame Wall can now consume a Lightning Infusion to grant additional Lightning Damage to projectiles that pass through it, as well as the Fire Damage.
  + Flame Wall no longer causes projectiles that pass through it to ignite enemies. Now deals 1.8 to 59.6 Fire Damage per second to enemies in the wall at Gem levels 1-20.
  + Now ignites as though dealing 5-8 Fire Damage at gem level 1 (previously 9 to 14), scaling up to 181-271 at gem level 20 (previously 299-448).
  + Now has a base Critical Hit chance of 5% (previously 0%).
