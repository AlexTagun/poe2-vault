# Tablets

Tablets are special items used at Precursor Towers to enhance Atlas encounters. They support up to two mods that modify map conditions.

## Tablet Types

| Tablet | Primary Effect |
|--------|---|
| Breach Precursor Tablet | (5–10) Maps in Range contain Breaches |
| Delirium Precursor Tablet | (5–10) Maps in Range contain Mirrors of Delirium |
| Precursor Tablet | (5–10) Maps in Range are Irradiated |
| Expedition Precursor Tablet | (5–10) Maps in Range contain Expedition Encounters |
| Ritual Precursor Tablet | (5–10) Maps in Range contain Ritual Altars |
| Abyss Precursor Tablet | Adds Abysses to a Map |

## Modifier Categories

### Rarity & Quantity
- Collector's: Item rarity increases
- Bountiful: Gold found increases
- Treasurer's: Additional rare chests

### Monster
- Breeding: Magic monster density boost
- Brimming: Rare monster frequency increase
- Challenger's: Unique monsters gain additional modifiers

### Specialized Encounter
- Of the Horde (Breach): Breach magic monster spawning
- Of Madness (Delirium): Mirror progression speed modification
- Of Sacrifice (Ritual): Tribute gain enhancement
- Of the Horde (Abyss): Abyss monster density increase

Modifiers typically offer 15-100% range adjustments depending on effect type.
