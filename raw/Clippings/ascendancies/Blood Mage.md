**Blood Mage** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Witch](/Witch "Path of Exile 2 Witch")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Blood Mage** taps into life energy, making sacrifices for incredible power. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Blood Mage Key Features

The Blood Mage is a master of life and energy. Unleash blood magic spells while also gaining the capability of leeching life back from enemies.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Blood Barbs](/Blood+Barbs "Path of Exile 2 Blood Barbs"): Bleeding you inflict on Cursed targets is Aggravated. Elemental Damage also Contributes to Bleeding Magnitude
* [Crimson Power](/Crimson+Power "Path of Exile 2 Crimson Power"): Gain Energy Shield from equipped Body Armour as extra maximum Life
* [Gore Spike](/Gore+Spike "Path of Exile 2 Gore Spike"): 1% increased Critical Damage Bonus per 50 Life
* [Grasping Wounds](/Grasping+Wounds "Path of Exile 2 Grasping Wounds"): 25% of Life Loss from Hits is prevented, then that much Life is lost over 4 seconds instead
* [Sanguimancy](/Sanguimancy "Path of Exile 2 Sanguimancy"): Grants Skill: [Life Remnants](/Life+Remnants "Path of Exile 2 Life Remnants"). Skills gain a Base Life Cost equal to Base Mana Cost
* [Sanguine Tides](/Sanguine+Tides "Path of Exile 2 Sanguine Tides"): Gain 1 Life Flask Charge per 4% Life spent, On Hitting an Enemy while a Life Flask is at full Charges, 40% of its Charges are consumed, and Gain 1% of damage as Physical damage for 3 seconds per Charge consumed this way
* [Sunder the Flesh](/Sunder+the+Flesh "Path of Exile 2 Sunder the Flesh"): Base Critical Hit Chance for Spells is 15%
* [Vitality Siphon](/Vitality+Siphon "Path of Exile 2 Vitality Siphon"): 10% of Spell Damage Leeched as Life
* [Whispers of the Flesh](/Whispers+of+the+Flesh "Path of Exile 2 Whispers of the Flesh"): Enemies you curse have at least 15% of Life Reserved and Targets Cursed by you have 100% reduced Life Regeneration Rate

### Path of Exile 2 Blood Mage Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Blood Mage**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Survivability:**A **Blood Mage** will have the option to leech life from enemies, allowing the **Blood Mage** to sustain Life in the middle of combat.

**+ Damage**: With the right equipment, a **Blood Mage** can deal massive amounts of damage to single-target and grouped-up enemies.

### Cons

**-****Gear Dependent:** Being **Gear Dependent** as a **Blood Mage** can be difficult, as resources can be pretty limited.

**- Trade-offs**: The **Sanguimancy** node can be a disadvantage if the player becomes careless, as mana cost will also apply to life.
