**Rhoa Mount** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Rhoa Mount** can be obtained by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Rhoa Mount Effects

**Rhoa Mount**provides the following effects:

* 100% increased Minion Accuracy Rating
* 15% increased Movement Speed while mounted
* 80% reduced Movement Speed Penalty from using Skills while mounted

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Minions have (0–20)% more Maximum Life

### How to get Rhoa Mount in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Rhoa Mount** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 14**.
* To use the Rhoa Mount gem, it requires Character Level 58, with 133 [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity").

### Path of Exile 2 Rhoa Mount Notes and Tips

* Rhoa Mount now grants 15% increased movement speed while mounted. However, it now grants 80% less movement speed penalty while using skills (previously 100%), approximately cancelling out the bonus while using a skill in Patch 0.3.0
* Heavy Stun buildup on you now starts decaying if you avoid getting hit for 2 seconds while riding the Rhoa in Patch 0.3.0
