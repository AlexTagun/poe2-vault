**Pin Buildup** is a **[Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. This passive Skill increases your chances to Pin enemies. Players can unlock any skill from the Passive Skill Tree by spending a skill point on a skill connected to their starting point or already unlocked skills, Passive Skills that grant attributes, let you select which attribute you want to increase, allowing players to explore the tree in any direction.

### Information for Pin Buildup in Path of Exile 2

* Type: [Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")
* [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"): [Tactician](/Tactician "Path of Exile 2 Tactician")
* Requirements: **TBA**

### Path of Exile 2 Pin Buildup Effects

Pin Buildup provides the following effects:

* 20% Increased Pin Buildup.

### Path of Exile 2 Pin Buildup Notes and Tips for

* Certain skills and effects allow damage to build up Pinned. Once this build up passes the enemy's Pinned Treshold, they are Pinned, preventing them moving, being moved, or Evading for 4 seconds. They are also Light Stunned when they become Pinned. Pinned targets count as immobilised.
