**Gemling****Legionnaire**is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Mercenary](/Mercenary "Path of Exile 2 Mercenary")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Gemling Legionnaire** enhances their abilities by embedding skill gems into their body. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

> The more you embrace virtue gems, the more power they offer...

### Path of Exile 2 Gemling Legionnaire Key Features

The **Gemling Legionnaire** embeds gems into their body to enhance the power of abilities. Gain additional skill slots, maximum resistances, and use the capability of gems to further increase their effects.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Adaptive Capability](/Adaptive+Capability "Path of Exile 2 Adaptive Capability"): [Attribute](/Stats+&+Attributes "Path of Exile 2 Stats & Attributes") Requirements of Gems can be satisfied by your highest [Attribute](/Stats+&+Attributes "Path of Exile 2 Stats & Attributes")
* [Advanced Thaumaturgy](/Advanced+Thaumaturgy "Path of Exile 2 Advanced Thaumaturgy"): Thaumaturgical Dynamism Buff, which while active causes you to passively generate a Power, Frenzy or Endurance Charge once every five seconds. The type of Charge you generate is determined by the [Attribute](/Stats+&+Attributes "Path of Exile 2 Stats & Attributes") Requirements of your socketed [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"). The higher the total Strength Requirement total of your socketed [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), the more likely you will be to generate an Endurance Charge. Higher total Dexterity Requirement corresponds to a higher chance for Frenzy Charges, and Intelligence to Power Charges.
* [Crystalline Potential](/Crystalline+Potential "Path of Exile 2 Crystalline Potential"): +10% to Quality of all Skills
* [Enhanced Effectiveness](/Enhanced+Effectiveness "Path of Exile 2 Enhanced Effectiveness"): 20% less [Attributes](/Stats+&+Attributes "Path of Exile 2 Stats & Attributes"). Inherent bonuses from [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence"), [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength"), and [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") are doubled
* [Gem Studded](/Gem+Studded "Path of Exile 2 Gem Studded"): For each colour of Socketed [Gem](/Gems "Path of Exile 2 Gems") that is most numerous, gain:
  + Red: Hits against you have no Critical Damage Bonus.
  + Blue: Skills have 30% less cost.
  + Green: 40% less Movement Speed Penalty from using Skills while Moving.
* [Implanted Gems](/Implanted+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Implanted Gems"): A choice to select one of the three possibilities: +2 to the the level of all Strength-based Skills, +2 to level of all Dexterity-based Skills, +2 to the level of all Intelligence-based Skills
* [Integrated Efficiency](/Integrated+Efficiency "Path of Exile 2 Integrated Efficiency"): Grants +20% increased Damage per red Support Gem, +6% increased Skill Speed per green Support Gem, and +20% increased Critical Hit Chance per blue Support Gem
* [Thaumatological Infusion](/Thaumatological+Infusion "Path of Exile 2 Thaumatological Infusion"): +1% to Maximum Cold Resistance per 3 Blue [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") Socketed. +1% to Maximum Fire Resistance per 3 Red [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") Socketed. +1% to Maximum Lightning Resistance per 3 Green [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") Socketed

### Path of Exile 2 Gemling Legionnaire Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Gemling Legionnaire**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Flexible builds:**Gemling Legionnaire can be a flexible build, as it can both be offensive and defensive.

**+ Attribute Stacking**: The Gemling Legionnaire has **Adaptive Capability**, an ascendancy node that bases equipment or skill requirements on the highest attribute.

### Cons

**- Gem Quality Dependent:** The ascendancy is dependent on **Gem Quality**, so it might take a bit for builds to take off.

**- Placeholders:** Path of Exile 2 is still in early access, so **Gem Quality** bonuses will most likely be changed
