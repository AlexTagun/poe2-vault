**Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub** can crush enemies on hit, which makes them stun if they are on full life. It can also increase your physical damage, but will sacrifice some of your accuracy and attack speed. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Drunken Erqi boasted to Tukohama; the God of War challenged him to a clash of strength. Woe to the Divine - he should have made it a test of skill!

### Path of Exile 2 Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub Stats & Modifiers

**Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (539-616) to (735-840)  
Critical Strike Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 0.715  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub**features the following Modifiers:

Warcries Empower an additional Attack  
+150 Strength Requirement  
(600-700)% increased Physical Damage  
(-300--200) to Accuracy Rating  
35% reduced Attack Speed  
Causes (40-60)% increased Stun Duration  
Heavy Stuns Enemies that are on Full Life

### PoE 2 Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub Requirements

To equip **Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 60, 239 Str

### How to get Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Marohi Erqi Totemic Greatclub** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
