**Spire of Ire Helix Spear** is a [Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Spire of Ire Helix Spear,** a spear that channels raw chaos, converting life leech to scale with corruption, triggering chaos infusions from charges, and lacing every strike with deadly energy. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The spear was specially forged to assassinate Voll,  
> but Maligaro never got a chance to use it...

### Path of Exile 2 Spire of Ire Helix Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Spire of Ire Helix Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 37-68  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.84-1.92)  
Weapon Range: 1.5

**Spire of Ire Helix Spear**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Spear Throw  
Grants Skill: Level 15 Chaotic Surge  
(15–20)% increased Attack Speed  
Leeches (10-20)% of Physical Damage as Life  
Adds (167-201) to (267-333) Chaos damage  
When you Consume a Charge Trigger Chaotic Infusion to gain 2 Chaos Infusion  
Life Leech recovers based on your Chaos damage instead of Physical damage

### PoE 2 Spire of Ire Helix Spear Requirements

To equip **Spire of Ire Helix Spear** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 65
* Stats: 36 Str, 89 Dex

### How to get Spire of Ire Helix Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Spire of Ire Helix Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
