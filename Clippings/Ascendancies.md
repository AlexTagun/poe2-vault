## Ascendancies | Path of Exile 2 Wiki

**Ascendancies or Ascendancy Classes** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")** are subclasses players can choose after reaching a certain level. Ascendancies are subclasses that allow players to specialize in specific gameplay styles and abilities. After progressing to a certain point in the game, players can select from three **Ascendancies** per [Class](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"), allowing for deeper customization of their character's abilities. Each **PoE 2 Ascendancy** path provides unique strengths and playstyle enhancement.

## Path of Exile 2 All Ascendancy Classes Guide

### The Role of Ascendancies in Path of Exile 2

**Ascendancies** serve as transformative subclasses, granting characters unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. They are pivotal in defining a character's playstyle, allowing players to tailor their builds to fit their preferences and overcome challenges innovatively.

In **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**, every base class branches into three distinct Ascendancy subclasses, offering 36 options. Unlike the original game, these Ascendancy classes are no longer unlocked through labyrinths but become available through progression in the new campaign during Acts 2, 3, and 4.

### Path of Exile 2 Unlocking the Ascendancy Skill Tree

Choosing an **Ascendancy** unlocks a specialized skill tree unique to that subclass, providing access to powerful talents and traits. Each character class has three Ascendancy options, giving players the flexibility to customize their approach to combat and progression deeply.

The video showcases several of their movesets and skills while giving brief information on their backgrounds.

### Spirit Walker Information

The [Spirit Walker](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Spirit+Walker "Path of Exile 2 Spirit Walker") is part of the [Huntress](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Huntress "Path of Exile 2 Huntress") Ascendancy Classes that you can choose if you decide to play as a Huntress in your run. The Spirit Walker has a deep connection with the Azmerian Animals and their spirits. This allows her to infuse her powers with 3 unique spirits and summon them to aid her in various combat situations.

- Stag: Summon a stampede of stags with each attack.
- Owl: Grants you boons in the form of their feathers, which will increase the amount of projectiles you throw and increase the projectile speed.
- Bear: Summon a giant bear to assist you in defeating nearby enemies.

The Spirit Walker revolves around three core Ascendancy paths: **Vivid Stampede**, **Primal Bounty**, and **Wild Protector**.

**Vivid Stampede** focuses on extra damage and AoE damage by allowing you to conjure stampeding stags whenever you attack. Any stags that travel the maximum possible distance also leave behind [Shocked Ground](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Shock "Path of Exile 2 Shock").

The **Primal Bounty** is centered around projectiles. Through this node, Owl Spirit grants mystical feathers that can be consumed to add extra projectiles to your next skill.

The **Wild Protector** focuses on companion-based combat. Activating it summons a powerful Bear Spirit Companion capable of leaping onto enemies, inflicting Maim, leeching life, and Intimidating enemies. One of the strongest advantages of this skill is that the Bear Spirit does not occupy your normal companion slot, allowing it to fight alongside your tamed beast at the same time.

Allocating points to all three nodes unlocks the **Sacred Unity**, a free passive that further enhances each skill node with stronger effects and additional utilities.

The Spirit Walker also gains access to strong supporting passives such as **Idolatry**, which greatly boosts minion-focused builds, and **The Natural Order**, which allows [Tame Beast](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Tame+Beast "Path of Exile 2 Tame Beast") to even control bosses.

### Martial Artist Information

The [Martial Artist](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Martial+Artist "Path of Exile 2 Martial Artist") is part of the [Monk](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Monk "Path of Exile 2 Monk") Ascendancy Classes that you can choose from if you have chosen to play as a Monk in your run. The Martial Artist has mastered 3 out of the 7 Hollow Techniques and is a force to be reckoned with. He can perform attacks that deal large amounts of AoE damage to clear enemies in your path.

- Hollow Form Technique: Create illusions of yourself and attack enemies with a skill of your choice.
- Hollow Focus Technique: Manifest your spiritual energy onto bells, which will appear around you and can be shattered by attacking.
- Hollow Resonance Technique: Manifest a bell on your back, it will ring each time you deal a Critical Attack and will damage enemies around you

## Ascension in Path of Exile 2: Trials and Challenges

In **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**, achieving Ascension requires players to overcome the **Great Trials**, which are scattered throughout the campaign. Each trial is deeply tied to one of the major cultures of Wraeclast, immersing players in the lore and traditions of the world. Below, we’ll explore the trials, their mechanics, and what players can expect on their journey to ascend.

![ascension trials poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_trials_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_trials_poe2_wiki_guide_600px")

The Trials in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")** are a cornerstone of the game's progression, combining challenging mechanics with meaningful rewards. Whether you’re earning boons, navigating treacherous rooms, or facing down powerful bosses, the Trials ensure a thrilling and dynamic path to Ascension.

### Path of Exile 2: The Trial of the Sekhema

### Introduction to the Maraketh Culture

The **Maraketh** are a people of the **Vastiri Plains**, defined by their harsh traditions and survival instincts in the unforgiving desert. At the pinnacle of their society lies the title of **Sekhema**, a position of unparalleled respect and authority. This title is not earned easily—it demands strength, resilience, and unwavering determination. To ascend as a Sekhema, one must complete the **Trial of the Sekhemas**, a brutal gauntlet designed to test every aspect of a warrior’s spirit: strength, cunning, willpower, and patience.

![ascension rituals poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_rituals_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_rituals_poe2_wiki_guide_600px")

### Earning the Right to Enter

Before attempting the trial, players must prove their worth by capturing the soul of a **Djinn** in a coin. Only by securing this mystical token can the trial begin. To proceed, you must place the coin in the **Relic Altar**, unlocking the entrance to the Sekhema’s challenge and begin the trial.

[![ascension relic altar poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_relic_altar_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_relic_altar_poe2_wiki_guide_600px")](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_relic_altar_poe2_wiki_guide_1920px.jpg "https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_relic_altar_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg")

### Navigating the Trial

The Trial of the Sekhemas consists of several rooms, each presenting unique challenges and requiring players to adapt their strategy.

- **Room Mechanics:** In one example, every rare monster slain sends its blood to a central chalice. Once the chalice is filled, players can progress to the next room.
- **Honour System:** During the trial, every hit you take not only deals damage but also diminishes your **honour**. If you run out of honour, you fail the trial and must secure another coin to attempt it again. The monsters' attacks are well-telegraphed, encouraging skilled movement and precision to avoid failure.

![ascension test of strength poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_test_of_strength_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_test_of_strength_poe2_wiki_guide_600px")

### Preparation and Strategy

Success in the Trial of the Sekhemas depends heavily on preparation. Ensuring your gear, skills, and flask setup are optimized is critical.

- **Room Rewards:** After completing a room, players are rewarded. For instance, you may earn a **key** to unlock treasure chests later in the trial.
- **Choice of Path:** At the end of each room, a map of the floor is revealed, offering players the opportunity to choose their next challenge. Each path has varying levels of danger and rewards, with some rooms inflicting **debilitating afflictions**. For example, the **Spiked Shell affliction** increases the life of all monsters for the remainder of the trial, making subsequent encounters more difficult.

[![ascension chests poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_chests_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_chests_poe2_wiki_guide_600px")](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_chests_poe2_wiki_guide_1920px.jpg "https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_chests_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg")

### Boons and Sacred Water

As players progress, they may encounter **Boons** —powerful blessings from the Djinn that provide significant advantages. For example, the **Sekhema’s Cloak** offers a second chance by reviving the player upon death. Another valuable resource found during the trial is **Sacred Water**, a rare and cherished item among the Djinn and Maraketh. This can be traded with the Djinn to:

- Restore honour.
- Gain additional boons.
- Remove afflictions that hinder your progress.

![ascension boons poe2 wiki guide 300px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_boons_poe2_wiki_guide_300px.jpg "ascension_boons_poe2_wiki_guide_300px") ![ascension boons services poe2 wiki guide 300px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_boons_services_poe2_wiki_guide_300px.jpg "ascension_boons_services_poe2_wiki_guide_300px")

### The Final Boss and Ascension

At the end of the floor lies a **powerful boss**, designed to push players to their limits. To ascend, you must defeat this formidable enemy. Upon victory, players earn the right to ascend and are rewarded with treasure.

- **Treasure Vault:** Use the keys found during the trial to open chests in the vault. Higher-tier keys, such as **Silver** or **Gold Keys**, unlock more valuable rewards, while lower-tier keys may restrict you to smaller treasures.
- **Altar of Ascendancy:** Completing your first ascension allows you to select your **Ascendancy Class** and grants **Two Ascendancy Points** to allocate in the skill tree.

[![ascension bosses poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_bosses_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_chests_poe2_wiki_guide_600px")](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_bosses_poe2_wiki_guide_1920px.jpg "https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_bosses_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg")

### Path of Exile 2: The Trial of Chaos

### The Vaal and Their Legacy

For players seeking a different challenge, the **Trial of Chaos** offers an alternative to the Trial of the Sekhemas. Found in the **Vaal Jungle**, this trial is rooted in the ancient practices of the Vaal civilization. Before embracing the dark science of corruption, the Vaal worshipped **Chaos**, and this trial was once used to test their High Priests. Entry requires a **token** from a mysterious entity known as the **Trialmaster**.

[![ascension templeofcaos poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_templeofcaos_poe2_wiki_guide_300px")](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_poe2_wiki_guide_1920px.jpg "https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg")

[![ascension templeofcaos 1 poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_1_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_templeofcaos_1_poe2_wiki_guide_600px")](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_1_poe2_wiki_guide_1920px.jpg "https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_1_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg")

### Mechanics of the Trial

The Trial of Chaos introduces unique mechanics and challenges that distinguish it from the Sekhema’s gauntlet.

- **Ultimatums:** Before entering each chamber, the Trialmaster offers a reward alongside a choice of **Tribulations** —modifiers that increase the difficulty of the trial. These tribulations can enhance monsters, curse the player, or introduce environmental hazards such as **Shocking Turrets** or **Trap Runes**.
- **Dynamic Rooms:** Each room presents a different challenge, such as:
	- **Survival Chambers:** Endure waves of monsters and environmental hazards for a set period.
		- **Escort Missions:** Protect a **Stone Idol** as it travels through the level while fending off descending enemies.

### Risk vs. Reward

After completing a chamber, players face a critical decision:

- **Claim Rewards:** Take the loot earned so far and exit the trial.
- **Double or Nothing:** Increase the value of rewards by adding additional tribulations, but at the risk of failure. Tribulations stack, quickly escalating the difficulty of subsequent encounters.

![ascension templeofcaos 2 poe2 wiki guide 300px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_2_poe2_wiki_guide_300px.jpg "ascension_loot_poe2_wiki_guide_300px")

### The Boss and Ascension

Once players clear the first three chambers, they face a **randomly chosen boss**. This randomized element ensures that no two runs feel the same, as different bosses combined with selected tribulations create varied challenges.

- **Rewards and Ascension Points:** Successfully defeating the boss grants rewards and two additional ascendancy points. Players may mix and match their trials or earn all their ascendancy points within a single trial.

[![ascension templeofcaos boss poe2 wiki guide 600px](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_boss_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg "ascension_bosses_poe2_wiki_guide_600px")](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_boss_poe2_wiki_guide_1920px.jpg "https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/file/Path-of-Exile-2/ascension_templeofcaos_boss_poe2_wiki_guide_600px.jpg")

### Customizing Your Path to Ascension

In **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**, the choice of trials is entirely up to the player. Whether you prefer the structured, honour-bound challenges of the Maraketh or the chaotic, risk-reward mechanics of the Vaal, you can tailor your Ascension journey to suit your playstyle.

- **Multiple Trials:** During early access, two trials (Sekhemas and Chaos) will be available, with a third trial planned for the full release.
- **Replayability:** Each trial offers unique rewards, challenges, and lore, making every attempt a fresh experience.
