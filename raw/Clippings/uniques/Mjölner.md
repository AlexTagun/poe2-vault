**Mjolner Torment Club** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Mjolner Torment Club,** a powerful weapon that demands exceptional Intelligence and Strength to wield, empowering lightning-based abilities and greatly enhancing physical force and striking speed. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Look the storm in the eye and you will have its respect.

### Path of Exile 2 Mjolner Torment Club Stats & Modifiers

**Mjolner Torment Club**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (154-198) to (256-329)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.6-1.74)  
Weapon Range: 1.1

**Mjolner Torment Club**features the following Modifiers:

+200 Intelligence Requirement  
+100 Strength Requirement  
(150–200)% increased Physical Damage  
(10–20)% increased Attack Speed  
+1 to Level of all Lightning Skills

### PoE 2 Mjolner Torment Club Requirements

To equip **Mjolner Torment Club** players must meet the following requirements:

* Requires: Level 65
* Stats: 214 Str, Int 200

### How to get Mjolner Torment Club in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Mjolner Torment Club** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
