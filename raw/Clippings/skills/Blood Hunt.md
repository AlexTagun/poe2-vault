**Blood Hunt** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Blood Hunt** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Blood Hunt Effects

**Lightning Spear**provides the following effects:

Dash

* +0.15 seconds to Total Attack Time
* Cannot cause Bleeding

Explosion

* Cannot cause Bleeding
* Explosion radius is 1.3 metres
* Enemies lose Life equal to 25% of Consumed Blood Loss
* 10% more area of effect per Blood Loss Consumed, up to 500%
* % more area of effect per (1–381) Blood Loss Consumed, up to 500%

Additional Effects From Quality:  
+(0–0.4) metres to explosion radius

### How to get Blood Hunt in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Blood Hunt**can be crafted by engraving it to an appropriate level of an [Uncut Skill Gem](/Uncut+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Uncut Skill Gems").

* **Blood Hunt**can be crafted by engraving it onto an Uncut Skill Gem Level 7.
* To use the Blood Hunt gem, it requires Character Level 7, and it must be used with a [Spear](/Spears "Path of Exile 2 Spears").

### Path of Exile 2 Blood Hunt Notes and Tips

* Blood Hunt explosion now causes Life Loss rather than unscalable damage in [Patch 0.3.0](/Patch+Notes#0.3.0 "Path of Exile 2 Patch Notes#0.3.0")
* A bug was fixed in [0.3.1 Hotfix 2](/Patch+Notes#0.3.1Hotfix2 "Path of Exile 2 Patch Notes#0.3.1Hotfix2"), where Life Loss from Blood Hunt's Explosions sometimes didn't apply when large amounts of damage were supposed to be dealt.
