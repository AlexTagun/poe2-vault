# Forgotten By Time

**Base:** Expedition Precursor Tablet
**Class:** Tablet

**Requires: Level 65**

## Implicit

- Adds a Kalguuran Expedition to a Map 10 uses remaining

## Modifiers

- Expedition Monsters in your Maps spawn with half of their Life missing
- Runic Monsters in your Maps are Duplicated

## Flavour

> Before the starlight barrier failed, before the end came, countless Kalguuran settlers sought a new life on Wraeclast. Of the towns they founded and the lives they lived, little remains...
