**The Burden of Shadows Chiming Staff** is a [Unique Staff](/Unique+Staves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Staves") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Burden of Shadows Chiming Staff** grants increased casting speed, converts skill mana costs into life costs, and allows skills to gain a portion of their damage as chaos damage based on their life cost. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Nothingness is loathe to relinquish its grip.  
> Every moment is a struggle to exist.
