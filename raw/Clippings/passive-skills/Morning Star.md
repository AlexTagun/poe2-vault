**Morning Star** is **[Weapon](/Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (POE2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. Morning Star is made of metal with a long shaft and a large head with spikes. [Weapons](/Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Weapons") are [Items](/Items "Path of Exile 2 Items") that players use to deal damage to enemies and bosses, weapons of higher ranking have additional stats and modifiers while normal weapons do not. Weapons can be obtained through opening chests, looting from enemies and bosses, and purchasing from [Merchants](/Merchants "Path of Exile 2 Merchants").

### Path of Exile 2 Morning Star Stats & Modifier

**Morning Star** has the following stats:

Physical Damage: 33-49  
Critical Hit Chance: 6.5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.45  
Weapon Range: 1.1

### Path of Exile 2 Morning Star Requirements

To equipment **Morning Star** players must meet the following requirements:

Level 45, 104 [Str](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength")

### How to Get Morning Star in Path of Exile 2

* Items such as **Morning Star** randomly drop from enemies and as loot from chests. However, farming rare enemies and bosses has a high chance of causing unique or high-tier items to drop.
