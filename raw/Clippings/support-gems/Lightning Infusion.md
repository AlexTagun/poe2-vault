**Lightning Infusion** is a **[Support Gem](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. Support Gems can be placed into the Support Gem Socket, and used to further tune the effects of Skill Gems.

### Information for Lightning Infusion in Path of Exile 2

* Type: Support
* Tier: 1
* Tags: Attack, Lightning
* Support Requirements: +5 Dex

### Path of Exile 2 Lightning Infusion Effects

**Lightning Infusion**provides the following effects:

* Supported Attacks Gain 25% of Damage as extra Lightning Damage
* Supported Attacks deal 50% less Cold and Fire Damage

**Lightning Infusion** can be used to support the following [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"):

### How to get Lightning Infusion in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Lightning Infusion** can be crafted by engraving the support skill to the associated level of an [Uncut Support Skill Gem](/Uncut+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Uncut Skill Gems").
