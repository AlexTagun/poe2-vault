# Blackheart

**Base:** Iron Ring
**Class:** Rings

**Requires: Level 1**

## Implicit

- Adds 1 to 4 Physical Damage to Attacks

## Modifiers

- (3 — 6) Life Regeneration per second
- Adds (4 — 6) to (8 — 10) Chaos Damage to Attacks
- 25 % chance to Intimidate Enemies for 4 seconds on Hit

## Flavour

> Fear is highly infectious.
