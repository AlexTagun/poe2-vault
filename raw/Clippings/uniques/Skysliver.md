**Skysliver Winged Spear** is a [Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Skysliver Winged Spear,** a purely elemental weapon with no physical bite, channeling lightning at extreme speed, striking with guaranteed power ranges and an amplified chance to shock. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Heads fall to the sand, just as the star fell from the sky

### Path of Exile 2 Skysliver Winged Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Skysliver Winged Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 12-22  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.9-2.15)  
Weapon Range: 1.5

**Skysliver Winged Spear**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Spear Throw  
No Physical Damage  
Adds 1 to (80–120) Lightning Damage  
(15–30)% increased Attack Speed  
(50–100)% increased chance to Shock  
Rolls only the minimum or maximum Damage value for each Damage Type

### PoE 2 Skysliver Winged Spear Requirements

To equip **Skysliver Winged Spear** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 16
* Stats: 12 Str, 25 Dex

### How to get Skysliver Winged Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Skysliver Winged Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
