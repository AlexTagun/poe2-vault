**Presence Area** is a **[Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. This passive increases the Area of Effect of your Presence. Players can unlock any skill from the Passive Skill Tree by spending a skill point on a skill connected to their starting point or already unlocked skills, Passive Skills that grant attributes, let you select which attribute you want to increase, allowing players to explore the tree in any direction.

### Information for Presence Area in Path of Exile 2

* Type: [Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")
* [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"): [Tactician](/Tactician "Path of Exile 2 Tactician")
* Requirements: **TBA**

### Path of Exile 2 Precense Area Effects

Presence Area provides the following effects:

* 20% increased Presence Area of Effect.

### Path of Exile 2 Presence Area Notes and Tips

* Your Presence is an area around your character within which certain effects (such as many Auras) are applied. By default, this has a 4 metre radius. The size of this area can be modified by Presence Area modifiers, but not by Skill Area modifiers.
