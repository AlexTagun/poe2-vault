# Currencies

Currencies are a type of Item in Path of Exile 2. Currencies can be used to modify, enhance, or craft gear, influence the economy, and provide utility in various ways.

## Orbs
- Chaos Orb
- Divine Orb
- Exalted Orb
- Regal Orb
- Vaal Orb
- Mirror of Kalandra
- Orb of Annulment
- Orb of Alchemy

## Crafting Materials
- Alchemy Shard
- Transmutation Shard
- Arcanist's Etcher
- Artificer's Orb

## Quality Enhancement
- Armourer's Scrap
- Blacksmith's Whetstone
- Glassblower's Bauble
- Gemcutter's Prism

## Socket Management
- Greater Jeweller's Orb
- Lesser Jeweller's Orb
- Perfect Jeweller's Orb

## Keys
- Bronze Key
- Silver Key
- Gold Key

## Artifacts
- Black Scythe
- Broken Circle
- Order
- Sun

## Catalysts (12 varieties)
Specialized crafting materials including: Adaptive, Carapace, Flesh, Neural, and Breach-themed catalysts:
- Chayula's Catalyst
- Esh's Catalyst
- Tul's Catalyst
- Uul-Netol's Catalyst
- Xoph's Catalyst
