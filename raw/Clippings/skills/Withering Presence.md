**Withering Presence** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Withering Presence** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Withering Presence Effects

**Withering Presence**provides the following effects:

* Withered duration is 6 seconds
* Withers enemies in your Presence every (1.42–1.8) seconds

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Withered duration is (0–1) second

### How to get Withering Presence in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Withering Presence** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 4**.
* To use the Withering Presence gem, it requires Character Level 10 and above, with 25 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Withering Presence Notes and Tips

* Withering Presence is now described as Aura Skills, and has the Aura tag in Patch 0.3.0
