# Hideouts — Fextralife Wiki

## What Are Hideouts?

Hideouts function as a personal space or base within Path of Exile 2. They are customizable private areas transformed from previously explored locations, serving as personal homes where players can organize resources and establish their own operational base.

## Key Benefits

- **Efficiency**: Allows for faster gameplay by keeping everything conveniently close, making crafting and map runs more efficient
- **Privacy**: Provides isolated spaces away from other players and overwhelming environmental effects
- **Customization**: Players can design and decorate their hideouts to match personal preferences

## Unlocking Hideouts

1. **Campaign Completion**: Finish all three acts on Normal Difficulty, then repeat on Cruel Difficulty (approximately 24 hours for Normal)
2. **Endgame Access**: Speak with Doryani after Act 3 completion to unlock the Atlas of Worlds
3. **Hideout Discovery**: Locate hideout icons appearing randomly on Atlas maps (typically within first 10 maps)
4. **Area Clearance**: Defeat all enemies and bosses within the discovered hideout location
5. **Activation**: Once cleared, the hideout becomes permanently accessible

## Hideout Features and Setup

**Access Method**: Open the Waypoint menu and locate the hideout button on the right side of the screen

**Customizable Elements**:
- Decorations (obtained from store or in-game rewards)
- Crafting Bench for item upgrades
- Map Device for endgame map progression
- Stash Tabs for inventory organization

**Management**: Players can switch between multiple unlocked hideouts using the hideout list
