**Disciple of Varashta** is one of the three unique [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer "Path of Exile 2 Sorcerer") [Ascendancy Classes](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") available in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Disciple of Varashta** primarily uses minions and djinns to handle her enemies while being able to stand in the back lines and cast spells and curses. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Disciple of Varashta Key Features

The **Disciple of Varashta** is a sorcereress who primarily uses Spells, Curses, Minions, and Djinns to help her deal with enemies. This allows players to clear out enemies or keep bosses busy while landing the heavier attacks.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* Ruzhan's Trap: Grans Skill: Comman Ruzhan to hurl his greatsword, spreading hazardous runes on the ground where it lands. If an enemy steps on a rune, it erupts in a fiery tornado that deals Spell damage.
* Ruzhan's Reckoning: Grans Skill: Command Ruzhan to teleport to the target location. Upon arrival, the Djinn deals Spell damage to enemies around him then slams his blazing sword into the ground, creating a wave of lava that also deals Spell damage.
* Ruzhan's Fury: Grans Skill: Comman Ruzhan to conjure two volleys of flaming embers and fire them forward. The embers explode on impact, dealing Spell damage.
* Sacred Rituals: 60% of your current Energy Shielf is added to your Armour for determining your Physical Damage Reduction from Armour
* Varashta's Intuition: 100% of Elemental Damage is taken from Mana before Life
* Navira's Fracturing: Command Navira to fracture all nearby areas of Chilled Ground that have enemies standing on them, doing high Spell damage with extreme Freeze potential.
* Navira's Oasis: Comman Navira to turn nearby Chilled Ground into Navira's Oasis, a mirage that grants you and Allies standing in it Flask charges and damage Recouped as Life and Mana. Plants in the area become Overgrown.
* Nariva's Well: Command Navira to bolster your Energy Shield, causing it to begin Recharging immediately and granting you increased Energy Shield Recharge Rate for a duration.
* Instruments of Power: You can equip a Focus while wielding a Staff. 50% reduced bonuses gained from Equipped Focus
* Baryanic Leylines: Non-Unique Time-Lost Jewels have 40% increased radius
* Kelari's Malediction: Harness Kelari's power to passively Consume Corpses in your Presence, causing Corpse Beetle Minions to burst forth. The Beetles passively follow you until Commanded. On your Command, they rush the target and explode.
* Kelari's Judgment: Channel and Command Kelari to Channel alongside you, creating blades that hang over the target location. The longer you Channel, the more blades Kelari will create. Upon release, his blades plunge into the ground and create shockwaves that deal Spell damage.
* Kelari's Deception: Command Kelari to create a blast at the target location that deals Spell damage, and leave behind a clone that persists for a short duration. This skill can store multiple charges, and using it again while a clone from a previous use is still alive causes that clone to explode, dealing even more damage.

### Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) Disciple of Varashta Skill Gems

##### Skill Gems and Customization:

To unlock new skills, [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") must be found and used. Activating a [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") opens the gemcutting menu, which allows players to level up existing skills or engrave new ones. Skill Gems retain their level, so newly engraved skills match the level of the gem, encouraging experimentation without penalty.

Since abilities are powered by Skill Gems, all classes have the freedom to use any gem they choose, offering limitless build possibilities.
