# Unique Off-Hand Equipment

Unique Off-Hand Equipment in Path of Exile 2 includes shields, bucklers, foci, and quivers with predefined modifiers tailored to specific playstyles, distinguishing them from randomized Magic and Rare items.

## Overview

- 36 unique off-hand items total
- Level requirements range from 1 to 107
- Categories: Shields, Bucklers, Foci, Quivers

## Acquisition

- Random enemy drops
- Orb of Chance on a Normal item of the appropriate base type (risk of destruction)
- Trading via the Official Path of Exile 2 Trade Site (Chaos Orbs, Exalted Orbs, Divine Orbs)

## Notable Uniques

| Name | Base | Notable Mechanic |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Svalinn | Crucible Tower Shield | Grants Cast on Block; lucky block chance |
| Drillneck | Penetrating Quiver | 100% pierce chance; projectile return mechanics |
| Rathpith Globe | Sacred Focus | Life-scaling spell damage and critical hit bonuses |
| The Surrender | Stone Tower Shield | Block recovery with stun threshold bonuses |

## Categories

### Unique Shields & Bucklers
Defense-focused with block chance and damage reduction. Some grant active skills on block.

### Unique Foci
Designed for spellcasters with spell damage and energy shield bonuses. Often have life or mana scaling mechanics.

### Unique Quivers
Bow-specific enhancements including attack speed, piercing, and projectile mechanics.
