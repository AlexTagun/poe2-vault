**Seeing Stars Plated Mace** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Seeing Stars Plated Mace** is a one-handed mace that adds cold and lightning damage, it has improved attack speed and energy regeneration. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Within lies a window.

### Path of Exile 2 Seeing Stars Plated Mace Stats & Modifiers

**Seeing Stars Plated Mace**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 33-69  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.54-1.68)  
Weapon Range: 1.1  
Lightning Damage: 1 to (40-45)

**Seeing Stars Plated Mace**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (12-16) to (22-25) Cold Damage  
Adds 1 to (40-45) Lightning Damage  
(10-20)% Increased Attack Speed  
Energy Generation is Doubled

### PoE 2 Seeing Stars Plated Mace Requirements

To equip **Seeing Stars Plated Mace** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 54, 96 [Str](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength")

### How to get Seeing Stars Plated Mace in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Seeing Stars Plated Mace** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.

### Path of Exile 2 Seeing Stars Plated Mace Notes and Tips for

* Other Notes & Tips for PoE 2 **Seeing Stars Plated Mace go here.**
* Seeing Stars now drops on the Marching Mace base-type (previously Plated Mace) in Patch 0.4.0. Existing items will not have this change.
* Seeing Stars now has "Adds 1-5 to 66-90 Lightning Damage" (previously 1 to 40-45) in Patch 0.4.0. Existing items will not have this change.
* Seeing Stars now has "Adds 24-31 to 36-46 Cold Damage" (previously 12-16 to 22-25) in Patch 0.4.0. Existing items will not have this change.
