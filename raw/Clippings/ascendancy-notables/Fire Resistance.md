**Fire Resistance** is a **[Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. This passive Skill provides you with a 15% resistance to Fire Damage. Players can unlock any skill from the Passive Skill Tree by spending a skill point on a skill connected to their starting point or already unlocked skills, Passive Skills that grant attributes, let you select which attribute you want to increase, allowing players to explore the tree in any direction.

### Information for Fire Resistance in Path of Exile 2

* Type: [Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")
* [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"): [Smith of Kitava](/Smith+of+Kitava "Path of Exile 2 Smith of Kitava")
* Requirements: **TBA**

### Path of Exile 2 Fire Resistance Effects

Fire Resistance provides the following effects:

* +8% to Fire Resistance

### Path of Exile 2 Fire Resistance Notes and Tips

* Fire Resistance has been decreased from 15% to 8% in Patch 0.3.0
* Resistances: Resistances reduce damage taken of the corresponding damage type - Fire, Cold, Lightning or Chaos - up to a Maximum. Fire, Cold and Lightning Resistances are Elemental Resistances.  
    
  Player resistances are lowered as you progress through the game, and can be improved with Equipment, Passives & Quest items.
