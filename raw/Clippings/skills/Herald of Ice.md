**Herald of Ice** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Herald of Ice** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Herald of Ice Effects

**Herald of Ice**provides the following effects:

* Attack Damage: 92%
* Explosion Radius is 1.8 Metres
* 100% of Explosion Physical Damage converted to Cold Damage

### How to get Herald of Ice in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Herald of Ice** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 4**.
* To use the Herald of Ice gem, it requires Character Level 10 and above, with 15 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") and 15 [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") or more.
