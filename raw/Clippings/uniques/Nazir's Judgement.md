**Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff** is a **[Unique Quarterstaff](/Unique+Quarterstaves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Quarterstaves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff** is a quarterstaff with enhanced physical damage, accuracy, and stun duration, causing daze buildup based on damage dealt. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The first witch hunter knew one critical tactic:never let your enemy have a clear moment.

### Path of Exile 2 Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff Stats & Modifiers

**Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (58-64) to (126-132)  
Critical Hit Chance: 10%  
Attacks per Second: 1.4  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (30-36) to (75-81) Physical Damage  
+(50–100) to Accuracy Rating  
(10–20)% increased Stun Duration  
Daze on Hit  
(35-50)% increased Melee Damage against Stunned Enemies

### PoE 2 Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff Requirements

To equip **Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff**, players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 28, 41 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity"), 18 [Int](/Int "Path of Exile 2 Int")

### How to get Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.

### Path of Exile 2 Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff Notes and Tips for

* Nazir's Judgement Steelpoint Quarterstaff effect "(80-120)% increased Physical Damage, +(50-100) to Accuracy Rating, (10-20)% increased Stun Duration, Causes Daze buildup equal to 100% of Damage Dealt" replaced with "Adds (30-36) to (75-81) Physical Damage, +(50–100) to Accuracy Rating, (10–20)% increased Stun Duration, Daze on Hit, (30-50)% increased Melee Damage against Stunned Enemies" in Patch 0.3.0.
