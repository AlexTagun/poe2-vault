**Guiding Palm of the Eye** is a **[Unique Scepter](/Unique+Sceptres "Path of Exile 2 Unique Sceptres")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Guiding Palm of the Eye** grants Purity of Ice that allows you to deal Cold Damage to enemies. It can also boost your stats, like Intelligence, and can boost the Cold Damage of your allies. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "With piercing eyes, you saw through the Stillness. Undulating as one, you gloriously covered all in white. But... I can bear you no longer." The Dreamer whispered with fogging breath, ice creeping down his hand.

### Path of Exile 2 Guiding Palm of the Eye Stats & Modifiers

**Guiding Palm of the Eye**has the following Stats:

Spirit: 100

**Guiding Palm of the Eye**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 8 [Purity of Ice](/Purity+of+Ice "Path of Exile 2 Purity of Ice")  
Gain 25% of Damage as Extra Cold Damage  
Allies in your Presence deal (15-23) to (28-35) added Attack Cold Damage  
50% of your Base Life Regeneration is granted to Allies in your Presence  
+(20-30) to Intelligence  
25% increased Light Radius  
Grants effect of Guided Freezing Shrine

### PoE 2 Guiding Palm of the Eye Requirements

To equip **Guiding Palm of the Eye** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 65, 17 STR, 38 INT

### How to get Guiding Palm of the Eye in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Guiding Palm of the Eye** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
