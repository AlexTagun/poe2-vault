# Valako's Roar

**Base:** Topaz Charm
**Class:** Charms

## Base Stats

Lasts 4 Second
Consumes 20 of 40 Charges on use
Currently has 40 Charges

**Requires: Level 5**

## Implicit

- Used when you take Lightning damage from a Hit

## Modifiers

- (30 — 40) % increased Charges gained
- Grants a Frenzy Charge on use
