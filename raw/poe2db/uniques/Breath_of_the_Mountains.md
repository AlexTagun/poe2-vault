# Breath of the Mountains

**Base:** Sapphire Charm
**Class:** Charms

## Base Stats

Lasts 4 Second
Consumes 20 of 40 Charges on use
Currently has 40 Charges

**Requires: Level 5**

## Implicit

- Used when you take Cold damage from a Hit

## Modifiers

- (10 — 15) % reduced Charges per use
- Grants a Power Charge on use

## Flavour

> "To scrape the sky, to touch the clouds themselves, is to know true freedom." - Mutewind saying
