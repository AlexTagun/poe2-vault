**Cast on Melee Kill** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Cast on Melee Kill** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Cast on Melee Kill Effects

**Cast on Melee Kill**provides the following effects:

Gains 300 Energy when you kill an enemy with a Melee Hit  
Triggers all Socketed Spells and loses all Energy on reaching maximum Energy  
Cooldown Time: 0.20 s  
  
Has 10 maximum Energy per 0.1 seconds of base cast time of Socketed Spells  
Socketed Skills deal 50% more Damage
