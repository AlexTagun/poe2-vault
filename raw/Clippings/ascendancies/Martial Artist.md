**Martial Artist** is one of the three unique [Monk](/Monk "Path of Exile 2 Monk") [Ascendancy Classes](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") available in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Martial Artist** was once a student of the craft, but now is a master to 3 Hollow Techniques after decades of training. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Martial Artist Key Features

After decades of intense training, he is now the master of three of the seven Hollow techniques. Perform attack dealing AoE damage to clear enemies in your path.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* Hollow Form Technique: Grants Skill: Hollow Form
* Way of the Mountain: 200% Surpassing chance per enemy Power to gai Mountain's Teachings on Immobilising an enemy, up to a maximum of 30. Lose a Mountain's Teaching when you are Hit, or when you use or Sustain an Attack that benefits from Mountain's Teachings.
* Hollow Focus Technique: Grants Skill: Follow Focus
* Hollow Resonance Technique: Grants Skill: Follow Resonance
* Runic Meridians: Can tattoo Runes onto your body, gaining additional Rune-only sockets:
  + 1 Helmet socket
  + 2 Body Armour sockets
  + 1 Gloves socket
  + 1 Boots socket
* Way of the Stonefist: Gloves you equip have their Base Type transformed to Fists of Stone while equipped, and their Prefixees and Suffixes are transformed into more powerful related Modifiers.

##### **Techniques:**

* Hollow Form Technique: Create illusions of yourself and attack enemies with a skill of your choice.
* Hollow Focus Technique: Manifest your spiritual energy onto bells, which will appear around you and can be shattered by attacking.
* Hollow Resonance Technique: Manifest a bell on your back, it will ring each time you deal a Critical Attack and will damage enemies around you

### Path of Exile 2 Martial Artist Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Martial Artist**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Covering Ground:**The **Martial Artist** has AoE skills that can cover a large area and gems that can significantly benefit the skills in the long run.

### Cons

**-****Vulnerability:** If not managed right, players can be left vulnerable when channeling into the techniques, leaving them open to take damage from surrounding enemies.

### Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) Martial Artist Skill Gems

##### Skill Gems and Customization:

To unlock new skills, [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") must be found and used. Activating a [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") opens the gemcutting menu, which allows players to level up existing skills or engrave new ones. Skill Gems retain their level, so newly engraved skills match the level of the gem, encouraging experimentation without penalty.

Since abilities are powered by Skill Gems, all classes have the freedom to use any gem they choose, offering limitless build possibilities.
