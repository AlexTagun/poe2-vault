## Class System Summary

Path of Exile 2 features **twelve unique classes** with **36 total Ascendancy classes** (3 per base class).

Key quote: "Any class can use any Skill Gem and those Skill Gems can be further modified by up to 5 Support Gems."

Players can also "Dual-Specialise your passive skill tree, allowing your character to switch between two wildly different fighting styles at will."

Passive skill tree contains **over 1,500 skills**.

## The 12 Classes

### Strength-Based

**Warrior** (STR)
- Features: Heavy Slams, Stuns and Knockback, Defenses
- Weapons: Two-handed maces or one-handed weapon + shield
- Ascendancies: Warbringer, Titan
- Description: "Classic tanky melee class" excelling in close combat with "exceptional survivability through blocking mechanics"

**Marauder** (STR) — *Coming soon*

### Dexterity-Based

**Ranger** (DEX)
- Features: Long Range, Agility, Crowd Control
- Weapons: Bows
- Ascendancies: Deadeye, Pathfinder
- Description: "Nimble, bow-wielding class" capable of "rain[ing] down elemental arrows (Fire, Cold, or Lightning) or poison-laden projectiles"

**Huntress** (DEX)
- Features: Hybrid melee-ranged combat, high mobility
- Weapons: Spears (melee and throwable)
- Ascendancies: Amazon, Ritualist
- Description: "Dexterity-based class that blends agility and precision with deadly spear techniques"

**Assassin** (DEX) — *Coming soon*

### Intelligence-Based

**Sorceress** (INT)
- Features: Fire Spells, Cold Spells, Lightning Spells
- Signature: No specific weapon; "true strength lies in her mastery of Elemental Spells"
- Ascendancies: Stormweaver, Chronomancer
- Description: "Quintessential spellcasting mage, harnessing the power of Fire, Cold, and Lightning magic"

**Witch** (INT)
- Features: Minions, Chaos Magic, Osteomancy
- Signature: Summons undead (Skeletons, Spectres); uses Spirit resource to govern active summons
- Ascendancies: Blood Mage, Infernalist
- Description: "Path of Exile 2's version of the Necromancer"; commands "vast armies of the undead"

### Hybrid Classes

**Mercenary** (STR/DEX)
- Features: Ammunition switching, Elemental Rounds, Versatile Range
- Weapons: Crossbows (various types with different ammunition)
- Ascendancies: Witchhunter, Gemling Legionnaire
- Description: "Wields Crossbows instead of Bows, offering a distinct playstyle"; specializes in "elemental grenades, which can freeze enemies or coat them in flammable oil"

**Monk** (INT/DEX)
- Features: Quick Strikes, Mobility, Combos
- Weapons: Quarterstaff, Unarmed combat
- Ascendancies: Invoker, Acolyte of Chayula
- Description: Uses "swift staff strikes and delivering unarmed finishing blows that empower devastating elemental explosions"

**Druid** (STR/INT)
- Features: Shapeshifting (Wolf, Bear, Wyvern), Elemental magic
- Signature: Transform into animals using Animal Talismans; Bear form generates Rage; Wyvern form manipulates lightning and fire
- Ascendancies: Oracle, Shaman

## Attribute System

Three primary attributes distributed across class combinations:
- **Strength (STR)**: Physical power, melee combat, defense
- **Dexterity (DEX)**: Agility, ranged attacks, evasion
- **Intelligence (INT)**: Spellcasting, minion control, elemental magic

Classes positioned at intersections:
- Pure: Warrior (STR), Ranger (DEX), Sorceress (INT)
- Hybrid: Druid (STR/INT), Monk (INT/DEX), Mercenary (STR/DEX)

## Key Features

**Ascendancy System**: Each base class has 3 Ascendancy specializations → "36 Ascendancy Classes to choose from, allowing for even greater customization and strategic variety."

**Skill Flexibility**: "Players can mix and match skills from different classes to craft their perfect build."

**Dual Specialization**: New feature — "allocate passive skill points across two distinct sets of skills" that "activate based on the weapon or ability type you use."
