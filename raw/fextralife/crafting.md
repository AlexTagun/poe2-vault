# Path of Exile 2 Crafting

## Overview

Crafting in Path of Exile 2 is a mechanic that allows players to create powerful gear and items using materials gathered from the environment. The system emphasizes customization through currency-based modifications rather than random rerolling.

A significant departure from the original game is the removal of the Orb of Scouring. Without this item, players cannot revert rare or magical items to their base uncrafted state, making white items substantially more valuable as clean slates.

## Item Rarity System

- **Normal Items**: Basic versions without modifiers
- **Magic Items**: One to two modifiers
- **Rare Items**: Up to six modifiers maximum
- **Unique Items**: Fixed, handcrafted modifiers with special effects

## Sockets and Runes

Items contain sockets for Ezomyte Runes. Body armor and two-handed weapons support two sockets, while other pieces support one socket each. Runes provide specific effects like resistances or elemental damage bonuses.

## Crafting Progression Path

### Transmutation
Transmuting is one of the first crafting processes you will use. Normal items transform to Magic items using an Orb of Transmutation.

### Magic to Rare Conversion
Magic items upgrade to Rare status via Regal Orb, which adds an additional modifier while preserving existing ones.

### Enhancement
Exalted Orbs add random affixes to Rare items, allowing players to reach the six-affix maximum.

## Essential Currency Items

| Currency | Function |
|----------|----------|
| Scroll of Wisdom | Identifies items |
| Orb of Transmutation | Normal → Magic (1 affix) |
| Orb of Augmentation | Adds affix to Magic items |
| Regal Orb | Magic → Rare (+1 affix) |
| Exalted Orb | Adds random affix to Rare items |
| Chaos Orb | Removes and replaces one random modifier |
| Divine Orb | Randomizes numerical values |
| Orb of Annulment | Removes single random modifier |
| Orb of Alchemy | Normal → Rare (4 affixes) |
| Artificer's Orb | Adds sockets to items |
| Vaal Orb | Corrupts items with unpredictable outcomes |
| Orb of Chance | Upgrades Normal items randomly |

## Quality Enhancement System

Different equipment types use specific quality materials:

- **Martial Weapons**: Blacksmith's Whetstone (increases physical damage)
- **Caster Weapons**: Arcanist's Etcher (improves wands, staves, sceptres)
- **Armor**: Armourer's Scrap (increases armor/evasion/energy shield)
- **Accessories**: Flesh Catalyst (increases modifiers; drops from Breach enemies)

Quality provides up to 20% boosts in base properties.

## Advanced Mechanics

### Essences
Essences are making a comeback in Path of Exile 2, but with a revamped functionality. Regular Essences transform Normal items to Magic with guaranteed modifiers, while Greater Essences upgrade Magic items to Rare with specific modifiers.

### Soul Cores
Advanced rune variants offering unique, powerful modifiers unavailable on standard runes. Once placed, they cannot be removed or replaced.

### Vaal Orbs
These corruption tools produce unpredictable outcomes:
- Adding enchantments above implicit modifiers
- Rerolling modifiers into new ones
- Adding extra sockets beyond normal limits
- Potentially rendering items unusable

Corruption permanently prevents further modifications, applying a Vaal Orb should always be the final step.

### Omens
Omens are unique items discovered while completing Rituals. These meta-modifiers influence crafting currency behavior, providing greater control. Example: the Omen of Dextral Annulment ensures the next Orb of Annulment only removes suffix modifiers. Only one omen applies per craft.

## Recent Updates (The Third Edict)

### Tiered Currencies
Normal, Greater, and Perfect variants now exist for major currency items, guaranteeing minimum modifier tiers to prevent low-value affixes.

### Desecrated Modifiers
Desecrated modifiers allow players to improve existing items without the risk of ruining them. Ancient bones from Abyss encounters enable desecration. The Well of Souls in Act 2 reveals three random modifier options for selection.

### Essence Rework
Essences now come in tiers functioning like Regal Orbs for Magic→Rare conversion. Perfect Essences work similarly to Chaos Orbs by removing and replacing modifiers with specific powerful ones.

## Removed Currencies

The following currencies do not return:
- Orb of Alteration
- Orb of Scouring
- Orb of Fusing
- Chromatic Orb

This streamlining simplifies crafting while removing obsolete mechanics due to system overhauls like the Uncut Gems system replacing linking.

## Item Salvaging

Disenchanting Magic items accumulates components toward Orb of Transmutation. Rare disenchantment builds toward Regal Orbs. Quality items provide Armourer's Scrap or Whetstones for enhancing other equipment.

## Economy Integration

Items sell for Gold, which enables merchant purchasing, gambling for random items, passive skill respecs, and player-to-player currency exchange (with small gold fees). Gold itself remains non-tradable.

## Early Game Strategy

Crafting encourages players to upgrade items they find during gameplay rather than starting from scratch. White bases provide clean slates for customization, while Magic bases offer head starts with useful affixes. Rare bases require greater investment but provide maximum initial power.
