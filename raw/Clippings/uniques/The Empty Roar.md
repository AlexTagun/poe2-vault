**The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer,** this greathammer unleashes shockwaves with each blow, drains vitality from enemies, may inflict bleeding, empowers all attacks, and prevents the use of warcries. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Secrecy and silence are powers all their own.

### Path of Exile 2 The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer Stats & Modifiers

**The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (61-71) to (89-99)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.05  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer**features the following Modifiers:

Strikes deal Splash Damage  
Adds (25–35) to (40–50) Physical Damage  
Leeches 10% of Physical Damage as Life  
(10–20)% chance to cause Bleeding on Hit  
All Attacks count as Empowered Attacks  
Cannot use Warcries

### PoE 2 The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer Requirements

To equip **The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer** players must meet the following requirements:

* Requires: Level 22, 41 Str

### How to get The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **The Empty Roar Cultist Greathammer** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
