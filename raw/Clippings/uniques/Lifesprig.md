**Lifesprig Attuned Wand** is a **[Unique Wand](/Unique+Wands "Path of Exile 2 Unique Wands")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Lifesprig Attuned Wand** is a wand that increases cast speed, boosts maximum mana, and leeches life when casting spells. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> From the smallest seeds to the tallest redwoods, Life endures in Wraeclast.

### Path of Exile 2 Lifesprig Attuned Wand Stats & Modifiers

**Lifesprig Attuned Wand**features the following Modifiers:

+(10-20) to maximum Mana  
+(1-3) to Level of all Spell Skill Gems  
(5-10)% increased Cast Speed  
Leeches 1% of maximum Life when you Cast a Spell

### PoE 2 Lifesprig Attuned Wand Requirements

To equip **Lifesprig Attuned Wand** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 2, 7 INT

### How to get Lifesprig Attuned Wand in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Lifesprig Attuned Wand** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
