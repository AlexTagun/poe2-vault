# Foci

Foci are a type of Off-Hand Equipment in Path of Exile 2 designed specifically for spellcasters. They provide bonuses to spell damage, mana regeneration, and spell-related attributes while sacrificing defensive capabilities (no block chance).

- Scale into endgame via Advanced and Expert tiers
