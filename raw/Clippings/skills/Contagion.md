**Contagion** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Contagion** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Contagion Effects

**Contagion**provides the following effects:

* Deals (2–93.9) Base Chaos Damage per second
* Modifiers to Spell Damage apply to Debuff's Damage over Time
* Debuff deals 100% more Damage for eachtime it has spread, up to 300%
* Spread radius is 1.7 metres
* Debuff duration is 5 seconds

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Contagion targets (0–2) additional Enemies when Cast

### How to get Contagion in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Contagion**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 2**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 3.

### Path of Exile 2 Contagion Notes and Tips

* Quality now provides Contagion targets 0-2 additional Enemies when Cast, instead of +0-100% to Maximum Debuff Damage bonus from spreading in Patch 0.3.0
* As of [Patch 0.1.0e](/Patch+Notes#15 "Path of Exile 2 Patch Notes#15"), increased the damage growth per level—results in approximately 33% more damage at Gem Level 25.
