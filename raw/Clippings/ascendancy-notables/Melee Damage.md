**Melee Damage** is a **[Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. This passive skill increases your Melee Damage. Players can unlock any skill from the Passive Skill Tree by spending a skill point on a skill connected to their starting point or already unlocked skills, Passive Skills that grant attributes, let you select which attribute you want to increase, allowing players to explore the tree in any direction.

### Information for Melee Damage in Path of Exile 2

* Type: [Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")
* [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"): [Smith of Kitava](/Smith+of+Kitava "Path of Exile 2 Smith of Kitava")
* Requirements: **TBA**

### Path of Exile 2 Melee Damage Effects

Melee Damage provides the following effects:

* 20% increased Melee Damage

### Path of Exile 2 Melee Damage Notes and Tips

* Melee Damage: Melee Attacks are those that directly hit with a melee Strike or a Slam, dealing Melee damage. Melee attacks usually scale from Weapon or Unarmed damage.  
    
  Any Projectiles these attacks create do not count as Melee damage.
