**Infernal Cry** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Infernal Cry** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Infernal Cry Effects

**Infernal Cry**provides the following effects:

Infernal Cry

* Empowers one Attack per 10 enemy Power inrange, counting up to 50 Power
* Empowered Attacks Gain (30–49)% of damage as extra Fire damage
* Warcry radius is 4 metres
* Destabilises Enemies for 8 seconds

Combust

* Use Time: 1.00 sec
* Critical Hit Chance: 7.00%
* Deals (3–112) to (5–167) Fire Damage
* Deals 10% more Fire Damage per 1 Power of exploding Corpse
* Explosion radius is 1.6 metres

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Empowered Attacks Gain (0–10)% of damage as extra Fire damage

### How to get Infernal Cry in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Infernal Cry**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 3**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 6, with at least 16 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength"), and must be used with a [Two Hand Mace](/Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Two Hand Maces") or [One Hand Mace](/One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 One Hand Maces").

### Path of Exile 2 Infernal Cry Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0  
  + Infernal Cry now empowers one Attack per 10 enemy Power in range, counting up to 50 power (previously one Attack, plus an additional Attack per Endurance Charge consumed).
  + It no longer has 4-38% increased Warcry Speed, while Empowered Attacks now gain 32-49% of Damage as Fire Damage (previously 5% per 5 Power of enemies in range, up to 100%).
  + Infernal Cry's Combust now deals 6-9 to 112-167 Fire Damage at Gem levels 3-20 (previously 11-17 to 120-180), though it now deals significantly more Damage at Gem levels past 20.
  + Infernal Cry now allows you to expend an endurance charge to bypass the cooldown, rather than consuming endurance charges to empower additional attacks.
  + Quality now provides Empowered Attacks gain 0-10% of Damage as Fire Damage (previously gained +0-10% of Damage as Fire Damage per 5 Power of enemies in range, up to +0-50%).
