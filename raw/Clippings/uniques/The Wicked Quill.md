**The Wicked Quill Withered Wand** is **[Unique Wand](/Unique+Wands "Path of Exile 2 Unique Wands")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Wicked Quill Withered Wand,** a twisted wand that greatly amplifies spell power, boosts mana reserves, offers resistance to chaotic forces, and allows spells to occasionally afflict enemies with a weakening curse upon impact. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> With a flick of the finger, their fates are written,  
> the pages torn to a million pieces.

### Path of Exile 2 The Wicked Quill Withered Wand Stats & Modifiers

**The Wicked Quill Withered Wand**features the following Modifiers:

(60–100)% increased Spell Damage  
+(60–100) to maximum Mana  
+(7–13)% to Chaos Resistance  
Spells have a 25% chance to inflict Withered for 4 seconds on Hit

### PoE 2 The Wicked Quill Withered Wand Requirements

To equip **The Wicked Quill Withered Wand** players must meet the following requirements:

* Requires: Level 1, 6 INT

### How to get The Wicked Quill Withered Wand in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **The Wicked Quill Withered Wand** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
