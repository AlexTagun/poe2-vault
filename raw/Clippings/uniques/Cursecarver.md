**Cursecarver Acrid Wand** is a **[Unique Wand](/Unique+Wands "Path of Exile 2 Unique Wands")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Cursecarver Acrid Wand** enhances spellcasting speed and potency, rapidly restores mana, empowers chilling magic, but exacts a health toll each time an enemy is slain. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Lost in the Black Fen, Erian hoped that dawn would save him. He had no idea how far away the light truly was.

### Path of Exile 2 Cursecarver Acrid Wand Stats & Modifiers

**Cursecarver Acrid Wand**features the following Modifiers:

(80–100)% increased Spell Damage  
(10–20)% increased Cast Speed  
Lose 10 Life per Enemy Killed  
(30–50)% increased Mana Regeneration Rate  
+4 to Level of [Random Curse] Skills

### PoE 2 Cursecarver Acrid Wand Requirements

To equip **Cursecarver Acrid Wand** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 33, 60 INT

### How to get Cursecarver Acrid Wand in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Cursecarver Acrid Wand** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
