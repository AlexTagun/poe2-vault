**Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff** is a **[Unique Quarterstaff](/Unique+Quarterstaves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Quarterstaves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff** is a quarterstaff that scales weapon damage, attack speed, and area of effect based on strength, dexterity, and intelligence. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Forged to rule the waves and tide  
> Destined to serve the monkey's paw  
> Strong as a tower of iron  
> Deft as the needle doubt.

### Path of Exile 2 Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff Stats & Modifiers

**Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 9-18  
Critical Hit Chance: 10%  
Attacks per Second: 1.4  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff**features the following Modifiers:

16% increased Melee Strike Range with this weapon  
1% increased Area of Effect for Attacks per 10 Intelligence  
1% increased Attack Speed per 10 Dexterity  
10% increased Weapon Damage per 10 Strength

### PoE 2 Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff Requirements

To equip **Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff**, players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 4, 9 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity")

### How to get Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Pillar of the Caged God Long Quarterstaff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
