**Time of Need** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Time of Need** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Time of Need Effects

**Time of Need**provides the following effects:

* (10.1–12) second duration between Blessings
* Blessing recovers (17–628) Life and removes Curses and Elemental Ailments

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Blessing recovers (0–40) Life and removes Curses and Elemental Ailments

### How to get Time of Need in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Time of Need**can be crafted by engraving it on an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 10**.
* To use the Time of Need gem, it requires Character Level 36 and above, with 46 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") and 46 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Time of Need Notes and Tips

* In Patch 0.3.0, the Attribute requirement ratio is now 75% Strength and 25% Intelligence (previously 100% Strength).
