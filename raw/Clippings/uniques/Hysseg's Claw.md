**Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman** is a **[Unique Talisman](/Unique+Talismans "Path of Exile 2 Unique Talismans")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman** will grant the user the skill Cackling Companions, as well as a huge increase in physical damage. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> In a time of great need, surrounded by Abyssals, the Wayward Druid came to the Sun Clan's aid. It is a debt they have never forgotten.

### Path of Exile 2 Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman Stats & Modifiers

**Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (34-40) to (58-68)  
Critical Hit Chance: 8%  
Attacks per Second: 1.25  
Weapon Range: 1.2

**Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 5 Cackling Companions  
Minions deal (30-50)% increased Damage  
(70-100)% increased Physical Damage  
5% increased Movement Speed  
+(8-15) to Strength  
+(8-15) to Intelligence  
(5-8)% increased Damage per Minion

### PoE 2 Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman Requirements

To equip **Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 16, 21 Str, 16 Int

### How to get Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Hysseg's Claw Familial Talisman** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
