# Traps

Traps are Dexterity and Intelligence-focused weapons designed to ambush and launch surprise attacks on unsuspecting enemies. Come in two varieties: manually activated and proximity-triggered.

## Affixes

### Prefix Mods
- Maximum Mana increases
- Elemental damage increases (Fire, Cold, Lightning)
- Chaos Damage increases
- Spell Physical Damage increases
- Trap Damage increases

### Suffix Mods
- # to Dexterity/Intelligence
- # to Level of all Trap Skill Gems
- Mana Regeneration Rate
- Life/Mana gain per enemy kill
- #% to Critical Hit Chance with Traps
- #% to Critical Damage Bonus with Traps
- Chance to Ignite/Freeze/Shock
