# Unique Jewellery

Unique Jewellery in Path of Exile 2 includes unique rings, amulets, and belts with fixed modifiers designed to enhance attacks, spells, or other specific aspects of a character.

## Notes

- Unique jewellery can be obtained via enemy drops, Orb of Chance on appropriate base, or trading
- Headhunter is among the most sought-after items in the game (steals buffs from killed rares)
- Kalandra's Touch mirrors the mods of the ring in the other slot
- Astramentis provides massive all-attribute bonus — popular for attribute-stacking builds
- Detailed mod values not available from wiki page structure (gallery layout)
