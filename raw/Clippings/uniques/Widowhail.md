**Widowhail Crude Bow** is a **[Unique Bow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Widowhail Crude Bow** is a bow that dramatically enhances bonuses from an equipped quiver for versatile archery builds. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "I loosed a volley of arrows into the heart of the manwho slew my beloved. There was no satisfaction, no healing, no revenge. There was only... emptiness."

**Widowhail Crude Bow**has the following Stats:
