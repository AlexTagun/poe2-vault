# Talismans

Animal Talismans used exclusively by the Druid class, which allows them to perform their Shapeshifting skill. Talismans enable the Druid to transform into Bear, Werewolf, or Wyvern forms, with seamless switching available during combat. Attributes: Strength + Intelligence.
