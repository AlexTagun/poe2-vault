**Atziri's Rule Reflecting Staff** is a [Unique Staff](/Unique+Staves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Staves") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Atziri's Rule Reflecting Staff** grants Mirror of Refraction, an increased level for all corrupted spell skill gems, cast speed, and life gain. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Bow before her... or suffer the most gruelling death imaginable.

### Path of Exile 2 Atziri's Rule Reflecting Staff Stats & Modifiers

**Atziri's Rule Reflecting Staff**features the following Modifiers:

(10-20)% Increased maximum Life  
+(3-5) to Level of all Corrupted Spell Skill Gems  
(10-20)% increased Cast Speed  
+(20-30) to Intelligence  
Spells which cost Life Gain (80-120)% of Damage as Extra Physical Damage

### PoE 2 Atziri's Rule Reflecting Staff Requirements

To equip **Atziri's Rule Reflecting Staff** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 70, 123 Int

### How to get Atziri's Rule Reflecting Staff in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Atziri's Rule Reflecting Staff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
