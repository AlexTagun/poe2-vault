# Forsaken Bangle

**Base:** Amethyst Charm
**Class:** Charms

## Base Stats

Lasts 4 Second
Consumes 30 of 40 Charges on use
Currently has 40 Charges

**Requires: Level 40**

## Implicit

- Used when you take Chaos damage from a Hit

## Modifiers

- (15 — 25) % increased Duration
- 50 % of Chaos damage you prevent when Hit Recouped as Life and Mana during effect

## Flavour

> Among the Templars, a secret few ate the sins of others. They bore this burden to empower their hidden Order.
