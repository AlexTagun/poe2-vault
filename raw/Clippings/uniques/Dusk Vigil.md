**Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff** is a **[Unique Staff](/Unique+Staves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Staves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff** is a staff that enhances spell damage, mana regeneration, and life gain while triggering a powerful firebolt skill. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The candlemass tradition was born in a time of darkness and fear.

### Path of Exile 2 Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff Stats & Modifiers

**Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff**features the following Modifiers:

Gain (30-50)% of Damage as Extra Fire Damage  
(80-120)% increased Spell Damage  
Gain (5-10) Life per enemy killed  
25% increased Mana Regeneration Rate  
Trigger Ember Fusillade Skill on casting a Spell

### PoE 2 Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff Requirements

To equip **Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff** players must meet the following requirements:

* Requires: Level 1, 6 INT

### How to get Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
