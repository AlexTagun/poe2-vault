**Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow** is a **[Unique Bow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow** grants Phantasmal Arrow and inflicts Fire Damage. It can also boost stats like Flammability Magnitude, Increased Ignite Magnitude, and its Attack Hits Inflict Spectral Fire for 8 Seconds. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The power of the Allflame rends souls just as easily as flesh.

### Path of Exile 2 Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow Stats & Modifiers

**Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 39-72  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.15

**Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 11 Phantasmal Arrow  
50% Reduced Projectile Range  
Adds (76-98) to (126-193) Fire Damage  
(30-50)% Increased Flammability Magnitude  
(10-20)% Increased Ignite Magnitude  
30% Reduced Life Recovery Rate  
-30 Physical Damge Taken From Hits  
Attack Hits Inflict Spectral Fire for 8 Seconds

### PoE 2 Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow Requirements

To equip **Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 45, 80 Dex

### How to get Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Fairgraves' Curse Artillery Bow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
