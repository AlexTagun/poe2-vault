# Maces

Maces are heavy weapons that excel in stunning enemies and breaking defenses, blending Strength with a touch of Intelligence. They support builds mixing physical power with elemental or magical enhancements.

## Affixes

### Prefix Mods
- Adds # to # Physical Damage
- Adds # to # Fire/Cold/Lightning Damage
- #% increased Physical Damage
- #% increased Physical Damage, # to Accuracy Rating
- # to Accuracy Rating
- #% increased Elemental Damage with Attacks

### Suffix Mods
- # to Strength
- #% reduced Attribute Requirements
- # to Level of all Melee Skills
- Leeches #% of Physical Damage as Life/Mana
- Gain # Life/Mana per Enemy Killed
- Grants # Life per Enemy Hit
- #% increased Attack Speed
- #% to Critical Hit Chance
- #% to Critical Damage Bonus
- # to Accuracy Rating, #% increased Light Radius
- #% increased Stun Duration
- Causes #% increased Stun Buildup
