**Deadeye** isan **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Ranger](/Ranger "Path of Exile 2 Ranger")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Deadeye** is an expert markswoman, excelling in precision and speed. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

>  A woman can change the world with a single well-placed arrow.

### Path of Exile 2 Deadeye Key Features

An expert in marksman, the Deadeye ascendancy class is an expert marksman capable of firing extra projectiles and has increased movement speed when attacking.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Avidity](/Avidity "Path of Exile 2 Avidity"): 50% chance to gain an additional Frenzy Charge when you gain a Frenzy Charge.
* [Called Shots](/Called+Shots "Path of Exile 2 Called Shots"): Grants Skill: [Called Shots (Meta Skill)](/Called+Shots+(Meta+Skill) "Path of Exile 2 Called Shots (Meta Skill)") which causes your projectiles to chain to marked enemies, and grants the new Called Shots Meta Skill that repeatedly marks enemies near you, bypassing your Mark limit.
* [Bullseye](/Eagle+Eyes "Path of Exile 2 Eagle Eyes"): Applies 10 stacks of Critical Weakness to enemies when you consume a Mark on them.
* [Endless Munitions](/Endless+Munitions "Path of Exile 2 Endless Munitions"): Skills fire an additional Projectile.
* [Gathering Winds](/Gathering+Winds "Path of Exile 2 Gathering Winds"): Gain Tailwind on Skill use; Lose all Tailwind when Hit.
* Mirage Deadeye: Grants Skill: the [Mirage Deadeye (Meta Skill)](/Mirage+Deadeye+(Meta+Skill) "Path of Exile 2 Mirage Deadeye (Meta Skill)").
* [Projectile Proximity Specialisation](/Projectile+Proximity+Specialisation "Path of Exile 2 Projectile Proximity Specialisation")
  + [Far Shot](/Far+Shot "Path of Exile 2 Far Shot"): Projectiles gain Damage as they travel farther, dealing up to 20% more damage with Hits to targets.
  + [Point Blank](/Point+Blank "Path of Exile 2 Point Blank"): Projectile Hits deal up tp 20% more Damage to targets at the start of their movement, losing the damage bonus as the projectile travels farther.
* [Thrilling Chase](/Thrilling+Chase "Path of Exile 2 Thrilling Chase"): Benefits from Consuming Frenzy Charges for your Skills have 50% chance to be doubled

### Path of Exile 2 Deadeye Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Deadeye**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Mobility:**A Deadeye has mobility and speed while dealing damage against enemies, making it easier to fight enemies with telegraphed attacks.

**+ Versatile**: The ascendancy has good versatility due to dealing huge single-target damage and having good AoE skills.

### Cons

**-****Gear Dependent:** Most Deadeye builds are gear dependent, making players encounter a wall if they do not have proper gear.

**- Mana**: The **Deadeye** ascendancy can face mana sustain issues, especially with limited Mana flask charges.
