**Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer** is a two-handed mace with increased physical damage, max mana, and improved critical hit chance. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The faithful may continue to serve, even after death.

### Path of Exile 2 Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer Stats & Modifiers

**Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (116-123) to (180-188)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.1  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer**features the following Modifiers:

+100 Intelligence Requirement  
Adds (58-65) to (102-110) Physical Damage  
+(80-100) to Maximum Mana  
+50 to Spirit  
Increases and Reductions to Minion Damage also affect you

### PoE 2 Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer Requirements

To equip **Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 33, 60 STR, 100 INT

### How to get Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Chober Chaber Leaden Greathammer** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
