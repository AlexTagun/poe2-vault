# Charms

Charms are equippable items that provide automatic defensive bonuses or immunities when specific conditions are met during gameplay.

## Mechanics
- Trigger-based: each charm activates upon meeting specific conditions
- Charges: limited charges (typically 80 max) consumed on activation; regenerate by killing monsters
- Duration: effects last 2-4 seconds depending on charm type
- Rarity: available in normal to rare quality with craftable enhancements
