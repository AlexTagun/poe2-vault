**The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff** is a **[Unique Quarterstaff](/Unique+Quarterstaves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Quarterstaves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff** is a quarterstaff that deals fire damage, grants fire resistance, and increases light radius while enhancing ignite chance. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The night Draven attacked, Erian was asleep at his post.

### Path of Exile 2 The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff Stats & Modifiers

**The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 16-26  
Critical Hit Chance: 12%  
Attacks per Second: 1.4  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff**features the following Modifiers:

No Physical Damage  
Adds (25-32) to (40-50) Fire Damage  
+(30-50) to Accuracy Rating  
+20% to Fire Resistance  
100% increased chance to Ignite  
30% increased Light Radius

### PoE 2 The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff Requirements

To equip **The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 11, 18 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity"), 9 [Int](/Int "Path of Exile 2 Int")

### How to get The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **The Sentry Gothic Quarterstaff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
