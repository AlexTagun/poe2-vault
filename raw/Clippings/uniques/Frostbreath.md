**Frostbreath Slim Mace** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Frostbreath Slim Mace** is a one-handed mace that deals physical and cold damage, contributing to freeze buildup and enabling culling strikes against frozen enemies. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> A merciful murderer swept through the streets of Sarn Robbing breath from the weak and worthless.

### Path of Exile 2 Frostbreath Slim Mace Stats & Modifiers

**Frostbreath Slim Mace**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (19-23)-(33-37)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.55  
Weapon Range: 1.1

**Frostbreath Slim Mace**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (8-12) to (16-20) Physical Damage  
Adds (8-12) to (16-20) Cold Damage  
+5% to Critical Hit Chance  
All Damage from Hits with this Weapon Contributes to Freeze Buildup  
Culling Strike against Frozen Enemies

### PoE 2 Frostbreath Slim Mace Requirements

To equip **Frostbreath Slim Mace** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 10, 21 [Str](/Str "Path of Exile 2 Str")

### How to get Frostbreath Slim Mace in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Frostbreath Slim Mace** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
