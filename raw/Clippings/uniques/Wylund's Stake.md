**Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer** is a one-handed mace that adds physical and fire damage, breaks armor, and applies fire exposure on full armor breaks. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Shaped metal never forgets the forge.

### Path of Exile 2 Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer Stats & Modifiers

**Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (10-12) to (17-19)  
Elemental Damage: 5.5-9  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.45  
Weapon Range: 1.1

**Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer**features the following Modifiers:

50% of this Weapon's base Physical Damage Converted to Fire Damage (Hidden)  
Adds (4-6) to (8-10) Physical Damage  
Adds (4-6) to (8-10) Fire Damage  
+(10-20) to Strength  
+(20-30)% to Fire Resistance  
Hits Break (30-50) Armour  
Inflicts Fire Exposure when this Weapon Fully Breaks Armour

### PoE 2 Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer Requirements

To equip **Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires:  Level 4, 11 [Str](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength")

### How to get Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Wylund's Stake Smithing Hammer** drop was disabled in Patch 0.3.0
