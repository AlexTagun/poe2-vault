**Infernalist** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Witch](/Witch "Path of Exile 2 Witch")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Infernalist** channels the power of hellfire, summoning a fiery companion and unleashing devastating flames. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

> You seek power at any cost... and there is no price too high to pay.

### Path of Exile 2 Infernalist Key Features

**The Infernalist** can summon a hellhound companion that can set enemies ablaze and can take hits for the character. Take advantage of infernal skills to wreak fiery skills and even transform into a devastating demon.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Altered Flesh](/Altered+Flesh "Path of Exile 2 Altered Flesh"): 20% of Cold Damage Taken as Fire Damage. 20% of Lightning Damage Taken as Fire Damage, 20% of Physical Damage Taken as Chaos Damage
* [Beidat's Gaze](/Beidat's+Gaze "Path of Exile 2 Beidat's Gaze"): Reserves 25% of Life. +1 to Maximum Mana per 6 Maximum Life
* [Beidat's Hand](/Beidat's+Hand "Path of Exile 2 Beidat's Hand"): Reserves 25% of Life. +1 to Maximum Energy Shield per 8 Maximum Life
* [Beidat's Will](/Beidat's+Will "Path of Exile 2 Beidat's Will"): Reserves 25% of Life. +1 to Maximum Spirit per 25 Maximum Life
* [Bringer of Flame](/Bringer+of+Flame "Path of Exile 2 Bringer of Flame"): All Damage from you and Allies in your Presence Contributes to Ignite Chance and Magnitude
* [Demonic Possession](/Demonic+Possession "Path of Exile 2 Demonic Possession"): Grants Skill: [Demon Form](/Demon+Form "Path of Exile 2 Demon Form")
* [Grinning Immolation](/Grinning+Immolation "Path of Exile 2 Grinning Immolation"): Become Ignited when you deal a Critical Hit, taking 15% of your Life and Energy Shield as Fire Damage per second. 50% more Critical Damage Bonus
* [Loyal Hellhound](/Loyal+Hellhound "Path of Exile 2 Loyal Hellhound"): Grants Skill: [Summon Infernal Hound](/Summon+Infernal+Hound "Path of Exile 2 Summon Infernal Hound")
* [Mastered Darkness](/Mastered+Darkness "Path of Exile 2 Mastered Darkness"): Demonflame has no maximum
* [Pyromantic Pact](/Pyromantic+Pact "Path of Exile 2 Pyromantic Pact"): Maximum Mana is replaced by Maximum Infernal Flame. Gain Infernal Flame instead of spending Mana for Skill costs. Take maximum Life and Energy Shield as Fire Damage when Infernal Flame reaches maximum. Lose all Infernal Flame on reaching maximum Infernal Flame. 50% of Infernal Flame lost per second if none was gained in the past 2 seconds
* [Seething Body](/Seething+Body "Path of Exile 2 Seething Body"): Gain Elemental Archon when you cast a Spell while on High Infernal Flame, Elemental Archon does not expire while on High Infernal Flame, and lose Elemental Archon on reaching maximum Infernal Flame

### Path of Exile 2 Infernalist Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Infernalist**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Minion Gameplay:**An **Infernalist** has the option to utilize minions in their builds, allowing for a different gameplay compared to other classes and ascendancies.

**+ Spellcasting**: The **Infernalist** ascendancy has the tools to excel as a spellcaster.

### Cons

**-****Builds:** Build options can be limited for an **Infernalist**, as there are few specific playstyles that an **Infernalist** can do.

**- Resource Management**: The **Infernalist** can encounter Life and Mana sustain issues as the player's tools for recovery are mostly from flasks.
