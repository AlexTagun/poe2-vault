**Decompose** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Decompose** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Decompose Effects

**Decompose**provides the following effects:

Poison Cloud

* Poisons enemies as though dealing Chaos damage equal to (30–39.5)% of Corpse's maximum Life
* Gains 50% more Area of Effect per second, up to a maximum of 200%
* Cloud radius is 1.6 metres
* Cloud duration is 4 seconds

Explosion

* Cloud radius is 1.6 metres
* Cloud duration is 4 seconds
* Deals (11–299) to (17–449) Fire Damage

Additional Effects From Quality:

* Cloud duration is (0–1) second

### How to get Decompose in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Decompose** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level (1–20)**.
* To use the **Decompose** gem, it requires Character Level (1–90), (1–205) [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").
