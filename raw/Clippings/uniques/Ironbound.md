**Ironbound Warden Bow** is a **[Unique Bow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Ironbound Warden Bow** is a bow that has an implicit to cause players' attacks to have a chance to chain an additional time. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Each crest was a Thane's word, bolted to Ivor's bow. They would join the Count's side, or die by his hands.

### Path of Exile 2 Ironbound Warden Bow Stats & Modifiers

**Ironbound Warden Bow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 12-20  
Critical Hit Chance: 5.00%  
Attacks Per Second: 1.25

**Ironbound Warden Bow**features the following Modifiers:

23% chance to Chain an additional time  
+119 to Armour  
9% increased Attack Speed  
+12% to Block chance  
5% increased Block chance per 100 total Item Armour on Equipped Armour Items  
Hits with this weapon have 1 to 4 Added Physical Damage per 1% Block Chance  
Arrows Return if they have Pierced a target which had Fully Broken Armour

### PoE 2 Ironbound Warden Bow Requirements

To equip **Ironbound Warden Bow** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 11, 23 DEX

### How to get Ironbound Warden Bow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Ironbound Warden Bow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
