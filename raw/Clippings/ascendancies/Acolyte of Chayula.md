**Acolyte of Chayula** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Monk](/Monk "Path of Exile 2 Monk")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Acolyte of Chayula** embraces the darkness, using it both as a shield and a weapon. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Acolyte of Chayula Key Features

The Acolyte embraces the darkness, using it both as a shield and a weapon.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Chayula's Gift](/Chayula's+Gift "Path of Exile 2 Chayula's Gift"): +10% to Maximum Chaos Resistance. Chaos Resistance is doubled
* [Deepening Shadows](/Deepening+Shadows "Path of Exile 2 Deepening Shadows"): Grants 1% increased Maximum Darkness per 1% Chaos Resistance
* [Embrace the Darkness](/Embrace+the+Darkness "Path of Exile 2 Embrace the Darkness"): Removes all Spirit. Base Maximum Darkness is 100. Damage taken is Reserved from Darkness before being taken from Life or Energy Shield. Darkness Reservation lasts for 5 seconds. +10 to Maximum Darkness per Level
* [Inner Turmoil](/Inner+Turmoil "Path of Exile 2 Inner Turmoil"): Mana Leech recovers based on other damage types as well as Physical Damage
* [Illusory Void](/Illusory+Void "Path of Exile 2 Illusory Void"): Grants Skill: [Void Illusion](/Void+Illusion "Path of Exile 2 Void Illusion")
* [Lucid Dreaming](/Lucid+Dreaming "Path of Exile 2 Lucid Dreaming"): A multi-choice Notable with the following choices:
  + Choice of Life – All Flames of Chayula you manifest are Red
  + Choice of Mana – All Flames of Chayula you manifest are Blue
  + Choice of Power – All Flames of Chayula you manifest are Purple.
* [Sap of Nightmares](/Sap+of+Nightmares "Path of Exile 2 Sap of Nightmares"): Leech recovers based on Chaos Damage as well as Physical Damage.
* [Unravelling](/Unravelling "Path of Exile 2 Unravelling"): Grants Chaos Damage from Hits also Contributes to Ignite Chance and Magnitude, Freeze Buildup, or Shock Chance, changing every 2 seconds
* [Waking Dream](/Waking+Dream "Path of Exile 2 Waking Dream"): Grants Skill: [Into the Breach](/Into+the+Breach "Path of Exile 2 Into the Breach")

### Path of Exile 2 Acolyte of Chayula Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Acolyte of Chayula**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Chaos Resistance: Chaos Resistance** can be hard to come by or add to gear, so **Chayula's Gift** gives more survivability to the player

**+ Chaos Damage**: Some enemies might have a low **Chaos Resistance**, allowing the player to deal more damage with converted Chaos damage.

### Cons

**-****Breach Dependent:** Breaches are not always a guarantee when exploring areas, so buffs when in breach do not always apply.

**- Gear Dependent**: Builds with this ascendancy will most likely be gear dependent, making gameplay difficult if the player has insufficient gear.
