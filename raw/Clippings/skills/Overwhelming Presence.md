**Overwhelming Presence** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Overwhelming Presence** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Overwhelming Presence Effects

**Overwhelming Presence**provides the following effects:

* Enemies in your Presence have (15–24)% reduced
* Elemental Ailment Threshold
* Enemies in your Presence have (15–25)% reduced Stun Threshold

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–15)% increased Aura Magnitudes

### How to get Overwhelming Presence in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Overwhelming Presence**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 8**.
* To use the Overwhelming Presence gem, it requires Character Level 26 and above, with 61 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Overwhelming Presence Notes and Tips

* Overwhelming Presence is now described as Aura Skills, and has the Aura tag in Patch 0.3.0
