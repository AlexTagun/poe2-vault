**Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear** is a [Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear,** a cruel spear infused with flame and steel, capable of inflicting bleeding and igniting synergy, with a high chance to aggravate bleeding and cause intense suffering on impact. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Born in a star of man's own make,  
> fused to her hand by her last mistake.

### Path of Exile 2 Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (34-46) to (65-70)  
Critical Hit Chance: 6.5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.6  
Weapon Range: 1.5

**Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Spear Throw  
Bleeding you inflict deals Damage (10-20)% faster  
Adds (14–26) to (27–32) Physical Damage  
Adds (33–41) to (47–53) Fire Damage  
(15–25)% chance to cause Bleeding on Hit  
Aggravating any Bleeding with this Weapon also Aggravates all Ignites on the target  
(25–40)% chance to Aggravate Bleeding on Hit

### PoE 2 Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear Requirements

To equip **Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 33
* Stats: 20 Str, 47 Dex

### How to get Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Saitha's Spear Barbed Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
