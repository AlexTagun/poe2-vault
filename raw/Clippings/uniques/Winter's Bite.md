**Winter's Bite Glass Shank** is a **[Unique Dagger](/Unique+Daggers "Path of Exile 2 Unique Daggers")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Winter's Bite Glass Shank** is a dagger that excels in cold damage, critical hits, and freezing enemies on full life. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Nothing stabs colder than pure hatred.

### Path of Exile 2 Winter's Bite Glass Shank Stats & Modifiers

**Winter's Bite Glass Shank**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 3-10  
Critical Hit Chance: 15%  
Attacks per Second: 1.55  
Weapon Range: 1

**Winter's Bite Glass Shank**features the following Modifiers:

No Physical Damage  
Adds (8-10) to (15-18) Cold Damage  
Freezes Enemies that are on Full Life
