**Barrier Invocation** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Barrier Invocation** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Barrier Invocation Effects

**Barrier Invocation**provides the following effects:

* 14% Increased Energy generated for Supported Skills
* When Hit, gain 1 Energy for each 5 Energy Shield lost to Damage Taken
* Maximum Energy is 500

Additional Effects From Quality:

* 0(0-15)% Increased Energy Generated for Supported Skills
* Socketed Spells consume 10 Energy per 0.11 seconds of Base Cast Time when Triggered

### How to get Barrier Invocation in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Barrier Invocation** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level ??**.
* To use the Barrier Invocation gem, it requires Character Level 26 and above, with 61 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") or more.
