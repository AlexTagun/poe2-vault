**The Dark Defiler Rattling Sceptre** is a **[Unique Sceptre](/Unique+Sceptres "Path of Exile 2 Unique Sceptres")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Dark Defiler Rattling Sceptre** is a sceptre that boosts mana, intelligence, and chaos damage per active undead minion. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Rare is the Necromancer who leadshis undead armies from the front.

### Path of Exile 2 The Dark Defiler Rattling Sceptre Stats & Modifiers

**The Dark Defiler Rattling Sceptre**has the following Stats:

Spirit: 100

**The Dark Defiler Rattling Sceptre**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: [Skeletal Warrior](/Skeletal+Warrior "Path of Exile 2 Skeletal Warrior")  
+(20-30) to maximum Mana  
+(5-10) to Intelligence  
(10-30)% increased Mana Regeneration Rate  
Gain 5% of Damage as Chaos Damage per Undead Minion
