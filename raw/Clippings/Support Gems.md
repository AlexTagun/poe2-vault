**Support Gems** are a type of **[Gem](/Gems "Path of Exile 2 Gems")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. Support Gems are designed to modify and enhance the effects of the Skill Gems they are linked to. Unlike [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), **Support Gems** do not have active abilities of their own; instead, they adjust the properties of a [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") in various ways. These modifications allow players to tailor their skills to their preferred playstyle. **Support Gems** can also add additional effects such as increased damage, increased range, or other situational bonuses.

## Support Gems Guide in Path of Exile 2

### Support Gem Overhaul in The Third Edict Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

In **Patch 0.3.0**, titled **The Third Edict**, **Support Gems** have undergone a major rework aimed at expanding player agency and allowing for creativity in their character builds. Previously, players could equip a specific **Support Gem** to only one skill per character, limiting build diversity and experimentation. This restriction has been removed, allowing players to acquire and obtain and equip the same **Support Gem** to several skills simultaneously. As a result, players can now explore complex interactions between [skills](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills") and **Support Gems**, leading to build diversity and unconventional builds.

This update represents a shift to the **Support Gem**system in relation to [skills](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills"). Instead of being static modifiers to skills, **Support Gems** now have a tier-based system that determines the value range of the stats, with a higher-tiered **Support Gem** having higher values. Alongside the higher stats, some higher-tiered **Support Gems**will have secondary effects that can create a unique interaction with a skill. This new system encourages players to min-max their builds as they go through endgame content, giving players a reason to experiment on their builds.

In **The Third Edict**, many existing **Support Gems** have also been rebalanced or merged into a single gem, combining redundant effects into one. **The Third Edict** update of **PoE2** not only removes the long-standing limitations for build diversity, but also introduces layers of customization for player builds.

### Lineage Supports in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Lineage Support Gems** are a new addition introduced in **The Third Edict** update, representing a powerful evolution of the traditional **Support Gem** system. These specialized gems act as enhanced variants of existing **Support Gems**, providing players with new ways to modify and experiment with their [skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills") setup. Each **Lineage Support Gem** offers an additional effect that significantly alters or augments the skill interaction, introducing possibilities and customization depth. However, these **Lineage Support Gems** have a downside that will also alter how players should approach their chosen skill.

The introduction of the **Lineage Support Gems** offers a deeper potential for customization across all skills, encouraging players to experiment and min-max their build. Some **Lineage Support Gems** in **PoE2** can grant additional damage output in exchange for reduced AoE, attack speed, or increased resource consumption, creating a dynamic interaction for unconventional skill setups that can become viable through customization.
