# Nascent Hope

**Base:** Thawing Charm
**Class:** Charms

## Base Stats

Lasts 3 Second
Consumes 40 of 40 Charges on use
Currently has 40 Charges

**Requires: Level 12**

## Implicit

- Used when you become Frozen

## Modifiers

- (20 — 25) % Chance to gain a Charge when you kill an enemy
- Energy Shield Recharge starts on use
