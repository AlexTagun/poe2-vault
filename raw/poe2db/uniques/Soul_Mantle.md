# Soul Mantle

**Base:** Sacrificial Mantle
**Class:** Body Armours

## Base Stats

Armour : (221-270)
Energy Shield : (82-101)
Base Movement Speed: -0.04

**Requires: Level 36 , 33 Str , 33 Int**

## Modifiers

- (80 — 120) % increased Armour and Energy Shield
- +10 to Strength
- +15 to Intelligence
- (20 — 30) % reduced Totem Life
- +1 to maximum number of Summoned Totems
- Inflicts a random Curse on you when your Totems die, ignoring Curse limit

## Flavour

> The greatest mistakes cause suffering long after they have been made
