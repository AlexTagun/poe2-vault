# Queen of the Forest

**Base:** Smuggler Coat
**Class:** Body Armours

## Base Stats

Evasion Rating : (948-1084)
Base Movement Speed: -0.03

**Requires: Level 51 , 82 Dex**

## Modifiers

- (250 — 300) % increased Evasion Rating
- (-15 — -10) % to Fire Resistance
- +(25 — 30) % to Cold Resistance
- +(10 — 15) % to Lightning Resistance
- Increases Movement Speed by 25%, plus 1% per 600 Evasion Rating, up to a maximum of 75 % Other Modifiers to Movement Speed except for Sprinting do not apply

## Flavour

> Shedding away her regal past, she forged a new destiny. Sacrificing the ephemeral joys of man, she embraced the eternal grasp of nature. Seizing her one true wish, she found peace at last.
