**Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow** is a **[Unique Crossbow](/Unique+Crossbows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Crossbows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow** is a crossbow that increases physical damage and attack speed while conserving bolts if recently reloaded. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "His approach to the gate was met with sounding trumpetsand an unfurling of banners. He never saw it coming."- anonymous Brotherhood of Silence report

### Path of Exile 2 Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow Stats & Modifiers

**Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (11-13) to (21-24)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (2.08-2.24)  
Reload Time: 0.85

**Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow**features the following Modifiers:

(20-30)% increased Bolt Speed  
(40-60)% increased Physical Damage  
(30-40)% increased Attack Speed  
30% reduced Reload Speed  
Bolts fired by Crossbow Attacks have 100% chance to not  
expend Ammunition if you've Reloaded Recently

### PoE 2 Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow Requirements

To equip **Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 4, 8 [Str](/Str "Path of Exile 2 Str"), 8 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity")

### How to get Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
