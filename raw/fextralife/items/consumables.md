# Consumables

Consumables in Path of Exile 2 are items that have one or multiple uses, that when used apply a certain effect and disappear after depletion.

## Life Flasks

| Flask | Level | Recovery | Duration |
|-------|-------|----------|----------|
| Lesser | — | 50 Life | 3 sec |
| Medium | 4 | 90 Life | 5 sec |
| Greater | 10 | 150 Life | 4 sec |
| Grand | 16 | 260 Life | 5 sec |
| Giant | 23 | 340 Life | 4 sec |
| Colossal | 30 | 450 Life | 4 sec |
| Gargantuan | 40 | 710 Life | 5 sec |
| Transcendent | 50 | 840 Life | 4 sec |
| Ultimate | 60 | 920 Life | 3 sec |

Unique: Olroth's Resolve (Ultimate, Lvl 60) – provides instant recovery with increased charges per use and converts excess healing to guard.
