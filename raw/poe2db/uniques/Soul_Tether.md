# Soul Tether

**Base:** Long Belt
**Class:** Belts

**Requires: Level 20**

## Implicit

- Has (1 — 3) Charm Slot
- (15 — 20) % increased Charm Effect Duration

## Modifiers

- +(40 — 60) to maximum Energy Shield
- +(20 — 30) to Intelligence
- You lose 5 % of maximum Energy Shield per second
- Excess Life Recovery from Leech is applied to Energy Shield
