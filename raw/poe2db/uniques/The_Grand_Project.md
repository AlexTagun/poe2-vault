# The Grand Project

**Base:** Precursor Tablet
**Class:** Tablet

**Requires: Level 65**

## Implicit

- Adds Irradiated to a Map 1 use remaining

## Modifiers

- Can only be applied to Precursor Tower Maps Completing the Tower makes all nearby Maps accessible

## Flavour

> "I see a pattern in these towers. Whoever the Precursors were, they sought to master this world, to shape it as they saw fit. My people had the same ambition..."
