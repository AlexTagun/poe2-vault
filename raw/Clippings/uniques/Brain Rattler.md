**Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub** is a two-handed mace with improved physical damage, lightning damage, attack speed and has the Electrocution Buildup effect. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The mind may have no limits, but the skill sure does.

### Path of Exile 2 Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub Stats & Modifiers

**Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (50-54) to (72-76)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.21-1.27)  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (18-22) to (24-28) Physical Damage  
Adds 1 to (50-55) Lightning Damage  
(10-15)% Increased Attack Speed  
All damage with this weapon causes Electrocution Buildup

### PoE 2 Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub Requirements

To equip **Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 16, 31 STR

### How to get Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Brain Rattler Studded Greatclub** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
