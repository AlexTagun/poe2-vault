**Premeditation** is a **[Support Gem](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. Support Gems can be placed into the Support Gem Socket, and used to further tune the effects of Skill Gems.

### Information for Premeditation in Path of Exile 2

* Type: Support
* Tier: 1
* Tags: -
* Support Requirements: +5 Str

### Path of Exile 2 Premeditation Effects

**Premeditation**provides the following effects:

Skills Empowered by Supported Skills deal 15% more Damage

### Premeditation Supported Skill Gems in PoE 2

**Premeditation** can be used to support the following [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"):

**Tier 0**

* [Unleash](/Unleash "Path of Exile 2 Unleash")

**Tier 3**

* [Infernal Cry](/Infernal+Cry "Path of Exile 2 Infernal Cry")

**Tier 5**

* [Barrage](/Barrage "Path of Exile 2 Barrage")

**Tier 9**

* [Mana Tempest](/Mana+Tempest "Path of Exile 2 Mana Tempest")
* [Mantra of Destruction](/Mantra+of+Destruction "Path of Exile 2 Mantra of Destruction")

**Tier 11**

* [Emergency Reload](/Emergency+Reload "Path of Exile 2 Emergency Reload")

### How to get Premeditation in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Premeditation** can be crafted by engraving the support skill to the associated level of an [Uncut Support Skill Gem](/Uncut+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Uncut Skill Gems").
