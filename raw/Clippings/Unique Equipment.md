**Unique Items** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")** have fixed modifiers and special effects that distinguish it from other item types like Magic or Rare gear. Unlike Magic or Rare items, which have randomized affixes, **PoE 2 Unique Item** come with predefined stats and modifiers.

### The Dawn of the Hunt NEW Unique Equipment

There are plenty of unique items waiting to be discovered, with even more being added to enhance exploration and variety.

The goal is to ensure that every base item type has at least one unique counterpart, allowing any base in the game to be chanced. With the introduction of the Huntress, a full range of Unique Spears is available from early levels to endgame. One such example is [Saitha’s Spear](/Saitha’s+Spear "Path of Exile 2 Saitha’s Spear"), which blends Bleed and Fire mechanics, enabling Ignites to be Aggravated for significantly increased damage.

Additionally, a new selection of unique bucklers introduces intriguing Parry mechanics. [Calgyra’s Arc](/Calgyra’s+Arc "Path of Exile 2 Calgyra’s Arc"), for instance, grants the ability to Parry incoming Projectiles regardless of distance.

For the first time, Unique Charms are being introduced. [Beira’s Anguish](/Beira’s+Anguish "Path of Exile 2 Beira’s Anguish") not only provides immunity to Ignite but also triggers a powerful fire nova whenever Ignition occurs.

With so many new uniques to uncover, the possibilities continue to expand.

### PoE 2 Unique Items

Unique Equipment differ from other rarities by having fixed, handcrafted modifiers instead of random affixes. They are rarer and offer unique effects that can significantly alter a character's playstyle. While not necessarily superior to Rare Items, they provide niche, powerful abilities that cater to specific builds.

### Weapon Sockets in Path of Exile 2

Sockets play a crucial role in item customization in Path of Exile 2. Both **Weapons** and [Armor](/Armor "Path of Exile 2 Armor") can feature sockets, which allow players to enhance their gear by inserting [Ezomyte Runes](/Ezomyte+Runes "Path of Exile 2 Ezomyte Runes"). These runes add powerful modifiers to the item, granting unique enhancements to tailor your build.

The number of sockets available varies by item type:

* Body armor and two-handed weapons can have up to two sockets.
* One-handed weapons, along with other armor pieces like helmets, boots, and gloves, can have a maximum of one socket.

##### Salvaging for Socket Crafting

If you come across socketed items that don’t fit your needs, you can take them to the salvage bench in town. Salvaging these items helps you progress toward earning an [Artificer's Orb](/Artificer's+Orb "Path of Exile 2 Artificer's Orb"), a valuable crafting currency that allows you to add sockets to items of your choice.

## Path of Exile 2 Unique Items

### How to Obtain Unique Weapons in PoE 2

In **Path of Exile 2**, most **Unique Weapons** drop randomly from enemies, while some of the rarer chase items can only be obtained from Pinnacle Bosses. Players can also use an [![orb of chance items path of exile 2 wiki guide 100px](/file/Path-of-Exile-2/orb_of_chance_items_path_of_exile_2_wiki_guide_100px.png "Orb of Chance PoE 2")Orb of Chance](/Orb+of+Chance "Path of Exile 2 Orb of Chance") on a normal item for a small chance to either upgrade it into a **Unique** item or destroy it permanently. An Orb of Chance can only transform an item into a **Unique** of the same base type. For example, using it on a [Pronged Spear](/Pronged+Spear "Path of Exile 2 Pronged Spear") gives players a chance to obtain [Atziri's Contempt](/Atziri's+Contempt+Pronged+Spear "Path of Exile 2 Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear").

For a guaranteed way to obtain a specific **Unique Weapon**, players can trade with other players through the **Official PoE 2 Trade Site**. Most **Unique** items are relatively affordable and are commonly traded using currencies such as [![chaos orb items path of exile 2 wiki guide 100px](/file/Path-of-Exile-2/chaos_orb_items_path_of_exile_2_wiki_guide_100px.png "Chaos Orb PoE 2")Chaos Orbs](/Chaos+Orb "Path of Exile 2 Chaos Orb") or [![exalted orb items path of exile 2 wiki guide 100px](/file/Path-of-Exile-2/exalted_orb_items_path_of_exile_2_wiki_guide_100px.png "Exalted Orb PoE 2")Exalted Orbs](/Exalted+Orb "Path of Exile 2 Exalted Orb"), while the rarer and most valuable Uniques can cost several [![divine orb items path of exile 2 wiki guide 100px](/file/Path-of-Exile-2/divine_orb_items_path_of_exile_2_wiki_guide_100px.png "Divine Orb PoE 2")Divine Orbs](/Divine+Orb "Path of Exile 2 Divine Orb").

**Special Ability:**25% increased rarity of items found

## Recommended Unique Equipment in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

In **Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)**, Unique equipment and armor can be used when starting out with your builds during the Acts. Some Unique equipment can be bought at a relatively low price for the value that they bring when leveling your character. Listed below are Unique equipment with information regarding their general usage, as these Unique equipment can be helpful if you are having a hard time while having the spare currency to buy them from other players.

### Recommended Unique Bows and Quivers

### [Widowhail widowhail crude bow weapon path of exile 2 wiki guide 200px min](/Widowhail+Crude+Bow "Path of Exile 2 Widowhail Crude Bow")

**Unique Effect:** (150-250)% increased bonuses gained from Equipped Quiver

### POE 2 Widowhail Guide

The [Widowhail](/Widowhail+Crude+Bow "Path of Exile 2 Widowhail Crude Bow") is a strong bow that can be generally used as it uniquely interacts with [Quivers](/Quivers "Path of Exile 2 Quivers"). From its single affix, it increases the effect of the equipped Quiver by 150% to 250%, which can be used conjunctively with jewels that have a "**Notable Passive Skills in Radius also grant #% increased bonuses gained from equipped Quiver**" and the **Master Fletching** notable passive to increase the bonus up to an additional 80%, making Quivers that can be bought for a low price in the market a deadly addition to your character's build.

Some [Quiver](/Quivers "Path of Exile 2 Quivers") affixes can become very powerful if used with the Widowhail, such as "**#% Increased Critical Hit Chance for Attacks**", "**+# to Level of All Projectile Skills**", and "**#% Increased Projectile Speed**", skills such [Poisonous Concoction](/Poisonous+Concoction "Path of Exile 2 Poisonous Concoction"), [Spark](/Spark "Path of Exile 2 Spark"), and [Bonestorm](/Bonestorm "Path of Exile 2 Bonestorm") can be made stronger.

**Recommended Skills**: [Poisonous Concoction](/Poisonous+Concoction "Path of Exile 2 Poisonous Concoction"), [Spark](/Spark "Path of Exile 2 Spark"), [Bonestorm](/Bonestorm "Path of Exile 2 Bonestorm")

### POE 2 Asphyxia's Wrath Guide

[Asphyxia's Wrath](/Asphyxia's+Wrath+Broadhead+Quiver "Path of Exile 2 Asphyxia's Wrath Broadhead Quiver") is a [Quiver](/Quivers "Path of Exile 2 Quivers") that excels at boosting the player's cold damage and helps in applying elemental ailments to enemies. This quiver can be helpful especially if your build has passive nodes that have increased damage against enemies with elemental ailments, as enemies will be **Frozen** and **Chilled** will take more damage. Bow Skills such as [Ice Shot](/Ice+Shot "Path of Exile 2 Ice Shot"), [Escape Shot](/Escape+Shot "Path of Exile 2 Escape Shot"), and [Freezing Salvo](/Freezing+Salvo "Path of Exile 2 Freezing Salvo") can work well with this quiver. Also, [Lightning Arrow](/Lightning+Arrow "Path of Exile 2 Lightning Arrow") can be used with this quiver if you have Lightning to Cold damage conversion, which the [Call of the Brotherhood](/Call+of+the+Brotherhood "Path of Exile 2 Call of the Brotherhood") has if you have the currency for it.

You can use the [Grip of Winter](/Grip+of+Winter+Firm+Bracers "Path of Exile 2 Grip of Winter Firm Bracers") gloves with this quiver to maximize Cold damage and Freeze buildup in your skills, and this unique can be bought for a low price in the trade market.

**Recommended Skills**: [Lightning Arrow](/Lightning+Arrow "Path of Exile 2 Lightning Arrow") (for Cold damage conversion), [Ice Shot](/Ice+Shot "Path of Exile 2 Ice Shot"), [Escape Shot](/Escape+Shot "Path of Exile 2 Escape Shot"), [Freezing Salvo](/Freezing+Salvo "Path of Exile 2 Freezing Salvo")

### POE 2 Blackgleam Guide

Similar to Asphyxia's Wrath, [Blackgleam](/Blackgleam+Fire+Quiver "Path of Exile 2 Blackgleam Fire Quiver") is a [Quiver](/Quivers "Path of Exile 2 Quivers") that increases Fire damage instead of Cold, while also increasing the chance for you to apply **Ignite** to enemies. This can be used as an alternative to Asphyxia's Wrath if its combination with your build does not work well, as it makes your projectiles pierce enemies that are inflicted with the **Ignite**elemental ailment. Even though there are no Fire Bow Skills yet in the game, the Blackgleam can be generally used by the player for the **Ignite** chance and its ability to pierce **Ignited** enemies. Blackgleam can be traded for a low price in the trade market, so it can be a good idea to invest a little currency in obtaining this item if you're hitting a wall. If you have allocated passive nodes for increased damage against enemies with elemental ailments inflicted on them, Blackgleam can be a good item to purchase if you do not have a lot of currency for equipment upgrades.

**Recommended Skills**: Any skill that can be used with a [Bow](/Bows "Path of Exile 2 Bows")

### POE 2 Lifesprig Guide

Lifesprig has been a great Unique wand for players who are in the early game as its modifiers are very helpful for builds that are just starting out. Lifesprig increases Spell Skill Gems by 1-3 levels, which can boost the damage of your skills. It also increases your Cast Speed, which makes it faster to deal damage to enemies and bosses, with those extra few seconds saved being crucial to surviving any boss or enemy encounter. It also increases your character's survivability as it offers a 1% maximum life leech whenever you cast a spell, which is beneficial for builds that use a Wand, especially with the [Witch](/Witch "Path of Exile 2 Witch") being a fragile class. This can be a good equipment to use early on in the game, as its modifiers can be valuable to builds that can use this in the early game. Do take note, however, of the level requirement of Lifesprig as it can be different as some variants can drop with a high level of [Mana Drain](/Mana+Drain "Path of Exile 2 Mana Drain"), making its level requirement higher.

**Recommended Skills**: Any Skill that can be used with a [Wand](/Wands "Path of Exile 2 Wands")

### POE 2 Earthbound Guide

[Earthbound](/Earthbound+Voltaic+Staff "Path of Exile 2 Earthbound Voltaic Staff") is a unique [Staff](/Staves "Path of Exile 2 Staves") that gives increased Lightning Damage and Cast Speed, which is a great boost to skills that deal Lightning Damage such as Spark. It also has an affix that causes Spark to be triggered every time a Shocked enemy is killed, making it a great [Staff](/Staves "Path of Exile 2 Staves") for clearing mobs of enemies that are grouped up together. Using Lightning spells along with this Staff will help you activate your build and clear out enemies if you're struggling with Crowd Control. Earthbound also helps your build slightly if you have mana sustain issues as it increases your mana regeneration rate by 30%. However, if you are having trouble with bosses or rare mobs, it can be good to have a weapon to swap it for, as this is only useful for clearing packs of monsters.

**Recommended Skills**: [Spark](/Spark "Path of Exile 2 Spark"), [Lightning Bolt](/Lightning+Bolt "Path of Exile 2 Lightning Bolt"), or any [Spell](/Spell+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Spell Gems") that deals Lightning Damage

### POE 2 Dusk Vigil Guide

[Dusk Vigil](/Dusk+Vigil+Ashen+Staff "Path of Exile 2 Dusk Vigil Ashen Staff") is an excellent Unique [Staff](/Staves "Path of Exile 2 Staves") to have if your build mainly uses spells that deal Fire Damage, as it grants both [Firebolt](/Firebolt "Path of Exile 2 Firebolt") and [Ember Fusillade](/Ember+Fusillade "Path of Exile 2 Ember Fusillade"), with the latter being triggered when casting a spell, making it scale with builds that prioritize and allocate Fire or Elemental damage nodes in the Passive Skill Tree. Dusk Vigil is similar to Earthbound, although Dusk Vigil is better at single-target damage because of Ember Fusillade. It will give a boost to your single-target damage, as Ember Fusillade unleashes a barrage of damage to a single enemy if no nearby mobs are around, making it a good staff to alternate with [Earthbound](/Earthbound+Voltaic+Staff "Path of Exile 2 Earthbound Voltaic Staff") for faster endgame zone clearing.

**Recommended Skills**: Any skill that deals Elemental damage, good for builds that allocate increased Fire or Elemental damage

### POE 2 Mist Whisper Guide

[Mist Whisper](/Mist+Whisper+Makeshift+Crossbow "Path of Exile 2 Mist Whisper Makeshift Crossbow") will give a huge boost to Mercenaries early on in the game, as its affixes are very powerful for skills that can be used early on such as [Fragmentation Rounds](/Fragmentation+Rounds "Path of Exile 2 Fragmentation Rounds") and [Permafrost Bolts](/Permafrost+Bolts "Path of Exile 2 Permafrost Bolts"). When using the Mist Whisper with Permafrost Bolts, the skill will chain to enemies, making groups of enemies easier to deal with as they will be frozen — leading to a satisfying explosion when Fragmentation Rounds is used. Mist Whisper can be bought for a small amount of currency in the trade market, making it a good Unique [Crossbow](/Crossbows "Path of Exile 2 Crossbows") to use when you are having trouble fighting groups of enemies. You can even purchase a corrupted Mist Whisper with an added cold damage implicit for a low price for the crossbow to have more effect for your build.

In addition to using the Mist Whisper is the [Grip of Winter](/Grip+of+Winter+Firm+Bracers "Path of Exile 2 Grip of Winter Firm Bracers") gloves, similar to the [Asphyxia's Wrath](/Asphyxia's+Wrath+Broadhead+Quiver "Path of Exile 2 Asphyxia's Wrath Broadhead Quiver") quiver, as it synergizes well with builds that utilize Cold damage and Freeze buildup.

**Recommended Skills**: [Permafrost Bolts](/Permafrost+Bolts "Path of Exile 2 Permafrost Bolts"), [Fragmentation Rounds](/Fragmentation+Rounds "Path of Exile 2 Fragmentation Rounds"), [Glacial Bolt](/Glacial+Bolt "Path of Exile 2 Glacial Bolt"), [Hailstorm Rounds](/Hailstorm+Rounds "Path of Exile 2 Hailstorm Rounds"), [Ice Shards](/Ice+Shards "Path of Exile 2 Ice Shards")

### POE 2 Rampart Raptor Guide

Rampart Raptor is a Unique [Crossbow](/Crossbows "Path of Exile 2 Crossbows") that gives you unlimited bolts for 4 seconds after reloading at the cost of 30% reduced reload speed. This is a great Crossbow for general usage, as you can use Crossbow skills that expend tons of bolts into a barrage for the whole 4 seconds that you have unlimited bolts, making it a great choice for skills such as [Galvanic Shards](/Galvanic+Shards "Path of Exile 2 Galvanic Shards"). [Rampart Raptor](/Rampart+Raptor+Tense+Crossbow "Path of Exile 2 Rampart Raptor Tense Crossbow") is a flexible weapon to use as all Crossbow skills will benefit from it, with skills dealing Physical Damage scaling from it. You will also receive a nice boost of 30-40% increased Attack Speed, making those 4 seconds of unlimited bolts more efficient.

**Recommended Skills**: [Galvanic Shards](/Galvanic+Shards "Path of Exile 2 Galvanic Shards"), any [Crossbow](/Crossbows "Path of Exile 2 Crossbows") skill

### POE 2 Frostbreath Guide

[Frostbreath](/Frostbreath+Slim+Mace "Path of Exile 2 Frostbreath Slim Mace") is a Unique one-handed [Mace](/Maces "Path of Exile 2 Maces") with an affix that helps build up **Freeze** on enemies, which can be a good defensive layer if your build uses melee skills, as you will be fighting enemies up close. Also, Frostbreath grants Culling Strike against **Frozen** enemies, which shatters Frozen enemies with less than 10% health, increasing your DPS by at least 10%. With the first affix, **Culling Strike** will almost be guaranteed as enemies will most likely be frozen due to the accelerated Freeze buildup. As mentioned earlier, freezing enemies will be a good defensive layer as **Frozen** enemies cannot hit you, making this a great [Mace](/Maces "Path of Exile 2 Maces") while starting out with your melee build.

This makes it so that your melee build will be less likely to be overwhelmed by enemies, as you the enemies early on will be killed pretty quickly by your Slam skills. However, this Unique item will not be as effective at around Act 2 or 3, so this can be switched for something more effective.

### POE 2 Bushwhack Guide

[Bushwhack](/Bushwhack+Lizardscale+Boots "Path of Exile 2 Bushwhack Lizardscale Boots"), even if it has a requirement of level 33 and 57 DEX, is still a recommended Unique Armor as it offers the player 15-25% increased Movement Speed — an affix that shows up randomly on boots that are **Item Level** 50. It also has a modifier, "**Physical Damage is Pinning**", which makes enemies unable to move for 4 seconds. This is a powerful modifier for builds that deal Physical Damage, as it is a beneficial effect that allows you to control mobs of enemies more easily while also maneuvering around them and their attacks safely. For its high DEX requirement, you can temporarily wear an amulet that gives high DEX, then remove it once Bushwhack is equipped, as it also increases your [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") by 10-20 points. In **PoE 2**, having increased movement speed will make the campaign much easier as you will be walking around large zones for most of it, making Bushwhack a great Unique to equip.

### POE 2 Luminous Pace Guide

[Luminous Pace](/Luminous+Pace+Straw+Sandals "Path of Exile 2 Luminous Pace Straw Sandals") is recommended not only for its 10% movement speed modifier, but also for its Energy Shield modifiers. When equipped, Luminous Pace has an effect that makes the Energy Shield Recharge start up faster in exchange for having a slower recharge rate — making it a good source of sustain in the early game for your character. Also, the 10% movement speed is a nice boost for completing the campaign, as you can obtain Luminous Pace for a cheap price in the trade market.

The 10% increased **Movement Speed** is also great, as you can stack its effect with the numerous cheap Unique Boots mentioned above, making yourself move faster across the vast zones of **Path of Exile 2**.

### POE 2 Greymake Guide

Early on in the game, [Greymake](/Greymake+Brimmed+Helm "Path of Exile 2 Greymake Brimmed Helm") solves your build's attribute woes as it increases all of your attributes ([Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength"), [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity"), [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence")) by 30-50 per attribute. It can be used from level 5, so it can be used pretty early in the game. The additional STR and INT also gives a nice boost to your character's Life and Mana, making survivability better once you have this equipped. However, its drawback is that you will lose -1 to all attributes per level, making it a useful item until you reach Level 30 or so. You need to be cautious when leveling, as you might not have sufficient attributes for either your equipment or skills because of the last affix.

Recommended class: Any [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes")

### POE 2 Ghostwrithe Guide

Obtaining [Ghostwrithe](/Ghostwrithe+Tattered+Robe "Path of Exile 2 Ghostwrithe Tattered Robe") early on will give a huge boost to your survivability if your build maximizes Energy Shield as a defensive layer due to its modifiers. It offers an additional 29-37 to Chaos Resistance. Chaos damage, which is a damage type that deals double damage to Energy Shield, can be minimized with the added Chaos Resistance that Ghostwrithe grants you. Also, when wearing Ghostwrithe, half of your HP will be converted into Energy Shield, allowing you to stack **Strength** or obtain equipment with + **Maximum Life** to increase your Energy Shield. Ghostwrithe can be used with [Luminous Pace](/Luminous+Pace+Straw+Sandals "Path of Exile 2 Luminous Pace Straw Sandals") for the faster recharge start of your Energy Shield, making you more durable to absorb more enemy attacks.

Recommended class: Any [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes") that utilizes Energy Shield well

### POE 2 Bitterbloom Guide

[Bitterbloom](/Bitterbloom+Feathered+Robe "Path of Exile 2 Bitterbloom Feathered Robe") is a great alternative to [Ghostwrithe](/Ghostwrithe+Tattered+Robe "Path of Exile 2 Ghostwrithe Tattered Robe") early on, as it offers the same function at a lower magnitude. Bitterbloom increases your Energy Shield by 50-100%, while also making the recharge rate speed up by 50%. However, it has a modifier that changes the way of starting up the Energy Shield recharges, as it requires you to use a Mana Flask for it. Bitterbloom can be a good item if you cannot afford to buy or obtain a Ghostwrithe early on in the campaign, but it should definitely be upgraded once you can purchase a Ghostwrithe in the trade market.

Recommended class: Any [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes") that utilizes Energy Shield well

### POE 2 Enfolding Dawn Guide

[Enfolding Dawn](/Enfolding+Dawn+Pilgrim+Vestments "Path of Exile 2 Enfolding Dawn Pilgrim Vestments") is a great [Unique Body Armor](/Unique+Body+Armor "Path of Exile 2 Unique Body Armor") for builds that use Spirit for [Persistent Skills](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), as this increases Spirit by 100. The Enfolding Dawn can be used by summoner builds to increase the total number of [Skeletal Snipers](/Skeletal+Snipers "Path of Exile 2 Skeletal Snipers") or whichever summon you prefer, or for builds that require certain Auras to be active at all times. It also has an affix that increases all Elemental Resistances, giving you a boost to your resistances which is nice to have early on in the game. However, its disadvantage is that Mana does not scale together with your [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence"), making it so that the only thing that can increase your Mana is by having prefixes that directly increase your maximum Mana.

Recommended class: Any build that utilizes Spirit for [Persistent Skills](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems") (such as [Witch](/Witch "Path of Exile 2 Witch"))

### POE 2 Thief's Torment Guide

[Thief's Torment](/Thief's+Torment+Emerald+Ring "Path of Exile 2 Thief's Torment Emerald Ring") is one of, if not the best Unique Equipment you can get early on in the game. Thief's Torment, aside from its generally great affixes such as an increase to all Elemental Resistances and increased Item Rarity, has two affixes that will solve any build's survivability and Mana sustain. With the Thief's Torment equipped, you will gain 25 Health and 15 Mana for every enemy hit with your attacks, making it so that you will not have problems in sustaining your skill's mana cost throughout the campaign. The only drawback of equipping the Thief's Torment is that you can only equip this ring and nothing else for the second slot. It isn't that much of a drawback for a ring that has tons of affixes that will make the early game a breeze for any build. Also, it costs a lot in terms of currency, so this is only an option if enemies drop one or if you are rolling a second/alt character.

Recommended class: Any [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes")

### POE 2 Revered Resin Guide

[Revered Resin](/Revered+Resin+Amber+Amulet "Path of Exile 2 Revered Resin Amber Amulet") is a straightforward Unique [Amulet](/Amulets "Path of Exile 2 Amulets"), as it provides your character with additional Life and Mana — giving you a boost in your resource sustain. Revered Resin also gives you an additional 10-15 to [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength"), which is great if you need more Strength for equipment or for scaling your HP. It also guarantees your Critical Hit Chance at the cost of not dealing extra damage when you Crit, which can be helpful if you have the [Cast on Critical](/Cast+on+Critical "Path of Exile 2 Cast on Critical") buff. Using the Revered Resin early on can be a huge boost to your survivability, making this a great choice if you are struggling against the mobs in the early acts.

Recommended class: Any [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes")
