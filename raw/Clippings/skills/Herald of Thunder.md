**Herald of Thunder** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Herald of Thunder** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Herald of Thunder Effects

**Herald of Thunder**provides the following effects:

Buff

* Lightning Bolts strike the targets of your next (2–3) Attack Hits after Killing a Shocked Enemy with Attack Damage

Bolt

* Attack Damage: (35–143)%
* Can't be Evaded
* Cannot Shock
* Bolt impact radius is 1 metre
* 100% of Bolt Physical Damage
* Converted to Lightning Damage

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Lightning Bolts strike the targets of your next (0–2) Attack Hits after Killing a Shocked Enemy with Attack Damage

### How to get Herald of Thunder in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Herald of Thunder** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 4** or higher.
* To use the Herald of Thunder gem, it requires Character Level 10 and above, with 25 [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Herald of Thunder Notes and Tips

* Herald of Thunder's Bolt impact radius is now 1 metre (from 1.4) in Patch 0.3.0
