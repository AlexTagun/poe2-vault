**Guiding Palm of the Heart** is a **[Unique Sceptre](/Unique+Sceptres "Path of Exile 2 Unique Sceptres")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Guiding Palm of the Heart** grants players the skill, [Purity of Fire](/Purity+of+Fire "Path of Exile 2 Purity of Fire"), and has modifiers that increases Fire Damage to enemies. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "When the Third Pact was written in stone, the Dreamer gave the alliance of men and beasts knowledge. In return, they gave him a drop of blood; one from each of the races of Wraeclast. In the centuries that followed, his Will began to subtly change." - Book of the Benevolent Dreamer, Histories 220:5

### Path of Exile 2 Guiding Palm of the Heart Stats & Modifiers

**Guiding Palm of the Heart**has the following Stats:

Spirit: 100

**Guiding Palm of the Heart**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 8 [Purity of Fire](/Purity+of+Fire "Path of Exile 2 Purity of Fire")  
Gain 25% of Damage as Extra Fire Damage  
Allies in your Presence deal (15-23) to (28-35) added Attack Fire Damage  
50% of your Base Life Regeneration is granted to Allies in your Presence  
+(20-30) to Strength  
25% increased Light Radius  
Grants effect of Guided Meteoric Shrine

### PoE 2 Guiding Palm of the Heart Requirements

To equip **Guiding Palm of the Heart** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 65, 17 STR, 38  INT

### How to get Guiding Palm of the Heart in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Guiding Palm of the Heart** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
