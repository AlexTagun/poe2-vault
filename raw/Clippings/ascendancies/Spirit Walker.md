**Spirit Walker** is one of the three unique [Huntress](/Huntress "Path of Exile 2 Huntress") [Ascendancy Classes](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") available in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Spirit Walker** has a deep connection with the Azmerian Animal Spirits, which allows her to infuse her power with various spirits. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Spirit Walker Key Features

Spirit Walkers infuse their power with the Azmerian Animal Spirits, the Stag, the Owl, or the Bear, to perform various attacks and skills.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* Vivid Stampede: Grants Skill: Vivid Stampede. Gain a Vivid Wisp for every 20 metres you move, up to a maximum of 3. Expend all Vivid Wisps to trigger Vivid Stampede when you Attack.
* Primal Bounty: Grants Skill: Primal Bounty. Gain a Primal Owl Feather every 3 seconds, up to a maximum of 3. Expend an Owl Feather when you Dodge to trigger Primal Bounty
* Wild Protector: Grants Skill: Wild Protector.
* The Morrigan's Guidance: Gain a Vivid Wisp when Vivid Stampede ends. Stags deal 20% more damage per leap. Stags have 20% more Shack Magnitude per leap
* The Mhacha's Gift: Dodging can expend up to 3 Owl Feathers, granting Primal Bounty 100 % more Empowerment effect per additional Feather expended. Gain Owl Feather 50% faster.
* The Catha's Balance: Companions deal additional Attack damage equal to 60% of your main hand weapon damage
* Sacred Unity: Bear Spirit gains Embrace of the Wild. Vivid Stags leap toward enemies. Central Projectile of Owl Feather- Empowered Skills leaves a trail of Soaring Ground
* The Natural Order: Tame Beast can capture Unique Beasts. Can have up to one Unique Beast summoned. Unique Tame Beasts have 30% increased movement speed. Tamed Beasts are possessed by random Azmeri Spirits, changing every 20 seconds.
* Idolatry: Companions deal 10% increased damage per Idol in your Equipment. 2% increased Reservation Efficiency of Skills per Idol in your Equipment. -4% to all Elemental Resistances per non-Odol Augment in  your Equipment

##### **Azmerian Animal Spirits:**

* Stag: Summon a stampede of stags with each attack.
* Owl: Grants you boons in the form of their feathers, whch will increase the amount of projectiles you throw and increases the projectile speed.
* Bear: Summon a giant bear to assist you in defeating nearby enemies.

### Path of Exile 2 Spirit Walker Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Spirit Walker**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Versatility:**With the Azmerian Animal Spirits by the **Spirit Walker's** side, it provides players with different forms, each with its own advantages. This allows players to use various skills that fit any scenario and any boss fight.

### Cons

**-****Complicated to Master:** Because of the many combinations of skills and forms, new players may get overwhelmed and find it difficult to find the right set of skills needed to fit the combat scenario they're in.

### Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) Spirit Walker Skill Gems

##### Skill Gems and Customization:

To unlock new skills, [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") must be found and used. Activating a [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") opens the gemcutting menu, which allows players to level up existing skills or engrave new ones. Skill Gems retain their level, so newly engraved skills match the level of the gem, encouraging experimentation without penalty.

Since abilities are powered by Skill Gems, all classes have the freedom to use any gem they choose, offering limitless build possibilities.

### Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) Spirit Walker Showcase Overview

Grinding Gear Games recently released a new showcase video highlighting the upcoming **Spirit Walker** Ascendancy Class, featuring several of its core mechanics, skills, and potential builds. Much of the Ascendancy revolves around three skill nodes: **Vivid Stampede**, **Primal Bounty**, and **Wild Protector**, each offering a distinct playstyle that can also synergize together through a special passive called **Sacred Unity**.

![spirit walker showcase1 path of exile 2 wiki guide 600px](/file/Path-of-Exile-2/spirit-walker-showcase1-path-of-exile-2-wiki-guide-600px.jpg "Spirit Walker Ascendancy Showcase Path of Exile 2 Wiki Guide")

Investing in all three skill nodes unlocks **Sacred Unity**, a passive that further enhances each skill node with stronger effects and additional utilities. The showcase also highlighted additional notable passives, including **Idolatry**, which strongly benefits minion-focused builds, and **The Natural Order**, which allows [Tame Beast](/Tame+Beast "Path of Exile 2 Tame Beast") to control even bosses.

### Spirit Walker Endgame Builds

Grinding Gear Games also showcased two endgame build examples to demonstrate the potential of the [Spirit Walker](/Spirit+Walker "Path of Exile 2 Spirit Walker") Ascendancy Class and give players ideas on how to build around and optimize its unique mechanics and playstyles.

#### Spirit Walker Stormbringer Build

![stormbringer spirit walker build path of exile 2 wiki guide 400px](/file/Path-of-Exile-2/stormbringer-spirit-walker-build-path-of-exile-2-wiki-guide-400px.jpg "Stormbringer Build Spirit Walker Ascendancy Class Path of Exile 2 Wiki Guide")

* The Stormbringer Build focuses on using [Whirling Slash](/Whirling+Slash "Path of Exile 2 Whirling Slash") and [Barrage](/Barrage "Path of Exile 2 Barrage") to generate devastating twisters that shred through enemies.
* By combining Barrage with the new **Primal Bounty**, the build produces a massive number of twisters.
* The build invests into **Wild Protector**, **Primal Bounty**, and **Vivid Stampede**, unlocking the **Sacred Unity** passive.
* **Sacred Unity** significantly enhances all three skill nodes, adding new utilities and stronger effects.
* Through **Primal Bounty** and **The Mhacha's Gift**, the build generates Primal Owl Feathers over time.
* These feathers are consumed after a dodge roll, empowering the next projectile attack and causing Barrage to release more twisters with increased speed and coverage.
* Vivid Stampede generates a Vivid Wisp every 10 meters traveled, which can be accelerated by moving along **Soaring Ground**.
* These Wisps can be consumed to unleash a stampede of spectral stags that deal damage and create Shocked Ground.

#### Spirit Walker Zookeeper Build

![zookeeper spirit walker build path of exile 2 wiki guide 400px](/file/Path-of-Exile-2/zookeeper-spirit-walker-build-path-of-exile-2-wiki-guide-400px.jpg "Zookeeper Build Spirit Walker Ascendancy Class Path of Exile 2 Wiki Guide")

* The Zookeeper Build is designed for players who prefer a more passive playstyle, letting an army of animal companions handle combat for them.
* The build focuses on maintaining up to six active companion skills at once, enabled by the new **Sylvan's Effigy** arriving with the [Return of the Ancients](/Return+of+the+Ancients "Path of Exile 2 Return of the Ancients") expansion.
* This Unique Sceptre removes the normal companion cap entirely and also summons an ancient **Azmerian Wolf** that buffs all allied companions on the battlefield.
* The setup combines **Wolf Pack**, the new **Wild Protector**, and multiple Tamed Beasts such as the **Quadrilla**, **Plague Harvester**, and **Mighty Silverfist**.
* Remaining slots are filled with [Skeletal Clerics](/Skeletal+Cleric "Path of Exile 2 Skeletal Cleric") for consistent healing and sustain across all companions.
* The core gameplay loop is simple: summon companions, position safely, and allow your minions to overwhelm enemies while you support from a distance.
* Companion damage is also expected to be buffed across the board with the [Return of the Ancients](/Return+of+the+Ancients "Path of Exile 2 Return of the Ancients") expansion.
* The build heavily scales through the Spirit Walker’s **Idolatry** passive, maximizing companion power via additional Idol slots.
* This is further amplified by the **Darkness Enthroned Fine Belt**, which increases Idol capacity even more.
* To further boost damage, the build allocates into **The Catha’s Balance** and uses a strong Spear such as the **Vengeance Edge Grand Spear**, allowing your weapon’s damage to directly scale your companions for maximum overall DPS.
