**Shockwave Totem** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Shockwave Totem** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Shockwave Totem Effects

**Shockwave Totem**provides the following effects:

Totem

* Totem duration is 12 seconds
* Limit 1 Totem
* Totem gains +(0–75)% to all Elemental Resistances
* Totem gains +(0–35)% to Chaos Resistance

Slam

* Attack Speed: 50% of base
* Attack Damage: (25–66)% of base
* Shockwave radius is 3 metres

Jagged Ground Eruption

* Shockwave Radius is 3 metres

Additional Effects From Quality:  
+(0–4) seconds to Totem duration

### How to get Shockwave Totem in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Shockwave Totem**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 3**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 3 and must be used with any Martial Weapon or Unarmed.

### Path of Exile 2 Shockwave Totem Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0  
  + Slam now has an Attack Speed of 50% of base (previously 100%).
  + Slam now deals 30-66% Attack Damage at Gem levels 3-20 (previously 96-211%)
  + Jagged Ground Eruption now deals 108-237% Attack Damage (previously 24-53%).
  + No longer has the Physical tag.
  + Quality now has +0-4 seconds to Totem Duration (previously Totem base attack time is 0 to -0.2 seconds).
  + Shockwave Totem cannot itself create Jagged Ground, and supports no longer apply to Shockwave Totem.
