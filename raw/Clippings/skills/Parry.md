**Parry** is a **[Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. Ready your Buckler to parry the next Strike or Projectile that would Hit you, Blocking the Hit and retaliating with a quick sweep that leaves enemies off balance, causing them to take massively increased Attack damage for a short duration. Parrying causes you to accumulate Heavy Stun buildup. You cannot Evade a Hit you could parry, but Evasion instead grants an equal chance to avoid this Heavy Stun buildup. Players can unlock any skill from the Passive Skill Tree by spending a skill point on a skill connected to their starting point or already unlocked skills, Passive Skills that grant attributes, let you select which attribute you want to increase, allowing players to explore the tree in any direction.

### Information for Parry in Path of Exile 2

* Type: [Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")
* [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"): All
* Requirements: [Bucklers](/Unique+Bucklers "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bucklers")

### Path of Exile 2 Parry Effects

**Parry**provides the following effects:

Can't be Evaded  
400% more Stun buildup  
Parried Debuff makes targets take 50% more Attack Damage  
Parried Debuff duration is 2 seconds  
3 to 4 Added Physical Damage per 5 Evasion Rating on Buckler  
Can Parry Projectiles fired from within 1 metre
