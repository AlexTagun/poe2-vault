# Merchants — Path of Exile 2

> Merchants are essential NPCs in Path of Exile 2, providing players with services and items to enhance their gameplay experience.

## Trading System

Nearly everything is up for trade in PoE 2, including gear, crafting currencies, and boss rewards. **Gold cannot be traded between players** — it is used exclusively for vendor purchases and respeccing skills. Trading between players requires inviting them to a party and accessing the trade window in a hideout; transactions use a barter system with crafting currencies.

## Passive Skill Respeccing

Players can reallocate passive skill points by spending gold. The passive skill tree contains approximately 1,500 nodes across all character classes.

## Merchant Inventory Refresh

When a character levels up, merchant stock updates to include items scaled to the current progression level.

## Currency Exchange (Marketplace)

A marketplace system allows players to exchange crafting currencies with other players. Features:
- Search functionality with market ratio displays
- Small gold fee applied based on transaction quantity

## Disenchanting

Players can break down unwanted equipment at the Magic Item Merchant:
- **Magic items** → Transmutation Orb components
- **Rare items** → Regal Orb components

## Gambling

Certain merchants offer randomized item purchases for a fixed gold cost. Available especially during early-to-mid game stages.
