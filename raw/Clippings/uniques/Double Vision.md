**Double Vision Dyad Crossbow** is a **[Unique Crossbow](/Unique+Crossbows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Crossbows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Double Vision Dyad Crossbow**grants Gemini Surge skill and adds Physical Damage Stats. It can also boost your Critical Hit chance and has a reduced Reload Speed. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> For those without a home in the Vastiri, the hot day is harsh, but the chill night is far worse.

### Path of Exile 2 Double Vision Dyad Crossbow Stats & Modifiers

**Double Vision Dyad Crossbow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (23-30) to (62-74)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.6  
Reload Time: 1.1

**Double Vision Dyad Crossbow**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 6 Gemini Surge  
Loads an additional bolt  
Adds (14-21) to (25-37) Physical Damage  
+5% to Critical Hit Chance  
(15-25)% increased Reload Speed  
When you reload, triggers Gemini Surge to alternately  
gain (2-6) Cold Surges or (2-6) Fire Surges  
Trigger reload infusion on reload [1]

### PoE 2 Double Vision Dyad Crossbow Requirements

To equip **Double Vision Dyad Crossbow** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 20, 22 STR, 22 DEX

### How to get Double Vision Dyad Crossbow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Double Vision Dyad Crossbow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
