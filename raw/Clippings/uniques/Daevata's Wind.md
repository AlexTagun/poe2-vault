**Daevata's Wind War Spear** is a [Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Daevata's Wind War Spear,** a versatile spear that enhances both melee and ranged damage based on recent combat style, increases projectile speed and physical strikes, and boosts evasion and attack speed. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> You killed their Golden Sekhema. The Maraketh will think of nothing  
> but vengeance now." - Dimos, Advisor to General Titucius

### Path of Exile 2 Daevata's Wind War Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Daevata's Wind War Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (24-29) to (47-52)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.76-1.92)  
Weapon Range: 1.5

**Daevata's Wind War Spear**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Spear Throw  
(25–35)% increased Projectile Speed with this Weapon  
Adds (10–15) to (21–26) Physical Damage  
+100 to Evasion Rating  
(10–20)% increased Attack Speed  
(30–60)% increased Melee Damage if you've dealt a Projectile Attack Hit in the past eight seconds  
(30–60)% increased Projectile Damage if you've dealt a Melee Hit in the past eight seconds

### PoE 2 Daevata's Wind War Spear Requirements

To equip **Daevata's Wind War Spear** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 21
* Stats: 14 Str, 31 Dex

### How to get Daevata's Wind War Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Daevata's Wind War Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
