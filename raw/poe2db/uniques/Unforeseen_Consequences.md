# Unforeseen Consequences

**Base:** Abyss Precursor Tablet
**Class:** Tablet

**Requires: Level 65**

## Implicit

- Adds Abysses to a Map 1 use remaining

## Modifiers

- % reduced Pack Size in Map
- (4 — 6) additional Rare Monsters are spawned from Abysses in Map
- Map contains (14 — 18) additional Abysses
- Map is overrun by the Abyssal
- % increased Quantity of Waystones found in Map

## Flavour

> The Well was naught. The Precursors dug too deep. The Well always had been.
