**Cast on Shock** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Cast on Shock** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

**NOTE:**As of Patch 0.3.0, **Cast on Shock** is no longer active, along with [Cast on Freeze](/Cast+on+Freeze "Path of Exile 2 Cast on Freeze") and [Cast on Ignite](/Cast+on+Ignite "Path of Exile 2 Cast on Ignite"). The three have been merged into [Cast on Elemental Ailment](/Cast+on+Elemental+Ailment "Path of Exile 2 Cast on Elemental Ailment").

### Path of Exile 2 Cast on Shock Effects

**Cast on Shock**provides the following effects:

* Gains 1 Energy per Power of enemies you Shock with Hits from Skills
* (0–57)% increased Energy gained
* Triggers all Socketed Spells and loses all Energy on reaching maximum Energy

Additional Effects from Quality:

* (0–15)% increased Energy gained
* Has 10 maximum Energy per 0.1 seconds of base cast time of Socketed Spells

### How to get Cast on Shock in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Cast on Shock** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 8**.
* To use the Cast on Shock gem, it requires Character Level 26 and above, with 61 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") or more.
