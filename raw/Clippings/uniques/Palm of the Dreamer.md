**Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre** is a **[Unique Sceptre](/Unique+Sceptres "Path of Exile 2 Unique Sceptres")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre** is a sceptre that increases the Chaos Damage dealt by you and your allies if you are close to them. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

### Path of Exile 2 Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre Stats & Modifiers

**Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre**has the following Stats:

Spirit: 100

**Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 8 [Impurity](/Impurity "Path of Exile 2 Impurity")  
 Allies in your Presence deal (13-17) to (25-37) added Attack Chaos Damage  
+(7-13) to all Attributes  
23% reduced Light Radius  
(-13-13)% increased Skill Effect Duration  
Gain 27% of Damage as Extra Chaos Damage  
Grants effect of Dreaming Gloom Shrine  
Corrupted

### PoE 2 Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre Requirements

To equip **Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 65, 17 STR, 38 INT

### How to get Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Palm of the Dreamer Shrine Sceptre** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
