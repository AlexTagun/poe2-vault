# Armor

Armor in Path of Exile 2 represents essential protective equipment that reduces incoming damage. Different armor types correspond to character attributes.

## Defense Types

| Defense | Attribute | Description |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Armour | Strength | Reduces physical damage taken |
| Evasion Rating | Dexterity | Chance to avoid attacks |
| Energy Shield | Intelligence | Secondary health pool |

Hybrid armor combinations (e.g., Armour + Evasion) require mixed attributes.

## Item Rarities

- **Normal** — No modifiers. Foundation for crafting.
- **Magic** — 1–2 modifiers. Apply Orb of Transmutation to Normal.
- **Rare** — Up to 6 modifiers. Apply Regal Orb to Magic, Exalted Orb to enhance further.
- **Unique** — Fixed predefined modifiers. Often build-enabling effects.

## Socket System

- Body Armour: up to 2 sockets
- Helmet, Boots, Gloves: 1 socket each
- Sockets accept Ezomyte Runes for unique effects
- Salvage unwanted armor at Salvage Bench → Artificer's Orbs for socket crafting

## Crafting

- Normal → Magic: Orb of Transmutation
- Magic → Rare: Regal Orb
- Further enhancement: Exalted Orb
- Disenchantment System: salvaging Rare armor contributes toward earning Regal and Exalted Orbs

## Item Tiers

Most armor bases come in three tiers:
- **Base** — early to mid progression
- **Advanced** — mid to high level (approx. Lvl 45–62)
- **Expert** — endgame (approx. Lvl 65–75)
