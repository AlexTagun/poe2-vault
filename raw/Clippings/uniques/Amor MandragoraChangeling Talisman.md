**Amor Mandragora Changeling Talisman** is a **[Unique Talisman](/Unique+Talismans "Path of Exile 2 Unique Talismans")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Amor Mandragora Changeling Talisman** will grant the user a huge amount of power, especially when the user spends rage. This unique weapon will also affect the damage and skill effect duration. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> A sensitive few among the first settlers of Ezomyr followed the wisps by canoe. On a misty forested island, Cirel of Tarth stood waiting to greet them.

### Path of Exile 2 Amor Mandragora Changeling Talisman Stats & Modifiers

**Amor Mandragora Changeling Talisman**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (25-29) to (38-42)  
Critical Hit Chance: 8%  
Attacks Per Second: 1.25  
Weapon Range: 1.2

**Amor Mandragora Changeling Talisman**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (16-20) to (23-27) Physical Damage  
+(8-15) to Intelligence  
(10-15)% increased Skill Effect Duration  
Enemies in your Presence are Hindered  
Gain 1 Druidic Prowess for every 20 total Rage spent
