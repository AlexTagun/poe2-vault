**Nebuloch Brigand Mace** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Nebuloch Brigand Mace,** a brutal mace infused with chaos energy, capable of devastating critical strikes fueled by Endurance Charges, but inflicts chaotic backlash on the user with each charge held. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> They hoped that, trapped in its prison,  
> the creature would age and perish.  
> But time would not touch the fiend.

### Path of Exile 2 Nebuloch Brigand Mace Stats & Modifiers

**Nebuloch Brigand Mace**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (79-88) to (129-139)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.45  
Weapon Range: 1.1

**Nebuloch Brigand Mace**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (39–48) to (69–79) Physical Damage  
+(20–30)% to Critical Damage Bonus  
Adds (25–36) to (44–55) Chaos damage  
Attacks consume an Endurance Charge to Critically Hit  
Take 100 Chaos damage per second per Endurance Charge

### PoE 2 Nebuloch Brigand Mace Requirements

To equip **Nebuloch Brigand Mace** players must meet the following requirements:

* Requires: Level 55
* Stats: 97 Str

### How to get Nebuloch Brigand Mace in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Nebuloch Brigand Mace** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
