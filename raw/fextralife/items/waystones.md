# Waystones

Waystones play a critical role in combating the corruption caused by The Beast, enabling players to cleanse the land and gather valuable resources. Players activate a portal device at the Ziggurat to access Atlas areas.

## Portal Colors
- Blue = accessible maps
- Grey = maps available for progression
- Green = completed maps

## Crafting
- Prefix mods enhance rewards
- Suffix mods increase difficulty

## Completion
To cleanse corruption, players must defeat all powerful, rare, and unique enemies in targeted areas.

## Death Penalty
Failed map runs render the area inaccessible until alternate routing is found.
