**Herald of Plague** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Herald of Plague** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Herald of Plague Effects

**Herald of Plague**provides the following effects:

* Spreads the most Damaging Poison on enemies you kill in a (1.5–1.8) metre radius (10–29)% chance to Hinder enemies on spreading Poison to them

Additional Effects From Quality:

* 0(0-20)% Chance to Hinder enemies on Spreading Poison to them

### How to get Herald of Plague in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Herald of Plague** can be crafted by engraving it to an Uncut Spirit Gem Level (1–20).
* To use the Herald of Plague gem, it requires Character Level (1–90) and above, with (1–205) [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity").
