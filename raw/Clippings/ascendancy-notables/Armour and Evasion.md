**Armour and Evasion** is a **[Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. This passive Skill increases your Armor and Evasion stats. Players can unlock any skill from the Passive Skill Tree by spending a skill point on a skill connected to their starting point or already unlocked skills, Passive Skills that grant attributes, let you select which attribute you want to increase, allowing players to explore the tree in any direction.

### Information for Armour and Evasion in Path of Exile 2

* Type: [Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")
* [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"): [Tactician](/Tactician "Path of Exile 2 Tactician")
* Requirements: **TBA**

### Path of Exile 2 Armour and Evasion Effects

Armour and Evasion provides the following effects:

* 15% increased Armor and Evasion rating

### Path of Exile 2 Armour and Evasion Notes and Tips

* Armour: Armour reduces Physical damage from hits. Damage reduction is proportional to the size of the hit, and is more effective at reducing smaller hits.
* Evasion: Evasion grants a chance to avoid enemy Strikes or Projectiles. Exact chance of success also depends on the attacker's Accuracy.
