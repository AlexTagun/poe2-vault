**Arctic Armour** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Arctic Armour** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Arctic Armour Effects

**Arctic Armour**provides the following effects:

* Deals 18 to 28 Cold Damage per Stage
* Gains a Stage every 1.42 seconds, up to a maximum of 3 Stages

Additional Effects from Quality:

* Gains a Stage every 0 seconds, up to a maximum of 0(0-1) Stages

### How to get Arctic Armour in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Arctic Armour** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level (1–20)**.
* To use the Arctic Armour gem, it requires Character Level 10 and above, with (1–90), with (1–205) [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").
