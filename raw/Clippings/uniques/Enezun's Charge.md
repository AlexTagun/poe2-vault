**Enezun's Charge Volatile Wand** is a **[Unique Wand](/Unique+Wands "Path of Exile 2 Unique Wands")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Enezun's Charge Volatile Wand** is a wand that boosts spell damage, critical hit chance, and mana gain per enemy killed while preserving corpses. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> He alone was welcome in the sacred spaces of the Titans.

### Path of Exile 2 Enezun's Charge Volatile Wand Stats & Modifiers

**Enezun's Charge Volatile Wand**features the following Modifiers:

(80-100%) increased Spell Damage  
25% chance to not destroy Corpses when Consuming Corpses Gain  
(10-15) Mana per Enemy Killed  
(30-50)% increased Critical Hit Chance for Spells

### PoE 2 Enezun's Charge Volatile Wand Requirements

To equip **Enezun's Charge Volatile Wand** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 16, 31 INT

### How to get Enezun's Charge Volatile Wand in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Enezun's Charge Volatile Wand** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
