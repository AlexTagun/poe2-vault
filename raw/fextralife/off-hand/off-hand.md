# Off-Hand Equipment

Off-Hand gear in Path of Exile 2 is used alongside your main weapon to provide extra benefits. It comes in three main categories designed for different playstyles.

## Off-Hand Categories

- **Shields** — Provide defensive bonuses (block chance, armor, evasion, energy shield)
- **Foci** — Increase damage for spell casting builds (energy shield, intelligence scaling)
- **Quivers** — Support ranged bow attacks with various attack modifiers

## Shield Sub-types

### Tower Shields
- Highest armor values
- -3% movement speed penalty
- Require Strength

### Targes (Crest Shields)
- Balanced armor / energy shield or armor / evasion
- -1.5% movement speed penalty
- Require Strength + Intelligence or Strength + Dexterity

### Bucklers
- Pure evasion rating
- No movement speed penalty
- Require Dexterity only

## Foci
Magical implements for spellcasters. Provide energy shield and bonuses to spells. Require Intelligence. No block chance.

## Quivers
Bow-exclusive off-hand. Provide implicit attack modifiers. No defense stats. Require only level (no attribute requirements on most).
