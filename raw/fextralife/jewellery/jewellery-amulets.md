# Amulets

Amulets are a type of Jewellery in Path of Exile 2. They provide various benefits such as increased elemental resistances, enhanced life and mana, and bonuses related to specific skills or attributes. Players can equip only one amulet at a time.

## All Amulets

| Name | Implicit Mod |
|------|-------------|
| Crimson Amulet | (2–4) Life Regeneration per second |
| Azure Amulet | (20–30)% increased Mana Regeneration Rate |
| Amber Amulet | +(10–15) to Strength |
| Jade Amulet | +(10–15) to Dexterity |
| Lapis Amulet | +(10–15) to Intelligence |
| Lunar Amulet | +(20–30) to Maximum Energy Shield |
| Bloodstone Amulet | +(30–40) to Maximum Life |
| Stellar Amulet | +(5–7) to All Attributes |
| Solar Amulet | +(10–15) to Spirit |
| Gold Amulet | (12–20)% increased Rarity of Items Found |

## Notes

- No attribute requirements or level requirements listed on base types
- Amulet choice heavily influences character's attribute and stat priorities
- Unique amulets can dramatically alter playstyles (e.g., Astramentis for attribute stacking)
