# Omens

Omens in Path of Exile 2 are special items that enable meta crafting, modifying crafting items to influence other crafting outcomes. These consumable items can be purchased with tribute and activate while held in inventory.

## Omen List

| Omen Name | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Omen of Amelioration | Prevent 75% of Experience loss when you die |
| Omen of Corruption | Next Vaal Orb application becomes more unpredictable |
| Omen of Dextral Alchemy | Next Orb of Alchemy will result in the maximum number of suffix modifiers |
| Omen of Dextral Annulment | Next Orb of Annulment will remove only suffix modifiers |
| Omen of Dextral Coronation | Next Regal Orb will add only suffix modifiers |
| Omen of Dextral Erasure | Next Chaos Orb will remove only suffix modifiers |
| Omen of Dextral Exaltation | Next Exalted Orb will add only suffix modifiers |
| Omen of Greater Annulment | Next Annulment removes two modifiers instead of one |
| Omen of Greater Exaltation | Next Exalted Orb will add two random modifiers |
| Omen of Refreshment | Fully recover your flask and charm charges when you reach Low Life |
| Omen of Resurgence | Fully recover your Life, Mana and Energy Shield when you reach Low Life |
| Omen of Sinistral Alchemy | Next Orb of Alchemy will result in the maximum number of prefix modifiers |
| Omen of Sinistral Annulment | Next Orb of Annulment will remove only prefix modifiers |
| Omen of Sinistral Coronation | Next Regal Orb will add only prefix modifiers |
| Omen of Sinistral Erasure | Next Chaos Orb will remove only prefix modifiers |
| Omen of Sinistral Exaltation | Next Exalted Orb will add only prefix modifiers |
| Omen of Whittling | Next Chaos Orb will remove the lowest level modifier |
