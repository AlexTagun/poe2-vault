# Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies — Fextralife snapshot

## Overview

Ascendancies (Ascendancy Classes) — это субклассы, которые игрок выбирает по достижении определённого уровня. Каждый из **семи базовых классов** даёт три варианта Ascendancy → **21 ascendancy** всего (по версии источника). Открываются по ходу кампании в Актах 2, 3, 4 — не через лабиринт, как в PoE 1.

> Замечание: источник пишет "seven base classes", но перечисляет восемь (Mercenary, Monk, Ranger, Sorceress, Warrior, Witch, Huntress, Druid). У Ranger и Druid в списке только по 2 ascendancy — вероятно, третья ещё не вышла.

## Ascendancy Classes by Base Class

### Mercenary
- **Witchhunter** — Uses wards for elemental defense and rituals causing enemy explosions
- **Gemling Legionnaire** — Embeds gems into body to enhance ability power and gain additional skill slots
- **Tactician** — Former soldier trained in martial command, supports allies in combat

### Monk
- **Invoker** — Triggers elemental waves and can specialize in specific elements for freezing/shocking effects
- **Acolyte of Chayula** — "Embraces the darkness, using it both as a shield and a weapon"
- **Martial Artist** — Master of three Hollow Techniques, performing AoE attacks to clear enemies

### Ranger
- **Deadeye** — "Expert in marksman, capable of firing extra projectiles and increased movement speed"
- **Pathfinder** — Expert in flasks and poison; throws explosive bottles using flask charges

### Sorceress
- **Stormweaver** — Master of elements unleashing devastating elemental attacks
- **Chronomancer** — Manipulates time to stop enemies or rewind for health/mana recovery
- **Disciple of Varashta** — Uses spells, curses, minions, and djinns to devastate enemies

### Warrior
- **Warbringer** — "Channels the might of their ancestors to gain tremendous power" including corpse explosions
- **Titan** — Utilizes heavy-hitting attacks triggering shockwaves and after-effects
- **Smith of Kitava** — Master blacksmith embracing Kitava's power; forges weapons

### Witch
- **Blood Mage** — Master of life and energy; unleashes blood magic while leeching from enemies
- **Infernalist** — Summons hellhound companion and transforms into devastating demon form
- **Lich** — Master of chaos and curses; uses jewel as phylactery after sacrificing soul portion

### Huntress
- **Amazon** — Master huntress using precise critical strikes and revealing enemy weaknesses
- **Ritualist** — Dark practitioner using blood sacrifice and corrupted lifeforce against foes
- **Spirit Walker** — Infuses power with Azmerian animal spirits (Stag, Owl, Bear)

### Druid
- **Oracle** — "Perfected the power of Foresight, using it to gain advantage by peering into the future"
- **Shaman** — Channels nature's fury, harnessing rage to empower spells and rain meteors/lightning

## Key Ascendancy Mechanics

### Spirit Walker (New)
Core paths:
- **Vivid Stampede** — Conjures stampeding stags dealing AoE damage and leaving shocked ground
- **Primal Bounty** — Owl spirit grants feathers consumed for extra projectiles
- **Wild Protector** — Summons bear spirit companion without occupying normal companion slot
- **Sacred Unity** — Free passive enhancing all three nodes with stronger effects

### Martial Artist (New)
Three Hollow Techniques:
- **Hollow Form Technique** — Creates illusions attacking with chosen skill
- **Hollow Focus Technique** — Manifests spiritual energy as bells around character
- **Hollow Resonance Technique** — Manifests bell on back, ringing on critical hits

## Ascension Trials System

### Trial of the Sekhemas (Maraketh Culture)

**Entry Requirements:** Capture djinn soul in coin; place in Relic Altar.

**Mechanics:**
- Multiple rooms with unique challenges (rare monster blood collection, etc.)
- **Honour system** — damage reduces honour; running out causes trial failure
- Room rewards (keys, treasures) unlock later progression
- Player chooses next challenge from map after each room completion

**Afflictions:** Некоторые пути накладывают дебаффы (пример: Spiked Shell — увеличивает HP всех монстров).

**Boons & Resources:**
- Powerful blessings от djinn (пример: Sekhema's Cloak — revive on death)
- **Sacred Water** can restore honour, grant boons, or remove afflictions

**Final Challenge:** Defeat powerful boss → ascend, выбор ascendancy class, +2 ascendancy points.

**Rewards:** Открытие treasure chests ключами (silver/gold keys → более ценный лут).

### Trial of Chaos (Vaal Culture)

**Entry Requirements:** Token от существа Trialmaster.

**Mechanics:**
- Перед каждой комнатой Trialmaster предлагает reward + выбор **Tribulations** — модификаторов, повышающих сложность
- Environmental hazards (Shocking Turrets, Trap Runes)
- Room types: survival waves, escort missions (защита Stone Idol)

**Risk vs. Reward:**
- Забрать лут и выйти, либо добавить tribulations и удвоить награды (риск провала)
- Tribulations стэкаются, эскалируя сложность

**Boss:** Случайно выбран — варьируется от ранa к рану.

**Rewards:** +2 ascendancy points за победу над боссом.

## Ascendancy Point Allocation

Игрок может смешивать испытания или собрать все точки в одном trial. Каждое прохождение даёт 2 точки в выбранное ascendancy-дерево.
