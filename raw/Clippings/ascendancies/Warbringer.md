**Warbringer** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Warrior](/Warrior "Path of Exile 2 Warrior")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Warbringer** channels the power of ancestral spirits to unleash devastating abilities. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Warbringer Key Features

Channels the might of their ancestors to gain tremendous power such as encasing their body with a protective layer to absorb damage or trigger the corpses of fallen enemies to explode.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Answered Call](/Answered+Call "Path of Exile 2 Answered Call"): Grants Skill: [Ancestral Spirits](/Ancestral+Spirits "Path of Exile 2 Ancestral Spirits"). Trigger Ancestral Spirits when you Summon a Totem. also provides +1 to the Maximum number of Summoned Totems.
* [Anvil's Weight](/Anvil's+Weight "Path of Exile 2 Anvil's Weight"): Break Armour equal to 10% of Hit Damage dealt
* [Imploding Impacts](/Imploding+Impacts "Path of Exile 2 Imploding Impacts"): You can Break Enemy Armour to below 0 and grants Fully Broken Armour you inflict increases all Damage Taken from Hits instead
* [Jade Heritage](/Jade+Heritage "Path of Exile 2 Jade Heritage"): Grants Skill: [Encase in Jade](/Encase+in+Jade "Path of Exile 2 Encase in Jade"). Gain a stack of Jade every second
* [Renly's Training](/Renly's+Training "Path of Exile 2 Renly's Training"): Gain 35% Base Chance to Block from Equipped Shield instead of the Shield's value
* [Turtle Charm](/Turtle+Charm "Path of Exile 2 Turtle Charm"): You take 30% of damage from Blocked Hits, and Maximum Block chance is 75%.
* [Warcaller's Bellow](/Warcaller's+Bellow "Path of Exile 2 Warcaller's Bellow"): Warcries Explode Corpses dealing 25% of their Life as Physical Damage. Ignore Warcry Cooldowns.
* [Wooden Wall](/Wooden+Wall "Path of Exile 2 Wooden Wall"): 20% of Damage from Hits is taken from your nearest Totem's Life before you

### Path of Exile 2 Warbringer Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Warbringer**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Defensive :**As a melee ascendancy, the **Warbringer** has defensive options that can be utilized when facing enemies head-to-head.

**+ Crowd Control**: A **Warbringer** can deal with groups of enemies easily, having enough damage and defensive skills to withstand and clear mobs.

### Cons

**-****Life and Mana:** It can be difficult to manage Life and Mana, as a **Warbringer** will use skills and be in the middle of combat as a melee class.

**- Mechanics**: Due to being a melee ascendancy, a **Warbringer** will most likely have a harder time against ranged classes and ascendancies.
