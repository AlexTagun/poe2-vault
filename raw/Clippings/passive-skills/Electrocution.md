**Electrocution** is an **[Elemental Ailment](/Status+Ailments "Path of Exile 2 Status Ailments")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. When a unit is afflicted with **Electrocution**, their actions will be interrupted, and they will be prevented from performing any action. The base duration for Electrocution lasts for 5 seconds and can be triggered when the Electrocution Buildup is filled. Electrocution is associated with Lightning Damage, however, not all Lightning Damage will contribute to **Electrocution Buildup**.

### Electrocution Information and Mechanics Path of Exile 2

When a unit gets afflicted by Electrocution, their actions will be canceled and they will be unable to move for the next 5 seconds. Electrocution is afflicted through Lightning damage, however, only hits with the Electrocution effect will contribute to the Electrocution Buildup.

* Only Lightning damage from Hits with specific skills or effects contribute to Electrocution Buildup on enemies until they become Electrocute. Other sources of Lightning Damage will not build up towards Electrocution.
