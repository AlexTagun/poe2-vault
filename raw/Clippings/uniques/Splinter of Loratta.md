**Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear** is a [Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear,** a long-reaching spear that consistently applies poison on hit and allows multiple toxins to linger simultaneously, delivering precise physical strikes. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The Baleful Gem's corruption lingers still...

### Path of Exile 2 Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (7-8) to (15-17)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.6  
Weapon Range: 1.5

**Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (2–3) to (6–8) Physical Damage  
Deal no Elemental Damage  
20% increased Melee Strike Range with this weapon  
Any number of Poisons from this Weapon can affect a target at the same time  
Always Poison on Hit with this weapon

### PoE 2 Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear Requirements

To equip **Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear** players must meet the following requirements:

* Requires: Level 1

### How to get Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Splinter of Loratta Hardwood Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
