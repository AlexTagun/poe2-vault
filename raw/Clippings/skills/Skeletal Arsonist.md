**Skeletal Arsonist** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Skeletal Arsonist** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Skeletal Arsonist Effects

**Skeletal Arsonist**provides the following effects:

Command

* Cost: (9–93) Mana
* Can target Minions with (29–1379) Life or less

Explosive Demise

* Cooldown Time: 3.00 s
* Attack Damage: 300% of base

*Detonate an allied Minion whose current Life is below a threshold, dealing heavy Attack damage to nearby enemies*.

* Converts 100% of Physical Damage to Fire Damage
* Deals additional Fire Damage equal to 8% of Minion's maximum Life
* Can target Minions with (30–1096) Life or less
* Explosion radius is 2.4 metres

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Deals additional Fire Damage equal to +(0–4)% of Minion's maximum Life

### How to get Skeletal Arsonist in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Skeletal Arsonist**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 3**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 6, with at least 16 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").

### Path of Exile 2 Skeletal Arsonist Notes and Tips

* Skeletal Arsonist's Explosive Demise is now an Attack instead of a Spell, dealing 300% Attack Damage. Now deals additional Fire Damage equal to 8% of Minion's maximum Life (previously 12%), and can now target Minions with 30-1096 Life or less (previously 27-913) in Patch 0.3.0
* As of [Patch 0.1.0e](/Patch+Notes#15 "Path of Exile 2 Patch Notes#15"), Skeleton Arsonists' Spirit Costs now match Frost and Ice Mages from Level 5 onwards, making them more expensive while keeping their costs early-game the same.
