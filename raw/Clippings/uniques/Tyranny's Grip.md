**Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear** is a [Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear,** a brutal polearm that favors strength over speed, delivering devastating splash attacks that knock enemies back, while forbidding the use of ranged techniques. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The might of the Eternal Empire was formidable,  
> but rebels of every culture stood together as one.

### Path of Exile 2 Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (23-27) to (30-36)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.36-1.44)  
Weapon Range: 1.5

**Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear**features the following Modifiers:

(150–200)% increased Physical Damage  
(10–15)% reduced Attack Speed  
+(15–30) to Strength  
Strikes deal Splash Damage  
Knocks Back Enemies on Hit  
Cannot use Projectile Attacks

### PoE 2 Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear Requirements

To equip **Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 5
* Stats: 10 Dex

### How to get Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Tyranny's Grip Ironhead Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
