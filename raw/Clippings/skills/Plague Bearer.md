**Plague Bearer** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Plague Bearer** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Plague Bearer Effects

**Plague Bearer**provides the following effects:

* Stores 6% of Expected Poison Damage
* Stores up to 240 Poison Damage

Additional Effects From Quality:

* Stores (0–2)% of Expected Poison damage
* Poisons Enemies on Hit
* 80% less Stun buildup
* Stored Poison grants up to 600% more Area of Effect

### How to get Plague Bearer in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Plague Bearer** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 4** or higher.
* To use the Plague Bearer gem, it requires Character Level (1–90) and above, with (1–205) [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") or more.
