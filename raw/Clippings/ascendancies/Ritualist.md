**Ritualist** is one of the three unique [Huntress](/Huntress "Path of Exile 2 Huntress") [Ascendancy Classes](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") available in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Ritualist**  represent the darker sides of Azmeri traditions, harnessing the powers of animal sacrifices and plagues. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Ritualist Key Features

The Ritualist is a master of life and energy. Unleash blood magic spells while also gaining the capability of leeching life back from enemies.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [As the Whispers Demand](/As+the+Whispers+Demand "Path of Exile 2 As the Whispers Demand"): [Ritual Sacrifice](/Ritual+Sacrifice+(Ritualist) "Path of Exile 2 Ritual Sacrifice (Ritualist)") can be used on yourself to remove 20% of maximum Life to grant a random Monster Modifier. Only one modifier can be granted this way. Grants Skill: Ritual Sacrifice - Sacrifice the Corpse of a Rare Monster to grant you its Modifiers for a duration. Stolen Modifier Duration is 82-120 seconds.
* [Mind Phylacteries](/Mind+Phylacteries "Path of Exile 2 Mind Phylacteries"): Can instead consume 25% of maximum Mana to trigger Charms with insufficient charges.
* [Corrupted Lifeforce:](/Corrupted+Lifeforce "Path of Exile 2 Corrupted Lifeforce") Grants Skill: [Blood Boil](/Blood+Boil "Path of Exile 2 Blood Boil"). +15% more damage against enemies affected by Blood Boils
* [Intricate Sigils](/Intricate+Sigils "Path of Exile 2 Intricate Sigils"): +1 Charm Slot. 20% more Charm Charges gained.
* [Unfurled Finger](/Unfurled+Finger "Path of Exile 2 Unfurled Finger"): +1 Ring Slot.
* [Mystic Attunement](/Mystic+Attunement "Path of Exile 2 Mystic Attunement"): 25% increased bonuses gained from Equipped Rings and Amulets.
* [Wildwood Persistence](/Wildwood+Persistence "Path of Exile 2 Wildwood Persistence"): 10% increased Life Recovery rate per 5% missing Unreserved Life

### Path of Exile 2 Ritualist Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Ritualist**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Survivability:**Many builds can be tanking and offer great survivability.

**+ Flexibility**: Different builds can offer very different strengths, allowing for great adaptability.

### Cons

**-****Gear Dependent:** Many builds can be expensive and challenging to get the gear for before it is playable.

**- Slow League Start**: Many builds, due to requiring specific items, can struggle at the beginning of the league.

### Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) Ritualist Skills

##### Skill Gems and Customization:

To unlock new skills, [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") must be found and used. Activating a [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") opens the gemcutting menu, which allows players to level up existing skills or engrave new ones. Skill Gems retain their level, so newly engraved skills match the level of the gem, encouraging experimentation without penalty.

Since abilities are powered by Skill Gems, all classes have the freedom to use any gem they choose, offering limitless build possibilities.
