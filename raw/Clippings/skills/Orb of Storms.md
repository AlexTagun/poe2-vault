**Orb of Storms** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Orb of Storms** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Orb of Storms Effects

**Orb of Storms**provides the following effects:

* Deals (1–14) to (8–260) Lightning Damage
* Chains (3–5) times
* Fires a bolt every 4 seconds, and when you cast a Spell near the Orb
* Orb expires after firing 6 bolts
* Orb radius is 3.6 metres
* Orb duration is 12 seconds
* Limit 1 Orb of Storms

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Chains (0–3) times

### How to get Orb of Storms in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Orb of Storms**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 3**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 6, with at least 16 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").

### Path of Exile 2 Orb of Storms Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0  
  + Orb of Storms now triggers from casting any spell in its radius (previously only lightning spells, but incorrectly described as all lightning skills). It now creates a Lightning Infusion Remnant if all of its bolts are used up or its duration expires.
  + It now has a maximum of 6 bolts at all Gem levels (previously 12–29 at Gem levels 3–20).
  + It now has a base radius of 3.6 metres (previously 2.8 metres). It now deals 1–15 damage at gem level 3 (previously 4–12), scaling up to 14–260 damage at gem level 20 (previously 63–190).
  + Now fires a bolt every 4 seconds, and when you use a Lightning Skill near the Orb (previously every 3.4-2.55 seconds at Gem levels 3-20).
  + Orb of Storm's Orb duration is now 12 seconds (previously 10).
