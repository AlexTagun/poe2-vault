# Path of Exile 2 Lore

## Game Setting

The narrative takes place twenty years after the original game's events, when the Exile defeated Kitava, the Karui God of Corruption. Players control a new Exile encountering figures from that earlier era.

## World History & Mythology

### Lake of Kalandra
A mysterious trapped individual discovered an inexplicable lake surrounded by basalt pillars. Despite repeated escape attempts, she always returned with no memory of how. Over time, various figures visited her location, including ambitious mortals and the Order of the Djinn, who eventually abandoned her after learning the truth about the lake.

### Primeval Civilization
Evidence suggests an ancient society preceded both the Vaal and Lightless civilizations, with remains found in the Azurite Mines. Researchers theorize artifacts like the Golden Ibis may originate from this era.

### Divine Ascension
Wraeclast's gods were originally mortal beings who achieved divinity through Divinity power, fueled by follower faith. However, their human flaws persisted despite godhood.

## Innocence and Sin — Creation Myth

The foundational story describes two entities born to a woman. One was virtuous and obedient; the other was rebellious and received punishment. When the rebellious one was burned, their ashes spread corruption throughout the village, creating a giant entity from collective hatred. The virtuous one vowed to cleanse the world with purifying flames.

## The Beast (The Nightmare)

An ancient divine creature residing in mountains near Highgate serves as the source of Thaumaturgy. It transforms reality itself and can consume souls, trapping victims eternally. Figures like Queen Atziri and High Templar Dominus sought to control its power. Malachai succumbed to it and became its servant. Sin created the Beast to restrain the gods, though it still attracts those seeking to manipulate it.

## The Vaal Civilization

### Society & Culture
The Vaal became the dominant civilization in their era, pioneering the use of virtue gems. They conducted extensive human and blood sacrifices through rituals to power their contraptions, also relying heavily on slavery.

### Queen Atziri & Doryani
Queen Atziri was the final Vaal ruler, known for immense wealth and ruthlessness toward opponents. She patronized thaumaturgist Doryani and obsessively pursued immortality and eternal youth, inspired by the noble Zerphi who allegedly lived 168 years while maintaining a youthful appearance.

Atziri sent countless young people to be "processed" by Doryani in her quest for similar power. This obsession may have contributed to the civilization's collapse.

### Fall of the Vaal
Rather than external invasion, internal catastrophe caused the civilization's downfall. Around 400 BIC (Before Imperialus Conceptus), Doryani conducted his final experiment using the Doryani's Catalyst. Historical records suggest he sought "either immortality for all, or death for all." The experiment resulted in widespread death and ended Vaal civilization, though some survivors underwent strange transformations. References to "the Beast" suggest its involvement in the disaster.

## Path of Exile 2 Story Structure

### Act I
Player avoids execution by The Iron Count, helps citizens of the Island of Ogham resolve problems, tracks the Seed of Corruption, and ultimately defeats The Iron Count to access the Vastiri Desert.

### Act II
Player travels via Maraketh caravan through the Vastiri Desert searching for Faridun. The Ardura Caravan serves as the central hub. Multiple quests culminate in defeating Tor Gul the Defiler, granting access to the Ziggurat Encampment where Act III begins.
