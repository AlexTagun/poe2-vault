**Amazon** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the [Huntress](/Huntress "Path of Exile 2 Huntress") in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Amazons** are elite hunters who swore to protect the Azmeri Elders with precise strikes and fury. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

> You became a weapon of war so that others could live in peace.

### Path of Exile 2 Amazon Key Features

The Amazon is a master of life and energy. Unleash blood magic spells while also gaining the capability of leeching life back from enemies.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Elemental Surge](/Elemental+Infusion "Path of Exile 2 Elemental Infusion"): Grants Skill: [Elemental Surge (Attack)](/Infuse+Weapon+(Amazon) "Path of Exile 2 Infuse Weapon (Amazon)")
* [Predatory Instinct](/Predatory+Instinct "Path of Exile 2 Predatory Instinct"): Reveal Weaknesses against Rare and Unique enemies. 50% more damage against enemies with an Open Weakness
* [Mystic Harvest](/Mystic+Harvest "Path of Exile 2 Mystic Harvest"): Life leech recovers based on your Elemental damage as well as Physical damage
* [Critical Strike](/Critical+Scaling "Path of Exile 2 Critical Scaling"): Chance to hit with Attacks can exceed 100%. Gain additional Critical Hit Chance equal to 25% of excess chance to hit with Attacks
* [Azmeri Brew](/Azmeri+Brew "Path of Exile 2 Azmeri Brew"): Life Flasks also recover Mana. Mana Flasks also recover Life
* [Infused Avatar](/Infused+Avatar "Path of Exile 2 Infused Avatar"): 50% chance for Infusions to Infuse your Projectiles without being Consumed
* [Stalking Panther](/Stalking+Panther "Path of Exile 2 Stalking Panther"): Evasion Rating from Equipped Helmet, Gloves and Boots is doubled. Evasion Rating from Equipped Body Armour is halved
* [In for the Kill](/In+for+the+Kill "Path of Exile 2 In for the Kill"): 20% increased Movement Speed while an enemy with an Open Weakness is in your Presence. 40% increased Skill Speed while an enemy with an Open Weakness is in your Presence
* [Penetrate](/Penetrate "Path of Exile 2 Penetrate"): Attacks using your Weapons have Added Physical Damage equal to 25% of the Accuracy Rating on the Weapon
* [Surging Avatar](/Surging+Avatar "Path of Exile 2 Surging Avatar"): Upon consuming a charge, Trigger Elemental Surge

### Path of Exile 2 Amazon Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing an **Amazon** and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ High Damage:**Depending on the build, the Amazon can have excellent damage against a single-target, AoE groups, or both. This is especially accurate for endgame clears.

**+ Scaling**: The class scales very well through the game, particularly due to its high base critical hit.

### Cons

**-****Survivability:** Most builds for the Amazon suffer from poor survivability, which can be somewhat off-set by gear.

**- Trade-offs**: Due to its flexibility, the Amazon suffers notable trade-offs for its strengths. Such as sacrificing single-target damage for AoE and vice versa.

### Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) Amazon Skills

##### Skill Gems and Customization:

To unlock new skills, [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") must be found and used. Activating a [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") opens the gemcutting menu, which allows players to level up existing skills or engrave new ones. Skill Gems retain their level, so newly engraved skills match the level of the gem, encouraging experimentation without penalty.

Since abilities are powered by Skill Gems, all classes have the freedom to use any gem they choose, offering limitless build possibilities.
