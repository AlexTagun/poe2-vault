**Oracle** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Druid](/Druid "Path of Exile 2 Druid")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Oracle** has perfected the power of Foresight, using it in combat to gain a massive advantage by peering into the future to predict a monster's movements and even allocate passives from alternate timelines!. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Oracle Key Features

The Oracle is one of the two Ascendancy Classes that was introduced in Patch 0.4.0. This Ascendancy class has perfected the power of Foresight, using it in combat to gain a massive advantage by peering into the future to predict a monster's movements and even allocate passives from alternate timelines.

#### Oracle Unique Abilities

* [Entwined Realities](/Entwined+Realities "Path of Exile 2 Entwined Realities") - Allows you to allocate Keystone Passive Skills without being connected to present tree
* [The Unseen Path](/The+Unseen+Path "Path of Exile 2 The Unseen Path") - Unlocks [Paths Not Taken](/Paths+Not+Taken "Path of Exile 2 Paths Not Taken"), which reveals additional Passive nodes that are only available to the Oracle.
* [Harmony Within](/Harmony+Within "Path of Exile 2 Harmony Within") - Hit Damage is absorbed by Mana Pool if the current Mana is higher than the current Life
* [Converging Paths](/Converging+Paths "Path of Exile 2 Converging Paths") - Grants the Moment of Vulnerability skill, which lets you interact with the target's future state with the present to deal multiple damage instances in the present and future.
* [Fateful Vision](/Fateful+Vision "Path of Exile 2 Fateful Vision") - Grants the Align Fate skill, which increases your damage output when you match the predicted  skill in the Oracle's vision
* [Unnamed Heartwood](/Unnamed+Heartwood "Path of Exile 2 Unnamed Heartwood") - Increases maximum number of Totems that can be summoned
* [The Lesser Harm](/The+Lesser+Harm "Path of Exile 2 The Lesser Harm") - Turns the enemy's Critical Hit Chance and Damage of enemies hitting you to Unlucky
* [Forced Outcome](/Forced+Outcome "Path of Exile 2 Forced Outcome") - Grants Inevitable Critical Hits, which grants Critical Hits when you match the specific conditions that are seen in the Oracle's vision.

### Path of Exile 2 Oracle Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing an **Oracle**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Exclusive Oracle Nodes**: The Oracle has access to The Unseen Path, which gives access to almost 130 Passive Nodes that can only be used by the Oracle.

**+ Strong Damage**: While in Human form, the Oracle can see into the future and manipulate its surroundings to increase its damage.

### Cons

**- Resource Management**: The Oracle typically feeds on Mana, which requires finding Equipment or Gems that can provide decent Mana regeneration.

**- Low Health, Squishy**: The Oracle is the class that can inflict serious damage, at the cost of having low health. This can be extremely difficult to manage when you're faced with enemies or bosses that can take you out with a few attacks.

### Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) Oracle Skill Gems

##### Skill Gems and Customization:

To unlock new skills, [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") must be found and used. Activating a [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") opens the gemcutting menu, which allows players to level up existing skills or engrave new ones. Skill Gems retain their level, so newly engraved skills match the level of the gem, encouraging experimentation without penalty.

Since abilities are powered by Skill Gems, all classes have the freedom to use any gem they choose, offering limitless build possibilities.
