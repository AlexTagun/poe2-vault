**Banner Area** is a **[Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. This passive Skill provides all your Banner-related skills with an extra 20% Area of Effect. Players can unlock any skill from the Passive Skill Tree by spending a skill point on a skill connected to their starting point or already unlocked skills, Passive Skills that grant attributes, let you select which attribute you want to increase, allowing players to explore the tree in any direction.

### Information for Banner Area in Path of Exile 2

* Type: [Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")
* [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"): [Tactician](/Tactician "Path of Exile 2 Tactician")
* Requirements: **TBA**

### Path of Exile 2 Banner Area Effects

Banner Area provides the following effects:

* Banner Skills have 20% increased Area of Effect

### Path of Exile 2 Banner Area Notes and Tips

* Banner Skills generate Valour while carried, and can be placed to create powerful Buffing Areas which are more powerful the more Valour was consumed on placement.
