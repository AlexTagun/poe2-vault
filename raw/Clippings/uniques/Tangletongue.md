**Tangletongue Forked Spear** is a [Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Tangletongue Forked Spear,** a cunning weapon that enhances critical precision, bolsters intellect, forks critical strikes, and twists mana cost into life cost for sustained pressure. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "In the hands of Orbala, it made a god bleed." - Wranga, tale-woman of the Wahida akhara

### Path of Exile 2 Tangletongue Forked Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Tangletongue Forked Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (27-3) to (68-74)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.55  
Weapon Range: 1.5

**Tangletongue Forked Spear**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Spear Throw  
Adds (14-18) to (30-36) Physical Damage  
+(5-8)% to Critical Hit Chance  
+(15-25) to Intelligence  
Bifuricates Critical Hits  
10% of Skill Mana Costs Converted to Life Costs

### PoE 2 Tangletongue Forked Spear Requirements

To equip **Tangletongue Forked** **Spear**,players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 26
* Stats: 17 Str, 38 Dex

### How to get Tangletongue Forked Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Tangletongue Forked Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.

* Tangletongue Forked Spear effect "Adds (14–18) to (30–36) Physical Damage, +(10–20)% to Critical Hit Chance, +(15–25) to Intelligence, Forks Critical Hits, 10% of Skill Mana Costs Converted to Life Costs" replaced with "Adds (14-18) to (30-36) Physical Damage, +(5-8)% to Critical Hit Chance, +(15-25) to Intelligence, Bifuricates Critical Hits, 10% of Skill Mana Costs Converted to Life Costs" in [Patch 0.3.0](/Patch+Notes#0.3.0 "Path of Exile 2 Patch Notes#0.3.0").
