**The Blood Thorn Wrapped Quarterstaff** is a **[Unique Quarterstaff](/Unique+Quarterstaves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Quarterstaves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **The Blood Thorn Wrapped Quarterstaff** is a quarterstaff that inflicts bleeding, boosts strength, and deals physical thorn damage on hit. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Touch not the thorn for only blood and pain await.

### Path of Exile 2 The Blood Thorn Wrapped Quarterstaff Stats & Modifiers

**The Blood Thorn Wrapped Quarterstaff**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (15-19) to (28-30)  
Critical Hit Chance: 10%  
Attacks per Second: 1.4  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**The Blood Thorn Wrapped Quarterstaff**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (8-12) to (16-18) Physical Damage  
+(10-15) to Strength  
Causes Bleeding on Hit  
(4-5) to (8-10) Physical Thorns damage
