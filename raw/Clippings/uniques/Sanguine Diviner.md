**Sanguine Diviner Bone Wand** is a **[Unique Wand](/Unique+Wands "Path of Exile 2 Unique Wands")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Sanguine Diviner Bone Wand** is a wand that boosts spell damage, converts mana cost to life cost, and has a chance to cause bleeding on hit. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> One way or another it will find what it seeks.

### Path of Exile 2 Sanguine Diviner Bone Wand Stats & Modifiers

**Sanguine Diviner Bone Wand**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Level 1 Bone Blast  
(80-100)% increased Spell Damage  
Gain (10-15) Life per Enemy Killed  
25% chance to inflict Bleeding on Hit  
25% of Spell Mana Cost Converted to Life Cost

### PoE 2 Sanguine Diviner Bone Wand Requirements

To equip **Sanguine Diviner Bone Wand**, players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 2, 7 INT

### How to get Sanguine Diviner Bone Wand in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Sanguine Diviner Bone Wand** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
