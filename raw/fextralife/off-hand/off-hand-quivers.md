# Quivers

Quivers are a type of Off-Hand Equipment in Path of Exile 2 designed exclusively for bow-wielding characters. They offer a variety of modifiers that enhance ranged attacks, improve critical strike potential, or add unique mechanics to bow skills.

## All Quivers

| Name | Implicit Mod | Level Req |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Broadhead Quiver | Adds 1 to 3 Physical Damage to Attacks | — |
| Fire Quiver | Adds 3 to 5 Fire Damage to Attacks | 8 |
| Sacral Quiver | Gain (2–3) Life per Enemy Hit with Attacks | 16 |
| Two-Point Quiver | (20–30)% increased Accuracy Rating | 24 |
| Blunt Quiver | (20–30)% reduced Enemy Stun Threshold | 33 |
| Serrated Quiver | Attacks have (10–15)% chance to cause Bleeding | 44 |
| Primed Quiver | (7–10)% increased Attack Speed | 51 |
| Penetrating Quiver | 100% chance to Pierce an Enemy | 55 |
| Visceral Quiver | (20–30)% increased Critical Hit Chance for Attacks | 65 |
| Volant Quiver | (20–30)% increased Arrow Speed | 61 |
| Toxic Quiver | (10–15)% chance to Poison on Hit with Attacks | 39 |

## Notes

- Quivers are usable only with bows
- No attribute requirements beyond level (no Str/Dex/Int)
- Each quiver type provides a distinct gameplay-focused implicit modifier
- No defensive stats (no armor, evasion, energy shield, block chance)
