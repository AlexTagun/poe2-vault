**Stormweaver** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Sorceress](/Sorceress "Path of Exile 2 Sorceress")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Stormweaver** is a master of the elements capable of unleashing devastating elemental attacks and effects. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Stormweaver Key Features

The Storm Weaver is a master of elemental magic, specializing in harnessing the destructive power of storms.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Constant Gale](/Constant+Gale "Path of Exile 2 Constant Gale"): You have Arcane Surge
* [Force of Will](/Force+of+Will "Path of Exile 2 Force of Will"): 1% increased Effect of Arcane Surge on you per 25 maximum Mana
* [Heavy Snows](/Heavy+Snows "Path of Exile 2 Heavy Snows"): Targets can be affected by two of your Chills at the same time. Your Chills can Slow targets by up to a maximum of 35%. 25% less Magnitude of Chill you inflict
* [Multiplying Squalls](/Multiplying+Squalls "Path of Exile 2 Multiplying Squalls"): +2 to Limit for Elemental Skills
* [Refracted Infusion](/Refracted+Infusion "Path of Exile 2 Refracted Infusion"): Gains another Elemental Infusion when collecting Elemental Infusions
* [Shaper of Storms](/Shaper+of+Storms "Path of Exile 2 Shaper of Storms"): All Damage from Hits Contributes to Shock Chance
* [Shaper of Winter](/Shaper+of+Winter "Path of Exile 2 Shaper of Winter"): All Damage from Hits Contributes to Chill Magnitude
* [Storm's Recollection](/Storm's+Recollection "Path of Exile 2 Storm's Recollection"): Remnants you create reappear once, 3 seconds after being collected
* [Strike Twice](/Strike+Twice "Path of Exile 2 Strike Twice"): Targets can be affected by two of your Shocks at the same time. 25% less Magnitude of Shock you inflict
* [Tempest Caller](/Tempest+Caller "Path of Exile 2 Tempest Caller"): Grants Skill: [Elemental Storm](/Elemental+Storm "Path of Exile 2 Elemental Storm"). Trigger Elemental Storm on Critical Hit with Spells

### Path of Exile 2 Stormweaver Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Stormweaver**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Burst Damage:**A **Stormweaver** has good burst damage, allowing them to easily dispose of bosses or skipping boss mechanics.

**+ Ailment Application**: **Stormweavers** can easily apply ailments to enemies, allowing for faster damage or putting distance between the player and the enemy.

### Cons

**-****Survivability:** The **Stormweaver** is a glass-cannon ascendancy, so defensive options are very limited or non-existent.

**- Mobility**: With the **Stormweaver** having bad defensive options, they also have limited mobility.
