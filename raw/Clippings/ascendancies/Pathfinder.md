**Pathfinder**is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Ranger](/Ranger "Path of Exile 2 Ranger")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Pathfinder** specializes in poison mechanics and flask usage, utilizing throwable concoctions to devastate enemies. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

> There are venoms and virtues aplenty in the wilds, if you know where to look.

### Path of Exile 2 Pathfinder Key Features

An expert in flasks and poison. As a **Pathfinder**, you can choose several throwable items that allow spending of flask charges to throw out explosive bottles that can deal various types of damage such as bleed, poison, and more.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Brew Concoction](/Brew+Concoction "Path of Exile 2 Brew Concoction"): Grants the following skills
  + [Bleeding Concoction](/Bleeding+Concoction+(Attack) "Path of Exile 2 Bleeding Concoction (Attack)")
  + [Explosive Concoction](/Explosive+Concoction+(Attack) "Path of Exile 2 Explosive Concoction (Attack)")
  + [Shattering Concoction](/Shattering+Concoction+(Attack) "Path of Exile 2 Shattering Concoction (Attack)")
  + [Fulminating Concoction](/Fulminating+Concoction+(Attack) "Path of Exile 2 Fulminating Concoction (Attack)")
  + [Acidic Concoction](/Acidic+Concoction+(Attack) "Path of Exile 2 Acidic Concoction (Attack)")
* [Connected Chemistry](/Connected+Chemistry "Path of Exile 2 Connected Chemistry"): 50% more Flask Charges gained
* [Enduring Elixirs](/Enduring+Elixirs "Path of Exile 2 Enduring Elixirs"): Life Flask Effects are not removed when Unreserved Life is Filled. Life Flask Effects do not queue.
* [Path Seeker](/Path+Seeker "Path of Exile 2 Path Seeker"): Allows access to the Path of the Warrior or Path of the Sorceress.
* [Overwhelming Toxicity](/Overwhelming+Toxicity "Path of Exile 2 Overwhelming Toxicity"): Double the number of Poisons you can inflict on Enemies. 35% less Poison Duration.
* [Relentless Pursuit](/Relentless+Pursuit "Path of Exile 2 Relentless Pursuit"): Your speed is unaffected by Slows.
* [Running Assault](/Running+Assault "Path of Exile 2 Running Assault"): 50% less Movement Penalty when using Skills while moving and Cannot be Heavy Stunned while Sprinting
* [Sustainable Practices](/Sustainable+Practices "Path of Exile 2 Sustainable Practices"): 50% of Evasion Rating also grants Elemental Damage reduction
* [Traveller's Wisdom](/Traveller's+Wisdom "Path of Exile 2 Traveller's Wisdom"): Grants Attribute Passive Skills offer additional options.

### Path of Exile 2 Pathfinder Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Pathfinder**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Survivability:**A **Pathfinder** can deal damage from afar, allowing them to stay in a safer position compared to other classes and ascendancies.

**+ Flask Charges**: The **Pathfinder** ascendancy will have ways of replenishing flask charges, making it easier to survive in battle.

### Cons

**-****Flask Dependent:** Some **Pathfinder** builds are flask dependent, so finding flasks with good rolls and effects can be tedious.

### Pathfinder Notes and Tips for Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Pathfinder** notes and tips go here.
* As of [Patch 0.1.0e](/Patch+Notes#15 "Path of Exile 2 Patch Notes#15"), Concoction skills from the Pathfinder Ascendancy Class now consume 3 charges from Mana Flasks (from 5). Their damage has been increased and deal approximately 66% more Damage when the skill is Level 20. In addition the base added damage has been increased by at least 50% for all Concoction skills, with Poisonous and Bleeding Concoction receiving greater damage buffs. Fulminating Concoction now has a Critical Strike Chance of 8% (from 6%), Shattering Concoction now has a Critical Strike Chance of 11% (from 7%), Explosive Concoction now has 100% more chance to Ignite Enemies and 100% more magnitude of Ignites inflicted, Fulminating Concoction now has 100% more chance to inflict Shock and Shattering Concoction now has 100% more Freeze Buildup.
