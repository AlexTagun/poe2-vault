**Invoker**is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Monk](/Monk "Path of Exile 2 Monk")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Invoker** harnesses the elements, creating waves of destruction and powerful elemental effects. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Invoker Key Features

The Invoker can trigger waves of elemental power and gains access to the Meditate ability to double its energy shield. As an Invoker, you can specialize in certain elements to unleash powerful effects such as freezing enemies or even shocking them.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [And I Shall Rage](/And+I+Shall+Rage "Path of Exile 2 And I Shall Rage"): Grants Skill: [Unbound Avatar](/Unbound+Avatar "Path of Exile 2 Unbound Avatar")
* [And Protect me from Harm](/And+Protect+me+from+Harm "Path of Exile 2 And Protect me from Harm"): Evasion Rating also grants Physical Damage reduction. 35% less Evasion Rating
* [And Scatter them to the Winds](/And+Scatter+them+to+the+Winds "Path of Exile 2 And Scatter them to the Winds"): Grants Skill: [Elemental Expression](/Elemental+Expression "Path of Exile 2 Elemental Expression"). Trigger Elemental Expression on Melee Critical Hit
* [Faith is a Choice](/Faith+is+a+Choice "Path of Exile 2 Faith is a Choice"): Grants Skill: [Meditate](/Meditate "Path of Exile 2 Meditate")
* [I am the Blizzard](/I+am+the+Blizzard "Path of Exile 2 I am the Blizzard"): Gain 10% of Damage as Extra Cold Damage. On Freezing Enemies create Chilled Ground
* [I am the Thunder](/I+am+the+Thunder "Path of Exile 2 I am the Thunder"): Gain 10% of Damage as Extra Lightning Damage. 25% chance on Shocking Enemies to created Shocked Ground
* [Lead me through Grace](/Lead+me+through+Grace "Path of Exile 2 Lead me through Grace"): Gain 1 Spirit for every 8 Energy Shield on equipped Body Armour. Gain 1 Spirit for every 20 Evasion Rating on equipped Body Armour. Cannot gain Spirit from Equipment
* [Sunder my Enemies](/Sunder+my+Enemies "Path of Exile 2 Sunder my Enemies"): Critical Hits ignore non-negative Enemy Monster Elemental Resistances
* [The Soul Springs Eternal](/The+Soul+Springs+Eternal "Path of Exile 2 The Soul Springs Eternal"): Meta Skills gain 35% more Energy and Meta Skills have 50% increased Reservation Efficiency.

### Path of Exile 2 Invoker Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing an **Invoker**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Elemental Damage:****Invoker** builds can focus on Elemental Damage, as there are ascendancy nodes that focus on building elemental damage

**+ Crowd Control**: **Invoker**builds can deal with groups of enemies with elemental ailments

### Cons

**-****Elemental Ailments:** Elemental Ailments are required when playing as an Invoker, limiting build choices

**- Life and Mana Sustain**: It can be difficult to sustain life and mana with the Invoker ascendancy outside of Life and Mana flasks
