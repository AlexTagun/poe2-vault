**Scavenged Plating** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Scavenged Plating** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Scavenged Plating Effects

**Scavenged Plating**provides the following effects:

* Scavenged Plating duration is 8 seconds
* 4% more Armour per Scavenged Plating
* Maximum 10 Scavenged Plating
* (2–43) to (2–64) Thorns per Scavenged Plating

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Scavenged Plating duration is (0–2) seconds

### How to get Scavenged Plating in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Scavenged Plating**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 4**.
* To use the Scavenged Plating gem, it requires Character Level 10 and above, with 25 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Scavenged Plating Notes and Tips

* Scavenged Plating now has 4% more Armour per Scavenged Plating at all Gem levels (previously 3-5% at Gem levels 4-20) in Patch 0.3.0
* Scavenged Plating now has 6 to 8 Thorns per Scavenged Plating at Gem level 4 (previously 8 to 12), scaling up to 43 to 64 at Gem level 20 (previously 60 to 90) in Patch 0.3.0
* Quality now provides Scavenged Plating duration is +0-2 seconds (previously 0-2% more Armour per Scavenged Plating) in Patch 0.3.0
