# Shattering Spite

**Base:** Attack
**Class:** AoE , Physical , Lightning , Fire

## Base Stats

Level: (1 — 20)
Attack Damage: (125 — 453)% of base

**Requires: Spears**

## Modifiers

- Converts 40 % of Physical damage to Fire damage
- Converts 40 % of Physical damage to Lightning damage
- +0.35 seconds to Total Attack Time
- Cannot inflict Bloodstone Lances
- Life Leech recovers based on this Skill's Elemental damage as well as Physical damage
- Explosion radius is 1.5 metres
- Explosions deal 20 % more Damage per Bloodstone Lance
- Explosions with at least 5 Bloodstone Lances have 5 % increased Area of Effect per Bloodstone Lance
- Leech 10 % of Physical Attack Damage as Life from Explosions with at least 10 Bloodstone Lances
- Life Leech from Explosions with at least 15 Bloodstone Lances is instant
- Bloodstone Lances are shattered based on 200 % of your Presence area
