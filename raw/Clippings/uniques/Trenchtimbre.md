**Trenchtimbre Spiked Club** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Trenchtimbre Spiked Club,** a club tuned to the rhythm of summoned allies, increasing both your and your minions’ attack tempo while empowering minion abilities with enhanced damage and precision. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> Ezomyte loyalty was not given to distant leaders.  
> It was earned by comrades in arms.

### Path of Exile 2 Trenchtimbre Spiked Club Stats & Modifiers

**Trenchtimbre Spiked Club**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (28-30) to (46-49)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.74-1.89)  
Weapon Range: 1.1

**Trenchtimbre Spiked Club**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (13–15) to (22–25) Physical Damage  
(20–30)% increased Attack Speed  
+(1–2) to Level of all Minion Skills  
Increases and Reductions to Minion Attack Speed also affect you

### PoE 2 Trenchtimbre Spiked Club Requirements

To equip **Trenchtimbre Spiked Club** players must meet the following requirements:

* Requires: Level 16
* Stats: 38 Str

### How to get Trenchtimbre Spiked Club in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Trenchtimbre Spiked Club** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
