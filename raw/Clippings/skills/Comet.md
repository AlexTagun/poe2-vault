**Comet** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Comet** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Comet Effects

**Comet**provides the following effects:

Comet

* Deals (24–829) to (36–1243) Cold Damage
* +1 second to Total Cast Time
* Impact radius is 2.8 metres

Fire-Infused

* +1 second to Total Cast Time
* Impact radius is 3.4 metres
* Deals (16–553) to (24–829) Fire Damage
* Deals (16–553) to (24–829) Cold Damage

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–10)% chance to repeat

### How to get Comet in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Comet**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 11**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 41, with at least 95 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").

### Path of Exile 2 Comet Notes and Tips

* Comet now deals 223–335 Cold Damage at Gem level 11 (previously 205-307), scaling up to 829-1243 damage at Gem level 20 (previously 708-1062) in Patch 0.3.0
* Comet can now be Fire-infused to deal extra fire damage and gain base radius in Patch 0.3.0
