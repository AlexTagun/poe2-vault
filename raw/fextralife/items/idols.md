# Idols

Idols are socketable items that provide modifiers to items, offering a range of bonuses such as stat increases, bonus damage, improved resistances, and others. Once socketed, they cannot be removed but can be replaced. Stack size: 10.

## Animal Spirit Idols (Drop Level 35)

| Name | Equipment Type | Effect |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| Bear | Helmets/Sceptres | Area of Effect +8% or ally physical damage |
| Boar | Gloves/Sceptres | Rage on hit or ally life regeneration |
| Cat | Boots/Sceptres | Critical damage reduction or ally crit chance |
| Owl | Boots/Sceptres | Cooldown recovery or ally cast speed |
| Ox | Boots/Sceptres | Presence area or ally elemental resistances |
| Primate | Helmets/Sceptres | Minion life or ally damage increase |
| Snake | Gloves/Sceptres | Curse magnitude or ally attack speed |
| Stag | Helmets/Sceptres | Exposure effect or ally accuracy |
| Wolf | Gloves/Sceptres | Bleeding magnitude or ally crit damage |

Source: "Haunted by the [Animal Name]" – Spirit-Possessed monsters at level 35+.
