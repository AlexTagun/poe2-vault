**Fresh Clip I** is a **[Support Gem](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. Support Gems can be placed into the Support Gem Socket, and used to further tune the effects of Skill Gems.

### Information for Fresh Clip I in Path of Exile 2

* Type: Support
* Category: Fresh Clip
* Tier: 2
* Support Requirements: +5 STR

### Path of Exile 2 Fresh Clip I Effects

**Fresh Clip I**provides the following effects:

Supported Crossbow Skills deal 1% more Damage for each Bolt Reloaded by that Skill in the past 6 seconds

### Fresh Clip I Supported Skill Gems in PoE 2

**Fresh Clip I** can be used to support the following [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"):

* [Armour Piercing Rounds](/Armour+Piercing+Rounds "Path of Exile 2 Armour Piercing Rounds")

* [Explosive Shot](/Explosive+Shot "Path of Exile 2 Explosive Shot")
* [Fragmentation Rounds](/Fragmentation+Rounds "Path of Exile 2 Fragmentation Rounds")

* [Glacial Bolt](/Glacial+Bolt "Path of Exile 2 Glacial Bolt")
* [Hailstorm Rounds](/Hailstorm+Rounds "Path of Exile 2 Hailstorm Rounds")
* [High Velocity Rounds](/High+Velocity+Rounds "Path of Exile 2 High Velocity Rounds")
* [Ice Shards](/Ice+Shards "Path of Exile 2 Ice Shards")

* [Permafrost Bolts](/Permafrost+Bolts "Path of Exile 2 Permafrost Bolts")

* [Stormblast Bolts](/Stormblast+Bolts "Path of Exile 2 Stormblast Bolts")

### How to get Fresh Clip I in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Fresh Clip I** can be crafted by engraving the support skill to an [Uncut Support Skill Gem](/Uncut+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Uncut Skill Gems").
* This support gem requires +5 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength") to add it to the supported Skill Gem socket.
