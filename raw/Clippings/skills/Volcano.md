**Volcano** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Volcano** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Volcano Effects

**Volcano**provides the following effects:

Eruption

* Deals (4—121) to (6—181) Fire Damage
* Modifiers to cast speed also affect this skill's cost
* Fires Projectiles every 0.5 seconds
* Channelling ends when reaching 4 Stages
* Eruption radius is 1.2 metres
* Volcano duration is 8 seconds
* Limit 1 Volcano
* 150% more Eruption damage per additional Stage

Projectiles

* Impact radius is 0.6 metres
* Fires Projectiles every 0.5 seconds, or when Slammed
* Initial eruption fires 4 additional
* Projectiles per additional Stage
* Volcano is destroyed after 10 Slams
* Deals (3—92) to (5—138) Fire Damage
* Fires (3—5) Projectiles

Hidden

* Fires Projectiles every 0.5 seconds
* Eruption radius is 0.8 metres
* Volcano duration is 8 seconds
* 150% more Eruption damage per additional Stage
* Deals (3—92) to (5—138) Fire Damage
* Fires (3—5) Projectiles

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Eruption radius is (0—0.2) metres
