**Berserk** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Berserk** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Berserk Effects

**Berserk**provides the following effects:

* Lose 0.1% of maximum Life per Rage per second while not losing Rage
* Grants (40–59)% increased Rage effect

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Grants (0–10)% increased Rage effect

### How to get Berserk in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Berserk**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 14**.
* To use the Berserk gem, it requires Character Level 58 and above, with 133 [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Berserk Notes and Tips

* Berserk now grants 53-59% increased Rage Effect at Gem levels 14-20 in Patch 0.3.0
* Berserk no longer Grants 0.2% of Physical Attack Leeched as Life per Rage in Patch 0.3.0
