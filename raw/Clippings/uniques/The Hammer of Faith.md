**The Hammer of Faith Giant Maul** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The Hammer of Faith Giant Maul is a two-handed mace that delivers powerful physical damage, increases attack speed, and always deals critical hits to heavily stunned enemies. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The secret order endured by publicly praying however the Templars demanded. One day, justice would fall upon them...

### Path of Exile 2 The Hammer of Faith Giant Maul Stats & Modifiers

**The Hammer of Faith Giant Maul** has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (384-432) to (576-648)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5.  
Attacks per Second: 0.99  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**The Hammer of Faith Giant****Maul**features the following Modifiers:

(300-350)% increased Physical Damage  
10% reduced attack speed  
+10% to all elemental resistances  
50% increased mana regeneration rate  
Every 10 seconds, gain a random non-damaging Shrine buff for 20 seconds

### PoE 2 The Hammer of Faith Giant Maul Requirements

To equip **The Hammer of Faith Giant Maul**players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 65, 114 Str

### How to get The Hammer of Faith Giant Maul in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Trephina Forge Maul** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
