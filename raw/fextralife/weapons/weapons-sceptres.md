# Sceptres

Sceptres merge Strength and Intelligence, enhancing both melee damage and elemental effects. They suit hybrid builds that wield physical force alongside magical spells. Sceptres provide Spirit and grant a built-in skill gem.

## Affixes

### Prefix Mods
- Maximum Mana bonuses
- Ally damage boosts (Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning)
- Ally damage and accuracy increases
- Spirit and Mana increases

### Suffix Mods
- # to Strength/Intelligence
- Ally elemental resistances
- #% reduced Attribute Requirements
- # to Level of all Minion Skills
- Ally Life Regeneration
- Mana Regeneration Rate
- Ally Attack/Cast Speed
- Ally Critical Hit Chance/Damage
- Presence Area of Effect
- Minion Maximum Life increases
