**Splinterheart Recurve Bow** is a **[Unique Bow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Splinterheart Recurve Bow** is a bow with increased physical damage, projectile speed, and the ability to split projectiles toward multiple targets. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The forests of the Vastiri held many secrets mystical and dark. Men learned not to wander, lest they return with a strange new purpose.

### Path of Exile 2 Splinterheart Recurve Bow Stats & Modifiers

**Splinterheart Recurve Bow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (33-39) to (68-81)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.1

**Splinterheart Recurve Bow**features the following Modifiers:

(120-160)% increased Physical Damage  
+(50-70)% to Accuracy Rating  
(20-30)% increased Projectile Speed  
Projectiles Split towards +2 targets

### PoE 2 Splinterheart Recurve Bow Requirements

To equip **Splinterheart Recurve Bow** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 16, 31 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity")

### How to get Splinterheart Recurve Bow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Splinterheart Recurve Bow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
