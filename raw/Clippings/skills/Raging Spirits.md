**Raging Spirits** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Raging Spirits** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Raging Spirits Effects

**Raging Spirits**provides the following effects:

* Minions from this skill convert 70% of Physical Damage to Fire Damage
* Limit 10 Summoned Raging Spirits
* Fire Area Spells summon a Spirit per 1.5 metres of radius
* Projectiles from Fire Spells summon a Spirit on hit
* Maximum 5 Spirits summoned per Spell cast

Additional Effects from Quality:

* Limit (0-1) Summoned Raging Spirits

### How to get Raging Spirits in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Raging Spirits** can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level (1–20)**.
* To use the Raging Spirits gem, it requires Character Level (1–90) and above, with (1–205) [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence") or more.
