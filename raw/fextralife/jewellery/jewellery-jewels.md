# Jewels

Jewels in Path of Exile 2 are special items placed into Jewel Sockets on the Passive Skill Tree to provide various bonuses. Some jewels alter the behavior of nearby Passive Skills within a specific radius.

## Jewel Categories

### Regular Jewels

| Name | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Timeless Jewel | Alters passives in radius; special timeless mechanic |
| Emerald | Standard jewel — Dexterity-aligned |
| Diamond | Standard jewel — generic/intelligence-aligned |
| Ruby | Standard jewel — Strength-aligned |
| Sapphire | Standard jewel — Intelligence-aligned |

### Time-Lost Jewels

| Name | Radius |
|------|--------|
| Time-Lost Emerald | 1000 |
| Time-Lost Diamond | 1300 |
| Time-Lost Ruby | 1000 |
| Time-Lost Sapphire | 1000 |

## Notes

- Jewels socket into the Passive Skill Tree, not equipment
- Time-Lost Jewels have larger radius and affect more passive nodes
- Grand Spectrum jewels reward stacking multiple copies (set bonus mechanic)
- Timeless Jewels fundamentally alter passive skills within their radius
- Detailed per-jewel mods are randomized (Magic/Rare jewels roll affixes like other items)
