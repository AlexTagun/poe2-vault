# Unique Armor

Unique Armor in Path of Exile 2 features predefined fixed modifiers tailored to specific builds and playstyles. Designed to improve survivability and defenses while providing unique effects that enhance a character's build.

## Acquisition

- Random enemy drops
- Orb of Chance on a Normal item of the appropriate base type
- Trading via the Official Path of Exile 2 Trade Site

## Notable Unique Helmets

- **Alpha's Howl**
- **Goldrim**
- **The Three Dragons**
- **Crown of Thorns**
- **Sandstorm Visage** (note: may appear miscategorized in wiki as belt — it is a helmet)

## Notable Unique Body Armour

- **Kaom's Heart** — Massive life bonus, no sockets
- **Belly of the Beast** — Life and resistances
- **Queen of the Forest** — Evasion scaling movement speed
- **Tabula Rasa** — 6 white linked sockets, no stats

## Notable Unique Gloves

- **Facebreaker** — Unarmed attack scaling
- **Maligaro's Virtuosity** — Critical strike focused
- **Atziri's Acuity** — Vaal Pact on critical strikes
- **Hand of Wisdom and Action** — Intelligence stacking

## Notable Unique Boots

- **Wanderlust** — Freeze immunity, movement speed
- **Darkray Vectors** — Frenzy charge movement speed
- **Atziri's Step** — Spell dodge
- **Wake of Destruction** — Lightning damage on walk

## Notes

- Level requirements range from 1 to 107
- Unique items cover all armor slots and defense types
- Some uniques have build-defining mechanics not achievable through rare items
