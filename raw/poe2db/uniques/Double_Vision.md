# Double Vision

**Base:** Runeforged Dyad Crossbow
**Class:** Crossbow

## Base Stats

Physical Damage: 58-185
Critical Hit Chance: 10.00%
Attacks per Second: 1.60
Reload Time: 0.88

**Requires: Level 78 , 48 Str , 48 Dex**

## Implicit

- Grants Skill: Level 18 Gemini Surge
- Loads an additional bolt

## Modifiers

- Adds 21 to 37 Physical Damage
- +5% to Critical Hit Chance
- 25% increased Reload Speed
- When you reload, triggers Gemini Surge to alternately gain 6 Cold Surges or 6 Fire Surges
