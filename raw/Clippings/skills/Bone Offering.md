**Bone Offering** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Bone Offering** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Bone Offering Effects

**Bone Offering**provides the following effects:

Buff

* Limit 1 Bone Offering Spike
* Spike duration is 8 seconds
* Minions take (20–25)% reduced damage from Hits while shielded
* Absorbs first Hit dealing over 25% of Minion's maximum life

Explosion

* Deals (256-917) to (385-1375) Physical Damage
* 2.4 Explosion Radius

Additional Effects From Quality:

Shielded Minions tale (0-30)% less damage for 1 second after shield lost

### How to get Bone Offering in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Bone Offering**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 11**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 41, with at least 95 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").

### Path of Exile 2 Bone Offering Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0
  + Bone Offering's spike duration is now 8 seconds (previously 5)
  + Explosion radius is now 2.4 metres (previously 2).
  + The explosion now deals 256-917 to 385-1375 Physical Damage at gem levels 11-20 (previously 205-734 to 308-1101)
  + Bone Offering's buff now has Minions take 20-25% reduced Damage from Hits while shielded, and also now has Absorbs first Hit dealing over 25% of Minion's Maximum Life.
  + Quality now provides Shielded Minions take 0-30% less Damage for 1 second after Shield is lost, instead of 0-40% more Physical Damage on the Explosion.
* As of [Patch 0.1.0e](/Patch+Notes#15 "Path of Exile 2 Patch Notes#15"), Bone Offering Explosions now have 15% Critical Strike Chance (from 13%).
