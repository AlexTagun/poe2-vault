**Brynhand's Mark Wooden Club** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Brynhand's Mark Wooden Club** is a one-handed mace that enhances physical damage and increases strength and accuracy, with a chance for double stun buildup. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The mark of the smith was widely known.

### Path of Exile 2 Brynhand's Mark Wooden Club Stats & Modifiers

**Brynhand's Mark Wooden Club**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (16-20) to (26-30)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.16  
Weapon Range: 1.1

**Brynhand's Mark Wooden Club**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (10-14) to (16-20) Physical Damage  
+(30-50) to Accuracy Rating  
20% reduces Attack Speed  
+(10-20) to Strength  
Causes Double Stun Buildup
