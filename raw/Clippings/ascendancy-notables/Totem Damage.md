**Totem Damage** is a **[Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. This passive Skill provides the Totems you summon with extra damage. Players can unlock any skill from the Passive Skill Tree by spending a skill point on a skill connected to their starting point or already unlocked skills, Passive Skills that grant attributes, let you select which attribute you want to increase, allowing players to explore the tree in any direction.

### Information for Totem Damage in Path of Exile 2

* Type: [Passive Skill](/Passive+Skills "Path of Exile 2 Passive Skills")
* [Class](/Classes "Path of Exile 2 Classes"): [Tactician](/Tactician "Path of Exile 2 Tactician")
* Requirements: **TBA**

### Path of Exile 2 Totem Damage Effects

Totem Damage provides the following effects:

* 20% Increased Totem Damage.

### Path of Exile 2 Totem Damage Notes and Tips for

* Totems are allied constructs which use skills for you. Totems are not Minions and their Skills benefit from your stats, though they have their own defensive stats and can be damaged or killed. Totem Limit is between different types of Totem by default.
