**Witchhunter** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Mercenary](/Mercenary "Path of Exile 2 Mercenary")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Witchhunter** is an assassin who combines sorcery with ruthless combat techniques. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Witchhunter Key Features

The Witch Hunter is capable of using wards to increase their defenses towards elemental hits and even increasing their rate of evasion. With rituals, the Witch Hunter can cause enemies to explode and apply strong passive effects such as dealing more damage against bosses.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Ceremonial Ablution](/Ceremonial+Ablution "Path of Exile 2 Ceremonial Ablution"): Sorcery Ward Barrier protects users against Phyiscal and Chaos damage
* [Judge Jury and Executioner](/Judge+Jury+and+Executioner "Path of Exile 2 Judge Jury and Executioner"): Decimating Strike: Hits against Full Life Enemies remove between 5% and 30% of Life, before the damage of the Hit is applied.
* [No Mercy](/No+Mercy "Path of Exile 2 No Mercy"): Deal up to 40% more Damage to Enemies based on their missing Concentration
* [Obsessive Rituals](/Obsessive+Rituals "Path of Exile 2 Obsessive Rituals"): Grants Skill: [Sorcery Ward](/Sorcery+Ward "Path of Exile 2 Sorcery Ward"). 35% less Armour and Evasion Rating
* [Pitiless Killer](/Pitiless+Killer "Path of Exile 2 Pitiless Killer"): Culling Strike: Cuuling Strikes kill normal enemies if their life is at 30% or below. Magic enemies are instead killed at 20%, Rare at 10% and Unique at 5%. These thresholds are checked before the damage of the Hit is applied.
* [Weapon Master](/Weapon+Master "Path of Exile 2 Weapon Master"): 100 Passive Skill Points become Weapon Set Skill Points. Grants 6% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate.
* [Witchbane](/Witchbane "Path of Exile 2 Witchbane"): Enemies have Maximum Concentration equal to 30% of their Maximum Life. Break enemy Concentration on Hit equal to 100% of Damage Dealt. Enemies regain 10% of Concentration every second if they haven't lost Concentration in the past 5 seconds
* [Zealous Inquisition](/Zealous+Inquisition "Path of Exile 2 Zealous Inquisition"): 10% chance for Enemies you Kill to Explode, dealing 100% of their maximum Life as Physical Damage. Chance is doubled against Undead and Demons

### Path of Exile 2 Witchhunter Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Witchhunter**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ High Single-Target Damage:**High single-target damage allows for a faster clear speed when facing Rare enemies or Unique bosses.

**+ Crowd Control**: The Witchhunter can clear grouped-up enemies with spells with massive AoE damage.

### Cons

**-****Gear Dependent:** Witchhunter builds are gear dependent, which means that a Witchhunter can struggle in the late game with improper gearing.

**- Limited AoE spell options**: Even though the Witchhunter excels at single-target damage, there are limited options when it comes to AoE spells.
