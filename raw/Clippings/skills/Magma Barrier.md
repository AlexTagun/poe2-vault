**Magma Barrier** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Magma Barrier** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Magma Barrier Effects

**Magma Barrier**provides the following effects:

Buff

* Fully imbues Shield in 8 seconds
* Grants 25% increased Block chance

Magma Spray

* Critical Hit Chance: 5.00%
* Attack Damage: (170–694)% of base
* 6 to 8 Added Fire Damage per 15 Armour on Shield
* 6 to 8 Added Fire Damage per 15 Evasion on Shield
* (4–103) to (6–155) Base Off Hand Fire Damage
* Magma Spray radius is 2.8 metres

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Grants (0–5)% increased Block chance

### How to get Magma Barrier in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Magma Barrier**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 4**.
* To use the Magma Barrier gem, it requires Character Level 10 and above, with (4-157) [Strength](/Strength "Path of Exile 2 Strength") or more.

### Path of Exile 2 Magma Barrier Notes and Tips

* As of Patch 0.3.0
  + Magma Barrier now passively increases your chance to Block while active. Over time, it charges up with lava. While fully charged, your next Block with your Shield raised will spray lava in front of you and grant you an Endurance Charge.
  + Now grants 25% increased Block Chance.
  + Magma Spray now has 6 to 8 Added Fire Damage per 15 Evasion on Shield, and has a radius of 2.8 metres.
  + Magma Spray deals 12-103 to 20-155 Base Off Hand Fire Damage at Gem levels 3-20 (previously 23-177 to 34-265), and 227%-684% Attack Damage (previously 172-471%).
  + Quality now provides +0-5% increased Block Chance (previously Raising your shield infuses it with magma for 0-0.2 seconds).
