# The Barrow Dweller

**Base:** Rogue Armour
**Class:** Body Armours

## Base Stats

Armour : (84-106)
Evasion Rating : (68-86)
Base Movement Speed: -0.04

**Requires: Level 11 , 13 Str , 13 Dex**

## Modifiers

- (60 — 100) % increased Armour and Evasion
- (-20 — -10) % to Fire Resistance
- +50 % to Cold Resistance
- Damage of Enemies Hitting you is Unlucky while you are on Low Life
- 50 % chance to Avoid Death from Hits

## Flavour

> In the mists they dwell, forever hungry, forever cold.
