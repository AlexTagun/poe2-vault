# Rings

Rings are a type of Jewellery in Path of Exile 2. They offer a variety of enhancements such as boosts to attributes, resistances, and specific abilities. Players can equip up to two rings at a time.

## All Rings

| Name | Implicit Mod |
|------|-------------|
| Golden Hoop | +(8–12) to All Attributes |
| Iron Ring | Adds 1 to 4 Physical Damage to Attacks |
| Lazuli Ring | +(20–30) to Maximum Mana |
| Ruby Ring | +(20–30)% to Fire Resistance |
| Sapphire Ring | +(20–30)% to Cold Resistance |
| Topaz Ring | +(20–30)% to Lightning Resistance |
| Amethyst Ring | +(7–13)% to Chaos Resistance |
| Emerald Ring | +(120–160) to Accuracy Rating |
| Pearl Ring | (7–10)% increased Cast Speed |
| Prismatic Ring | +(7–10)% to All Elemental Resistances |
| Gold Ring | (6–15)% increased Rarity of Items Found |
| Unset Ring | Grants 1 additional Skill Slot |

## Notes

- No attribute requirements or level requirements listed on base types
- Unset Ring is unique in granting a skill slot — valuable for builds needing extra active skills
- Resistance rings (Ruby/Sapphire/Topaz/Amethyst/Prismatic) are staple gearing options for reaching resistance caps
- Iron Ring is the only ring providing offensive damage (physical attack bonus)
- Pearl Ring provides Cast Speed — relevant for spellcasters
