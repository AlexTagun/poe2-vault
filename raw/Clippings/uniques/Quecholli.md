**Quecholli Crumbling Maul** is a **[Unique Two Hand Mace](/Unique+Two+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique Two Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Quecholli Crumbling Maul** is a two-handed mace with improved physical damage and it can recover 30 life to the character upon killing enemies. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> "The finest prosperity grows from the direst carnage. Such is the nature of progress." - Doryani of the Vaal

### Path of Exile 2 Quecholli Crumbling Maul Stats & Modifiers

**Quecholli Crumbling Maul**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (124-155) to (150-188)  
Critical Hit Chance: 8%  
Attacks per Second: 1.1  
Weapon Range: 1.3

**Quecholli Crumbling Maul**features the following Modifiers:

Causes Enemies to Explode on Critical Kill, for 10% of their Life as Physical Damage  
(100-150)% Increased Physical Damage  
+(10-15) to all Attributes  
Gain 30 Life per Enemy Killed  
This Weapon's Critical Hit Chance is 100%  
Critical Hits do not deal Extra Damage

### PoE 2 Quecholli Crumbling Maul Requirements

To equip **Quecholli Crumbling Maul** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 38, 68 STR

### How to get Quecholli Crumbling Maul in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Quecholli Crumbling Maul** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
