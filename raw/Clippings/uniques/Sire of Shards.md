**Sire of Shards Chiming Staff** is a **[Unique Staff](/Unique+Staves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Staves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Sire of Shards Chiming Staff,** a spellcaster’s staff that boosts elemental resistance, improves casting speed, expands visibility, and alters spell behavior to unleash multiple projectiles in a circular pattern. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> That which was broken may yet break.

### Path of Exile 2 Sire of Shards Chiming Staff Stats & Modifiers

**Sire of Shards Chiming Staff**has the following Stats:

Grants Skill: Level 7 Sigil of Power

**Sire of Shards Chiming Staff**features the following Modifiers:

(80–120)% increased Spell Damage  
(10–20)% increased Cast Speed  
+(5–10)% to all Elemental Resistances  
20% increased Light Radius  
Spells fire 4 additional Projectiles  
Spells fire Projectiles in a circle

### PoE 2 Sire of Shards Chiming Staff Requirements

To equip **Sire of Shards Chiming Staff** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 25, 46 INT

### How to get Sire of Shards Chiming Staff in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Sire of Shards Chiming Staff** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
