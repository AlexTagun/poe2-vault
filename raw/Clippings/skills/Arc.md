**Arc** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Arc** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Arc Effects

**Arc**provides the following effects:

* Deals (1–20) to (13–386) Lightning Damage
* Chains (6–9) times
* 50% more Magnitude of Shock inflicted
* Chains 6 additional times when Lightning Infused
* 200% more damage when Lightning Infused

Additional Effects From Quality

* Chains (0-2) Times

### How to get Arc in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Arc**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 5**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 14, with at least 34 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence").

### Path of Exile 2 Arc Notes and Tips

* Arc is now a projectile in Patch 0.3.0
* Arc has 50% more shock magnitude in Patch 0.3.0
* Arc can now be lightning-infused to chain additional times and deal more damage in Patch 0.3.0
* At gem level 5, Arc deals 2-39 damage(previously 5-31), scaling up to 20-386 (previously 49-277) at gem level 20 in Patch 0.3.0
* Quality now provides Chains +0-2 additional times instead of 0-2% more damage  in Patch 0.3.0
