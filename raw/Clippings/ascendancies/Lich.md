**Lich**is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Witch](/Witch "Path of Exile 2 Witch")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Lich**harnesses the power of curses and chaos, allowing you to curse even Hexproof enemies. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Lich Key Features

The Lich can summon the power of Unholy Might, granting 30% of all damage as extra Chaos Damage. When Cursed, enemies slain by the Lich also have a 33% chance to explode. That effect applies not only to the Lich but also Allies in your Presence, including Minions.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Rupture the Soul](/Rupture+the+Soul "Path of Exile 2 Rupture the Soul"): Cursed Enemies killed by you or Allies in your Presence have a 33% chance to Explode, dealing a quarter of their maximum Life as Chaos Damage.
* [Necromantic Conduit](/Necromantic+Conduit "Path of Exile 2 Necromantic Conduit"): You and Allies in your Presence have Unholy Might while you are not on Low Mana. Lose 5% of Mana per second.
* [Eldritch Empowerment](/Eldritch+Empowerment "Path of Exile 2 Eldritch Empowerment"): Non-Channeling Spells cost an additional 3% of maximum Energy Shield. Non-Channeling Spells deal 30% more damage. Spending Energy Shield does not interrupt Recharge.
* [Incessant Cacophony](/Incessant+Cacophony "Path of Exile 2 Incessant Cacophony"): Your Curses have infinite Duration
* [Blackened Heart](/Blackened+Heart "Path of Exile 2 Blackened Heart"): 4% increased Magnitude of Unholy Might buffs you grant per 100 Maximum Mana.
* [Crystalline Phylactery](/Crystalline+Phylactery "Path of Exile 2 Crystalline Phylactery"): Can Socket one non-Unique Basic Jewel. 100% increased Effect of Socketed Jewels. 50% more Mana Cost if you have no Energy Shield.
* [Soulless Form](/Soulless+Form "Path of Exile 2 Soulless Form"): 10% of Damage taken bypasses Energy Shield. No inherent Mana Regeneration. Regenerate Mana equal to 6% of maximum Life per second.
* [Eternal Life](/Eternal+Life "Path of Exile 2 Eternal Life"): Your Life cannot change while you have Energy Shield.
* [Price of Power](/Price+of+Power "Path of Exile 2 Price of Power"): Non-Channeling Spells consume Power Charges to deal 25% more Damage.

### Path of Exile 2 Lich Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Lich**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Minion Gameplay:**The **Lich** has the option to utilize minions in their build, allowing for a different gameplay compared to other classes and ascendancies.

**+ Spellcasting**: The **Lich**ascendancy has the tools to excel as a spellcaster, specializing in chaos damage and curses.

### Cons

**-** Restricted Jewel Usage**:** The **Lich**is granted a Jewel Socket that doubles the bonuses and effects of your equipped Jewel. It does, however, restrict you to Basic Jewels only.

**- Resource Management**: The **Lich** can encounter Mana sustain issues as Mana costs are increased when the player is out of Energy Shield.
