**Chainsting Hunting Spear** is a [Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears") and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Chainsting Hunting Spear,** a fast and accurate weapon that increases projectile travel speed, applies pressure through rapid strikes, and builds up pinning effects with each hit. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The Sacred Hunt ends with mercy.

### Path of Exile 2 Chainsting Hunting Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Chainsting Hunting Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (23-27) to (39-45)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.705  
Weapon Range: 1.5

**Chainsting Hunting Spear**features the following Modifiers:

Grants Skill: Spear Throw  
(15-25)% chance to Maim on Hit  
Adds (13–17) to (22–28) Physical Damage  
+(100–150) to Accuracy Rating  
10% increased Attack Speed  
All Damage from Hits with this Weapon Contributes to Pin Buildup  
(20–30)% increased Projectile Speed with this Weapon

### PoE 2 Chainsting Hunting Spear Requirements

To equip **Chainsting Hunting Spear** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 10
* Stats: 9 STR, 17 DEX

### How to get Chainsting Hunting Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Chainsting Hunting Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
