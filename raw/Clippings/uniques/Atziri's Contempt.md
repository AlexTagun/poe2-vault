**Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear** is a **[Unique Spear](/Unique+Spears "Path of Exile 2 Unique Spears")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear** grants Spear Throw and Shattering Sprite, as well as increased physical damage and critical hits. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> My people? I'm not doing this for them. They belong to me. They want this for me. Their sacrifice is a gift they give out of love and adoration... I deserve it. I am their Queen.

### Path of Exile 2 Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear Stats & Modifiers

**Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (60-66) to (178-196)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: (1.71-1.8)  
Weapon Range: 1.5  
Lightning Damage: 1 to (193-207)

**Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear**features the following Modifiers:

(100-120)% increased Physical Damage  
Adds (83-97) to (123-153) Fire Damage  
Adds 1 to (193-207) Lightning Damage  
(10-16)% increased Attack Speed  
(60-80)% increased Presence Area of Effect  
Spear Skills inflict a Bloodstone Lance on Hit, up to a maximum of 30 on each target

### PoE 2 Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear Requirements

To equip **Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 72, 46 Str, 115 Dex

### How to get Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Atziri's Contempt Pronged Spear** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
