# Blessed Bonds

**Base:** Linen Wraps
**Class:** Gloves

## Base Stats

Evasion Rating : (78-118)
Energy Shield : (39-59)

**Requires: Level 56 , 13 Dex , 13 Int**

## Modifiers

- +(60 — 100) to Evasion Rating
- +(30 — 50) to maximum Energy Shield
- Gain (25 — 35) Mana per enemy killed
- Inflict Cold Exposure on Igniting an Enemy
- Inflict Fire Exposure on Shocking an Enemy
- Inflict Lightning Exposure on Critical Hit

## Flavour

> "We survived the endless winter. We endure the long summer. One day, spring will return the rains."
