**Meditate** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Meditate** is a channelling spell skill acquired from the [Faith is a Choice](/Faith+is+a+Choice "Path of Exile 2 Faith is a Choice") ability of the [Invoker](/Invoker "Path of Exile 2 Invoker") ascendancy class, using this skill recharges the Energy Shield of the character, channelling Meditate is interrupted if the character takes damage or their Energy Shield is doubled to its normal maximum.

### Path of Exile 2 Meditate Effects

**Meditate**provides the following effects:

* (24-100)% more Energy Shield Recharge Rate

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–10)% more Energy Shield Recharge Rate

### How to get Meditate in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Meditate** can be acquired upon unlocking the [Faith is a Choice](/Faith+is+a+Choice "Path of Exile 2 Faith is a Choice") unique node from the skill tree of the [Invoker](/Invoker "Path of Exile 2 Invoker") ascendancy.

### Path of Exile 2 Meditate Notes and Tips

* Meditate now provides 24-100% more Energy Shield Recharge Rate at Gem levels 1-20 (previously 0-28%) in Patch 0.3.0
* Meditate, which is granted by the Faith is a Choice Ascendancy Passive Skill, now has 0–28% more Energy Shield Recharge Rate at Gem levels 1–20 (previously 40–78%).
  + In addition, Quality now grants 0–10% more Energy Shield Recharge Rate (previously 0–15%).
* The Meditate Skill granted by the Faith is a Choice Notable Passive Skill now has a Cooldown of 7-5.1 seconds at Gem levels 1-20 (previously 10-8.1 seconds). Meditate now provides 24-100% more Energy Shield Recharge Rate at Gem levels 1-20 (previously 0-28%).
