**Frozen Locus** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Frozen Locus** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Frozen Locus Effects

**Frozen Locus**provides the following effects:

Crystal

* Maximum 1 Ice Crystal
* Ice Crystal duration is 10 seconds
* Ice Crystal has (43–1364) maximum Life

Chilled Ground

* Attack Damage: (150–612)%
* Converts 100% of Physical Damage to Cold Damage
* Maximum 1 Ice Crystal
* Does not Hit, but Chills enemies as though Hitting them
* Gains 30% more Radius per second, up to a maximum of 180%
* Ice Crystal duration is 10 seconds
* Chilled Ground radius is 1.2 metres

Explosion

* Attack Damage: (150–612)%
* Converts 100% of Physical Damage to Cold Damage
* 50% more Freeze Buildup
* Maximum 1 Ice Crystal
* 80% less damage if destroyed within 0.5 seconds by something other than you
* Ice Crystal duration is 10 seconds
* Explosion radius is 3.4 metres

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–20)% more Magnitude of Chill inflicted

### How to get Frozen Locus in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Frozen Locus**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Spirit Gem Level 7**.
* To use the Frozen Locus gem, it requires Character Level 22, with at least 30 [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity") and 30 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Path of Exile 2 Intelligence"), and must be used with a [Quarterstaff](/Quarterstaves "Path of Exile 2 Quarterstaves").

### Path of Exile 2 Frozen Locus Notes and Tips

* Frozen Locus' Ice Crystal now deals 80% less damage in the first 0.5 seconds of its duration in Patch 0.3.0
