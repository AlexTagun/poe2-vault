**Sculpted Suffering Warpick** is a **[Unique One Hand Mace](/Unique+One+Hand+Maces "Path of Exile 2 Unique One Hand Maces")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Sculpted Suffering Warpick,** a finely crafted weapon that pierces deeply into enemy armor, delivering swift and precise blows with enhanced critical force and the ability to shatter foes whose armor has been completely broken. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The Abyssals were created, not born,  
> and every moment in the light was agony.

### Path of Exile 2 Sculpted Suffering Warpick Stats & Modifiers

**Sculpted Suffering Warpick**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (39-44) to (49-55)  
Critical Hit Chance: 7%  
Attacks per Second: (1.6-1.67)  
Weapon Range: 1.1

**Sculpted Suffering Warpick**features the following Modifiers:

+(5-10)% to Critical Damage Bonus  
Adds (21–26) to (25–31) Physical Damage  
(10–15)% increased Attack Speed  
Breaks Armour equal to 40% of damage from Hits with this weapon  
Fully Armour Broken enemies you kill with Hits Shatter

### PoE 2 Sculpted Suffering Warpick Requirements

To equip **Sculpted Suffering Warpick** players must meet the following requirements:

* Requires: Level 22
* Stats: 41 Str

### How to get Sculpted Suffering Warpick in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Sculpted Suffering Warpick** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
