# Guiding Palm of the Mind

**Base:** Shrine Sceptre
**Class:** Sceptres

## Base Stats

Spirit: 100

**Requires: Level 65 , 17 Str , 38 Int**

## Implicit

- Grants Skill: Level 8 Purity of Lightning

## Modifiers

- Gain 25 % of Damage as Extra Lightning Damage
- Allies in your Presence deal 1 to (56 — 70) added Attack Lightning Damage
- 50 % of your Base Life Regeneration is granted to Allies in your Presence
- +(20 — 30) to Dexterity
- 25 % increased Light Radius
- Grants effect of Guided Tempest Shrine

## Flavour

> "Deep in thought, you would tremble the very air before you. Wreathed in light, you nurtured them all. And yet... Your nature became you." The Dreamer mused with aching heart, as remnants of forking tendrils burst forth.
