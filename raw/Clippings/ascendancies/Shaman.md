**Shaman** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Druid](/Druid "Path of Exile 2 Druid")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Shaman** channels nature’s fury, harnessing Rage to empower his Spells and becoming a walking natural disaster raining down Meteors, Lightning, and Ice. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

### Path of Exile 2 Shaman Key Features

The Shaman channels nature’s fury, harnessing Rage to empower his Spells and becoming a walking natural disaster raining down Meteors, Lightning, and Ice. This Ascendancy is ideal for players who prefer maximizing their Hybrid gameplay, with Rage resource management taking the center stage. This type of gameplay focuses on maximizing spells that are amplified through the Rage-related skills.

#### Shaman Unique Abilities

* [Turning of the Seasons](/Turning+of+the+Seasons "Path of Exile 2 Turning of the Seasons") - Inflict Exposure to enemies within the Shaman's presence. Grants 10% of Damage as Extra Damage of a Random Element
* [Bringer of the Apocalypse](/Bringer+of+the+Apocalypse "Path of Exile 2 Bringer of the Apocalypse") - Grants the Apocalypse Skill, which calls down powerful storms upon dealing enough Elemental Damage
* [Druidic Champion](/Druidic+Champion "Path of Exile 2 Druidic Champion") - Every 2 points of Rage grants 1% more Spell Damage. An effective ability for hybrid gameplay
* [Reactive Growth](/Reactive+Growth "Path of Exile 2 Reactive Growth") - Reduces Elemental Damage by 10% and adapts to the highest Elemental Damage type of each hit received. You will also get 10% reduced damage on each Elemental Damage type per matching adaptations (Maximum 3)
* [Avatar of Evolution](/Avatar+of+Evolution "Path of Exile 2 Avatar of Evolution") - Transforms Physical Damage taken into Cold/Fire/Lightning Damage
* [Furious Wellspring](/Furious+Wellspring "Path of Exile 2 Furious Wellspring") - Prevents inherent loss of rage, while regenerating maximum rage per second, even in human form. Increases and Decreases to mana regeneration rate also apply to Rage regeneration rate. This skill works well with the Bear form.
* [Sacred Flow](/Sacred+Flow "Path of Exile 2 Sacred Flow") - Adds +40 Spirit for each empty Charm slot
* [Wisdom of the Magi](/Wisdom+of+the+Magi "Path of Exile 2 Wisdom of the Magi") - Gain the benefits of Bonded modifiers on Runes and Idols. This expands the exclusive Rune and Idol modifiers only the Shaman can use.

### Path of Exile 2 Shaman Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Shaman**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Combined Melee and Spellcasting:**The Shaman class in POE 2 focuses on the combined playstyle of Spellcasting, with aggressive melee combat. The Shaman can cast powerful spells while in Human form to create setups for the beast form to unleash its rage.

### Cons

**-****Animal form limitations:** The preferred animal form for the Shaman is the Bear because of the Rage resource. This animal form can lose a significant amount of mobility without rage, which can reduce the Shaman's effectiveness in combat if the skills are not utilized well.

**- Difficult to master**: The Shaman class combines aggressive gameplay with a mix of precise spellcasting. This gameplay requires a high level of understanding of skill synergies. Because of this, it will take players a significant amount of time to practice and learn how to utilize all of the Shaman's skills effectively.

**- Rage Management**: The Shaman typically feeds on Rage, which requires obtaining Equipment or Gems that can provide decent Rage regeneration.

### Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) Shaman Skill Gems

##### Skill Gems and Customization:

To unlock new skills, [Skill Gems](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") must be found and used. Activating a [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems") opens the gemcutting menu, which allows players to level up existing skills or engrave new ones. Skill Gems retain their level, so newly engraved skills match the level of the gem, encouraging experimentation without penalty.

Since abilities are powered by Skill Gems, all classes have the freedom to use any gem they choose, offering limitless build possibilities.
