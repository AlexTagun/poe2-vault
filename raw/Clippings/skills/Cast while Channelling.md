**Cast while Channelling** is a **[Buff Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Cast while Channelling** can be used by finding Uncut Spirit Gems and engraving a [Persistent Buff Skill Gem](/Persistent+Buff+Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Persistent Buff Skill Gems"), these reactive skills can further unlock unique effects, improve the damage, increase one's defenses, and more.

### Path of Exile 2 Cast while Channelling Effects

**Cast while Channelling**provides the following effects:

Gains 60 Energy per second while you are Channelling  
Loses 25 Energy per second while you are not Channelling  
Loses all Energy when Reservation becomes inactive  
Triggers all Socketed Spells and loses all Energy on reaching maximum Energy  
Has 10 maximum Energy per 0.1 seconds of base cast time of Socketed Spells
