**Smith of Kitava** is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Warrior](/Warrior "Path of Exile 2 Warrior")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Smith of Kitava** focuses on buffing his own weapons and equipment, gaining bonuses to his attack and defense. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. In Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

>  You hunger for power, and by force of arms, you will take it.

### Path of Exile 2 Smith of Kitava Key Features

The **Smith of Kitava** utilizes his anvil to enhance his weapons and equipment, imbuing them with powerful bonuses.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Dedication To Kitava](/Dedication+To+Kitava "Path of Exile 2 Dedication To Kitava"): Body Armour grants Armour also applies to Chaos Damage taken from Hits
* [Internal Layer](/Internal+Layer "Path of Exile 2 Internal Layer"): Body Armour grants Hits against you have 100% reduced Critical Damage Bonus
* [Leather Bindings](/Leather+Bindings "Path of Exile 2 Leather Bindings"): Body Armour grants regenerate 3% of maximum Life per second
* [Molten Symbol](/Molten+Symbol "Path of Exile 2 Molten Symbol"): Body Armour grants 25% of Physical Damage from Hits taken as Fire Damage
* [Flowing Metal](/Flowing+Metal "Path of Exile 2 Flowing Metal"): Body Armour grants 50% of Armour to also apply to Elemental Damage from Hits
* [Heatproofing](/Heatproofing "Path of Exile 2 Heatproofing"): Body Armour grants Unaffected by all Damaging Ailments
* [Support Straps](/Support+Straps "Path of Exile 2 Support Straps"): Body Armour grants 20% increased Strength
* [Kitavan Engraving](/Kitavan+Engraving "Path of Exile 2 Kitavan Engraving"): Body Armour grants 15% increased maximum Life
* [Kitavan Imprint](/Kitavan+Imprint "Path of Exile 2 Kitavan Imprint"): Body Armour grants 60% increased Glory Generation
* [Spiked Plates](/Spiked+Plates "Path of Exile 2 Spiked Plates"): Body Armour grants 100% increased Thorns Damage
* [Tribute to Utula](/Tribute+to+Utula "Path of Exile 2 Tribute to Utula"): Body Armour grants 30% increased Spirit
* [Tantalum Alloy](/Tantalum+Alloy "Path of Exile 2 Tantalum Alloy"): Body Armour grants +75% to Fire Resistance
* [Smith's Masterwork](/Smith's+Masterwork "Path of Exile 2 Smith's Masterwork"): Can only use Normal grade Body Armor, while granting access to the following skills: Dedication to Kitava, Heatproofing, Tantalum Alloy, Kitavan Engraving, Support Straps, Leather Bindings, Internal Layer, Flowing Metal, Molten Symbol.
* [Heat of the Forge](/Heat+of+the+Forge "Path of Exile 2 Heat of the Forge"): Grants Skill: [Fire Spell on Hit](/Fire+Spell+on+Hit "Path of Exile 2 Fire Spell on Hit")
* [Coal Stoker](/Coal+Stoker "Path of Exile 2 Coal Stoker"): Modifiers to Fire Resistance also grant Cold and Lightning Resistance at 50% of their value
* [Forged in Flame](/Forged+in+Flame "Path of Exile 2 Forged in Flame"): Modifiers to Maximum Fire Resistance also grant Maximum Cold and Lightning Resistance
* [Against the Anvil](/Against+the+Anvil "Path of Exile 2 Against the Anvil"): Grants Skill: [Temper Weapon](/Temper+Weapon "Path of Exile 2 Temper Weapon").
* [Living Weapon](/Living+Weapon "Path of Exile 2 Living Weapon"): Grants Skill: [Manifest Weapon](/Manifest+Weapon "Path of Exile 2 Manifest Weapon").

### Path of Exile 2 Smith of Kitava Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Smith of Kitava**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Weapon Enhancements:****Exploit**the elemental damages you are already giving and multiply them, increasing your damage output.

**+ Armor Enhancements**: The Smith of Kitava Ascendancy can grant you access to a miriad of Armour-based skills that provide several good resistances.

### Cons

**-****Stationary Anvil:** A **Smith of Kitava** needs to take some time to manipulate his Anvil, pining him down for a few seconds at a time.

**- Locked-In to Normal Armour**: In order to access the Armour skills for the Ascendancy, you need to be locked-in to a Normal grade Armour, affecting your physical defense.
