**Spark** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Spark** can be used by finding its [Skill Gem](/Skill+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Skill Gems"), and equipping it in one of the 9 Skill Slots. Active Skill Gems can be further tuned by using [Support Gems](/Support+Gems "Path of Exile 2 Support Gems") in the 5 Support Gem Sockets.

### Path of Exile 2 Spark Effects

**Spark**provides the following effects:

Base

* Deals (1–11) to (10–216) Lightning Damage
* Fires (4–9) Projectiles
* Projectile duration is 2 seconds
* Projectiles fired at the same time can Hit the same target no more than once every 0.66 seconds

Cold - Infused

* Critical Hit Chance: 11.00%
* Supported Skills fire Projectiles in a circle
* Projectile duration is 2 seconds
* +0.5 seconds to Cast Time
* Projectiles fired at the same time can Hit the same target no more than once every 0.66 seconds
* Deals (7–182) to (11–273) Cold Damage
* Deals (1–11) to (10–216) Lightning Damage
* Fires (12–16) Projectiles in a circle

Additional Effects From Quality:  
(0–30)% increased Projectile Speed

### How to get Spark in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Spark**can be crafted by engraving it to an **Uncut Skill Gem Level 1**.
* To use this skill gem, it requires Character Level 1.

### Path of Exile 2 Spark Notes and Tips

* In Patch 0.3.0 Spark can now be Cold-infused to fire a large number of icy sparks in a circle, dealing both cold and lightning damage.
