**Death's Harp Dualstring Bow** is a **[Unique Bow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Death's Harp Dualstring Bow** is a bow that boosts critical damage, fires additional arrows, and restores life and mana on kill. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> The mournful music of the strings,The creaking arch, the arrow sings.A choking cry, a rattled breath,The Reaper's Song, the Harp of Death.

### Path of Exile 2 Death's Harp Dualstring Bow Stats & Modifiers

**Death's Harp Dualstring Bow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: 19-35  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.15

**Death's Harp Dualstring Bow**features the following Modifiers:

+50% Surpassing chance to fire an additional Arrow  
+(20-25)% to Critical Damage Bonus  
Bow Attacks fire 3 additional arrows  
Gain (20-30) Life per enemy killed  
Gain (12-18) Mana per enemy killed

### PoE 2 Death's Harp Dualstring Bow Requirements

To equip **Death's Harp Dualstring Bow** players must meet the following requirements:

Requires: Level 28, 52 [Dex](/Dexterity "Path of Exile 2 Dexterity")

### How to get Death's Harp Dualstring Bow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Death's Harp Dualstring Bow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.

### Path of Exile 2 Death's Harp Dualstring Bow Notes and Tips for

* Death's Harp Dualstring Bow effect "(50-70)% to Critical Damage Bonus, Bow Attacks fire 3 additional Arrows, Gain (20-30) Life per Enemy Killed, Gain (12-18) Mana per Enemy Killed" replaced with "Bow Attacks fire an additional Arrow, +(20-25)% to Critical Damage Bonus, Bow Attacks fire 3 additional arrows, Gain (20-30) Life per enemy killed, Gain (12-18) Mana per enemy killed" in Patch 0.3.0.
