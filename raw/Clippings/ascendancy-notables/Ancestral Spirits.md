**Ancestral Spirits** is a **[Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Ancestral Spirits** is a skill acquired from the [Answered Call](/Answered+Call "Path of Exile 2 Answered Call") ability of the [Warbringer](/Warbringer "Path of Exile 2 Warbringer") ascendancy class, it is a skill that can summon an Ancestral Spirit Minion that can aid the character in combat.

### Path of Exile 2 Ancestral Spirits Effects

**Ancestral Spirits**provides the following effects:

* Modifiers to Totem Life also affect Minions from this Skill.
* Minions from this skill have 1% increased Attack Speed per 3 of your Dexterity
* Minions from this skill have 1% increased Damage per 3 of your Strength.

Additional Effects From Quality:  
Minions have (0–20)% more Maximum Life.

### How to get Ancestral Spirits in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

**Ancestral Spirits** can be acquired upon unlocking the [Answered Call](/Answered+Call "Path of Exile 2 Answered Call") unique node from the skill tree of the [Warbringer](/Warbringer "Path of Exile 2 Warbringer") ascendancy.

### Path of Exile 2 Ancestral Spirits Notes and Tips

* Ancestrals summoned now more consistently hits close-up enemies with the second part of its slam attack in Patch 0.4.0
* Ancestral Spirit Skill now provides +1 Maximum number of Summoned Totems in patch 0.3.0.
* Ancestral Spirits Skill now appears faster and can now start using skills sooner in patch 0.3.0.
* Ancestral Spirits Skill effect "Minions from this Skill have 1% increased Accuracy Rating per 3 of your Dexterity" has been changed to "Minions from this Skill have 1% increased Attack Speed per 3 of your Dexterity" in patch 0.3.0.
* Ancestral Jadecaster has had its Spell Casting range increased. The Pinning Jade Storm has had its Area of Effect increased, and the delay between it forming and exploding has been reduced in Patch 0.3.0.
* Ancestral Warhorn's horn buff and horn slam have had their Area of Effect and Duration increased in Patch 0.3.0.
* Ancestral Spirit Turtle's Aura Area of Effect increased in Patch 0.3.0.
* The Patch 0.2.0 update modified the effects of the **Ancestral Spirits**.
* The skill now includes:
  + "Minions from this Skill have 1% increased Accuracy Rating per 3 of your Dexterity."
  + "Minions from this Skill have 1% increased Damage per 3 of your Strength."
* Changes to minions from this skill include:
  + Ancestral Jadecaster's Pinning Skill cooldown reduced to 10 seconds (previously 15).
  + Ancestral Jade Hulk now has 300% more Stun Buildup (previously 200%).
  + The Warcaller's Bellow buff from Ancestral Warhorn now grants 10 Rage on Hit (previously 5) and 20% increased Skill Speed (previously 10%).
  + The Paua Shell Aura from the Ancestral Spirit Turtle now grants nearby Allies 80% increased Defences (previously 50%) and 40% increased Block Chance (previously 25%).
