# Stats & Attributes — Fextralife

## Core Attributes

**Strength**
- Requirement: Equipment granting Armour, melee-aligned weapons and skills
- Bonus per point: +2 Life
- Note: Does not grant damage except where specifically stated

**Dexterity**
- Requirement: Equipment granting Evasion Rating, ranged weapons and skills
- Bonus per point: +5 Accuracy Rating
- Note: Does not grant damage except where specifically stated

**Intelligence**
- Requirement: Equipment granting Energy Shield, spell-aligned weapons and skills
- Bonus per point: +2 Mana
- Note: Does not grant damage except where specifically stated

## Defensive Stats

**Life**
Lost when taking damage. Character dies at 0 Life.

**Energy Shield**
Protects Life by absorbing damage instead. Recharges rapidly if not recently damaged. Chaos damage removes twice as much Energy Shield. Bleeding and Poison bypass Energy Shield to damage Life directly.

**Mana**
Required for most skills. Regenerates slowly over time.

**Spirit**
Reserved by ongoing skills (buffs, minions). Player must have sufficient Spirit available to use these skills.

**Armour**
Reduces Physical Damage from attacks and spells. Maximum damage reduction: 90%. Provides no benefit against other damage types.

**Evasion**
Grants chance to avoid enemy strikes and projectiles. Success depends on attacker's Accuracy versus player's Evasion.

**Block**
Chance to block incoming strikes and projectiles, negating damage. Some large/dangerous enemy attacks cannot be blocked.

## Resistances

Four total resistances exist. Maximum resistance capped at 75% (can reach 90%). Negative values increase damage taken. Resistances are lowered during campaign progression.

- **Fire Resistance** — Reduces fire damage taken
- **Cold Resistance** — Reduces cold damage taken
- **Lightning Resistance** — Reduces lightning damage taken
- **Chaos Resistance** — Reduces chaos damage taken

## Permanent Upgrades (по актам кампании)

**Act 1 Upgrades:**
- 4 Passive Skill points
- +10% Cold Resistance
- +30 Maximum Spirit
- +20 Maximum Life
- Salvaging Bench access

**Act 2 Upgrades:**
- 4 Passive Skill points
- +10% Lightning Resistance
- Choice between: 30% increased Charm charges OR 15% increased Mana Recovery from Flasks

## Attribute Base Types (экипировка)

- **Strength equipment** — Armour
- **Dexterity equipment** — Evasion
- **Intelligence equipment** — Energy Shield
- **Hybrid types** (например, Strength-Dexterity) — обе соответствующих защиты (Armour + Evasion)

Items with attribute requirements cannot be equipped without sufficient attributes.

## Skill Tags Reference

- **Ailment** — Debuffs enemies (elemental, non-damaging, или damaging)
- **Ammunition** — Replaces crossbow ammunition
- **AoE** — Affects targets in area
- **Buff** — Boosts stats/attributes
- **Chaining** — Redirects to nearby enemies (6m projectiles, 4m others)
- **Channeling** — Button held for continuous use
- **Curse** — Debuffs targets (one maximum per enemy)
- **Duration** — Effect length
- **Life Leech** — Recovers Life/Mana based on damage
- **Mark** — Debuffs enemy (one per enemy maximum)
- **Physical** — Physical damage
- **Projectile** — Moving skill hitting enemies
- **Slam** — AoE melee attacks
- **Strike** — Melee attack
- **Totem** — Immobile summoned objects
- **Trigger** — Automatically used when conditions met

## Common Terminology (glossary)

- **AoE** — Area of Effect
- **Buff** — Positive effect (Movement Speed, Mana Regen)
- **CC** — Crowd Control
- **CD** — Cooldown
- **Crit** — Critical hit damage
- **Debuff** — Negative effect
- **DoT** — Damage over Time
- **DPS** — Damage Per Second
- **Proc** — Programmed Random Occurrence
