# Freedom of Faith

**Base:** Ritual Precursor Tablet
**Class:** Tablet

**Requires: Level 65**

## Implicit

- Adds Ritual Altars to a Map 10 uses remaining

## Modifiers

- Favours at Ritual Altars in Area costs (10 — 15) % increased Tribute
- Can Reroll Favours at Ritual Altars in your Maps twice as many times

## Flavour

> When darkness comes, we pray for light. When darkness is all that there is, we pray for death.
