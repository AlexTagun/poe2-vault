# Wraeclast Besieged

**Base:** Breach Precursor Tablet
**Class:** Tablet

**Requires: Level 65**

## Implicit

- Adds an Otherworldy Breach to a Map 10 uses remaining

## Modifiers

- Breaches expand to at least 20 metres in radius Breaches remain open while there are alive Breach Monsters
- Breaches in Map have (-10 — 20) % reduced Monster density
- map breach minimum monster power to not close [5]

## Flavour

> In his broken madness, Xesht spills forth the hordes, reaching out to wreak pain and ruination.
