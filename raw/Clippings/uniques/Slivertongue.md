**Slivertongue Zealot Bow** is a **[Unique Shortbow](/Unique+Bows "Path of Exile 2 Unique Bows")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Slivertongue Zealot Bow,** this sharp, precise bow amplifies physical damage, increases critical potential, and siphons both life and mana from enemies. Its arrows split and then pierce through all foes they touch. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> A hundred blind heads, each seeking the taste of prey on the air.

### Path of Exile 2 Slivertongue Zealot Bow Stats & Modifiers

**Slivertongue Zealot Bow**has the following Stats:

Physical Damage: (71-79) to (112-119)  
Critical Hit Chance: 5%  
Attacks per Second: 1.2

**Slivertongue Zealot Bow**features the following Modifiers:

Adds (40–48) to (65–72) Physical Damage  
+(4–6)% to Critical Hit Chance  
Leeches (5–8)% of Physical Damage as Life  
Leeches (4–7)% of Physical Damage as Mana  
Arrows Fork  
Arrows Pierce all targets after Forking

### PoE 2 Slivertongue Zealot Bow Requirements

To equip **Slivertongue Zealot Bow** players must meet the following requirements:

* Level 39
* Stats: 90 Dex

### How to get Slivertongue Zealot Bow in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* **Slivertongue Zealot Bow** can be obtained by farming [Bosses](/Bosses "Path of Exile 2 Bosses") and rare monsters with unique modifiers. These enemy types have a higher chance of dropping uniques.
