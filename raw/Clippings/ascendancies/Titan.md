**Titan**is an **[Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies")** for the **[Warrior](/Warrior "Path of Exile 2 Warrior")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. The **Titan** embodies raw strength, focusing on overwhelming physical power. [Ascendancies](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies") are subclasses that allow players unique abilities that significantly impact gameplay and strategy. Every Ascendancy Class offers unique mechanics that cater to different playstyles. Starting in Act 2, players will encounter Ascension Trials—challenging tests that must be completed to unlock an [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies").

> Incredible might has always laid buried within you. Now is the time to rise.

### Path of Exile 2 Titan Key Features

The **Titan** utilizes heavy-hitting attacks that can trigger after-effects such as a shockwave and more.

##### **Unique Abilities:**

* [Ancestral Empowerment](/Ancestral+Empowerment "Path of Exile 2 Ancestral Empowerment"): Every second Slam Skill you use yourself is Ancestrally Boosted
* [Colossal Capacity](/Colossal+Capacity "Path of Exile 2 Colossal Capacity"): Carry a Chest which adds 20 Inventory Slots
* [Crushing Impacts](/Crushing+Impacts "Path of Exile 2 Crushing Impacts"): Your Hits are Crushing Blows and deal more damage to heavily stunned enemies
* [Earthbreaker](/Earthbreaker "Path of Exile 2 Earthbreaker"): 25% chance for Slam Skills you use yourself to cause Aftershocks
* [Hulking Form](/Hulking+Form "Path of Exile 2 Hulking Form"): 50% increased effect of Small Passive Skills
* [Mountain Splitter](/Mountain+Splitter "Path of Exile 2 Mountain Splitter"): Every Third Slam skill that doesn't create Fissures which you use yourself causes additional Aftershocks
* [Mysterious Lineage](/Mysterious+Lineage "Path of Exile 2 Mysterious Lineage"): 15% more Maximum Life
* [Stone Skin](/Stone+Skin "Path of Exile 2 Stone Skin"): 50% more Armour from Equipped Body Armour

### Path of Exile 2 Titan Pros and Cons

Here you can learn about the strengths and weaknesses of playing a **Titan**, and whether this aligns with your style of play.

### Pros

**+ Melee Mechanics:****Slams** will be improved with the **Titan**ascendancy, allowing for satisfying melee hits against groups of enemies.

**+ Survivability**: The **Titan** can be quite tanky if given the proper tools since it is an ascendancy that needs to withstand enemy attacks.

### Cons

**-****Mobility:** A **Titan** will have trouble going around the battlefield, as a melee class will be slow when maneuvering.

**- Gear Dependent**: Most **Titan**builds will be gear dependent, so it can be difficult to be a melee class and gear dependent.
