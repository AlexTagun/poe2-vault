# PVP

## Overview

PvP or Player vs Player in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) refers to the mechanics involving fighting other players.

## Current Status

Currently, player-versus-player dueling is **not available** in Path of Exile 2. There is still no feature to duel players in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2). Dueling will most likely be added in future updates.

## How PvP Works in Original Path of Exile

The original Path of Exile includes PvP functionality with these characteristics:

- **Format**: Friendly duels between two players
- **Access Requirements**: Players must be in the same party to challenge each other
- **Rewards**: No loot drops following a duel
- **Balance Notes**: The game's balance excludes PvP as a design consideration, potentially creating "different interactions between HP and skill damage"

## Future Plans

The wiki indicates that dueling mechanics are expected to be implemented in Path of Exile 2 through future updates, though no timeline or specific details are currently available.
