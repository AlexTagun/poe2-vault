# Birth of Fury

**Base:** Stone Greaves
**Class:** Boots

## Base Stats

Armour : 102

**Requires: Level 33 , 45 Str**

## Modifiers

- 20 % increased Movement Speed
- +(40 — 60) to maximum Life
- +(20 — 30) % to Fire Resistance
- Drop Ignited Ground while moving, which lasts 8 seconds and Ignites as though dealing Fire Damage equal to 10 % of your maximum Life

## Flavour

> As the sun rises and the light approaches, so too shall your enemies fear you.
