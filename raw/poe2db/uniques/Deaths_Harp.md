# Death's Harp

**Base:** Dualstring Bow
**Class:** Bows

## Base Stats

Physical Damage: 19-35
Critical Hit Chance: 5%
Attacks per Second: 1.15

**Requires: Level 28 , 52 Dex**

## Implicit

- +50 % Surpassing chance to fire an additional Arrow

## Modifiers

- +(20 — 25) % to Critical Damage Bonus
- Bow Attacks fire 3 additional Arrows
- Gain (20 — 30) Life per enemy killed
- Gain (12 — 18) Mana per enemy killed

## Flavour

> The mournful music of the strings, The creaking arch, the arrow sings. A choking cry, a rattled breath, The Reaper's Song, the Harp of Death.
