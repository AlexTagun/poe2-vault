**Sustainable Practices** is an **[Ascendancy Skill](/Skills "Path of Exile 2 Skills")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (POE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. Sustainable Practices is an exclusive skill for the [Pathfinder](/Pathfinder "Path of Exile 2 Pathfinder") [Ascendancy Class](/Ascendancies "Path of Exile 2 Ascendancies"). Selecting an Ascendancy opens up a distinct skill tree tailored to that subclass, granting access to unique talents and traits. Every character class offers three Ascendancy choices, allowing players to personalize their combat style and progression path intricately.

### Sustainable Practices Path of Exile 2 Information

The Sustainable Practices skill has the following effects:

* 50% of Evasion Rating
* Grants Elemental Damage reduction

### How to Obtain Sustainable Practices in Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)

* Sustainable Practices is a skill that is exclusively located in the skill tree of the [Pathfinder](/Pathfinder "Path of Exile 2 Pathfinder") Ascendancy class. The [Pathfinder](/Pathfinder "Path of Exile 2 Pathfinder") subclass is exclusive to the [Ranger](/Ranger "Path of Exile 2 Ranger").

### Path of Exile 2 Notes and Tips for Sustainable Practices

* The Sustainable Practices Notable Passive Skill was increased and now grants 50% of Evasion Rating and Elemental Damage reduction (Previously 30%) in Patch 0.3.0
