**Earthbound Voltaic Staff** is a **[Unique Staff](/Unique+Staves "Path of Exile 2 Unique Staves")** and a **[Unique Weapon](/Unique+Weapons "Path of Exile 2 Unique Weapons")** in **[Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2)](/Path+of+Exile+2+Wiki "Path of Exile 2 Wiki")**. **Earthbound Voltaic Staff** is a staff that enhances lightning damage, cast speed, and mana regeneration while triggering a lightning skill on kill. **Unique Weapons** offer fixed modifiers and powerful abilities that enhance offensive capabilities or enable unique attack and spell mechanics, making them the ultimate equipment with high stat values and one-of-a-kind effects, unlike Magic and Rare gear that rely on randomized affixes.

> An ancient Azmeri staff, overgrown by roots...
